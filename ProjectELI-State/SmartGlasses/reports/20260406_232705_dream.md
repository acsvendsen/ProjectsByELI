### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules based on confidence states, V1 can ensure that subtitles remain clear, stable, and unobtrusive while maintaining readability during live conversations. This helps in preserving trust and improving the wearer's understanding without causing distraction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence speech where subtitles are placed at the bottom of the screen, and another for lower-confidence speech where subtitles are slightly reduced in size but remain visible near the center of the screen. This simple distinction helps in maintaining readability while reducing visual distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing these two subtitle placement modes, V1 can significantly enhance the wearer's ability to follow live conversations without being overwhelmed by text, thereby improving overall trust and understanding during interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
