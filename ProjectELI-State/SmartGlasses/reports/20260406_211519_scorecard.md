## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 is focused on delivering clear subtitles, reliable face/name recall, and discreet one-line assistance while maintaining a phone-first architecture to keep hardware lightweight and socially acceptable. The current efforts are aligning with core attractors but need more attention to ensure privacy and low-latency responsiveness.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The project is maintaining a simple hardware stack with frame-touch interaction and minimal local processing. The focus remains on keeping the glasses lightweight and social acceptability without overburdening battery or thermals.
- next focus: Optimize subtitle placement for readability while respecting social acceptability constraints.
- confidence: 0.85

## Software Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep the cognition stack practical, maintainable, and centered on subtitle quality, memory trust, and low-friction assistive behavior.
- limitation pressure: complexity drift and latency
