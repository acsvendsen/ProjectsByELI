### Weakening Signals

### Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Privacy vs. Usefulness**: Balance providing helpful assistive behavior without compromising privacy or creating invasive data capture.
- **Latency vs. Richness**: Ensure timely responses while avoiding overly complex processing that could introduce latency issues.

### Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Confidence Display**: Revisit the design of confidence labels and scores to enhance user trust without overloading them with information.
- **Local Processing**: Explore local processing capabilities on phones or glasses for certain tasks, ensuring it does not compromise battery life or usability.

### Concrete Tensions Around Core Areas

#### Subtitle Quality
- **Subtitle Placement**: Optimize subtitle placement to be readable and unobtrusive during live conversation.
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Ensure subtitles are visually clear but do not distract from the interaction.

#### Memory Trust
- **Face/Name Memory**: Improve face/name recognition by reinforcing memory with explicit remember-touch behavior rather than relying solely on automatic detection.
- **Memory Cache Policy**: Determine an appropriate cache policy for face and name data to balance local storage with cloud support.

#### Confidence Display
- **Label Format**: Decide between a numeric score, label only, or label + short reason to represent confidence levels effectively.
- **Visual Clarity vs. Discreet UX**: Balance the need for clear visual cues with the requirement that these cues should not be too intrusive in social settings.

#### Phone/Cloud Boundary
- **Real-Time Subtitles**: Prioritize real-time subtitle quality over heavy cloud processing, ensuring live transcripts are delivered promptly.
- **Low-Friction Interaction**: Ensure interaction is as simple and unobtrusive as possible to maintain natural user experience.
