### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By establishing stable subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, the system ensures that subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive while still providing essential assistance. This reduces cognitive load for the wearer by ensuring that critical information is always visible when needed.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence speech (e.g., names and familiar phrases) placed above or near the speaker's face, and another for lower-confidence speech (e.g., unknown words or environmental sounds) placed in a fixed position at the bottom of the field of view.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will improve the wearer’s ability to focus on critical information while reducing distractions from less important details. It will enhance trust in the subtitle system by ensuring that relevant text is always visible and unobtrusive, thereby improving overall user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format will enhance trust in the system's reliability by clearly communicating to the wearer how certain or uncertain the system is about its information. This clarity is crucial for maintaining the wearer’s faith in the assistive nature of the glasses, especially during critical interactions where real-time accuracy is paramount.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a confidence format that uses both numeric scores and short reason phrases to convey certainty levels succinctly. For example, use labels like "High" (score 0.9+), "Medium" (score 0.5-0.8), and "Low" (score <0.4) with corresponding reasons such as "Seen recently", "Weak match", or "First time seen".

3. **Likely payoff**

   A clear, concise confidence display will help the wearer understand when to trust the subtitles and memory prompts more readily. This improved trust can lead to better engagement and higher daily-use value, ensuring that the wearer is more confident in using the glasses without feeling unsure about their accuracy.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
