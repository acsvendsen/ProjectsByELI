### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles by making them more consistent and reliable. Stable subtitle positioning ensures that the subtitles are always visible, readable, and unobtrusive, which is crucial for maintaining the wearer's ability to follow the conversation without distraction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define three subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence scenarios (e.g., familiar people or well-known names), one for medium-confidence scenarios (e.g., likely known but not as familiar), and one for low-confidence scenarios (e.g., new faces or names). Each rule should be simple, predictable, and consistent to reduce cognitive load on the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By providing clear and stable subtitle placement rules, the system can significantly enhance the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles. This stability will help maintain focus during conversations and improve overall understanding without causing visual distraction.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

   Improving the confidence display format will enhance trust by making the certainty level explicit and understandable to the wearer. This clarity ensures that the wearer knows when the system is highly confident versus uncertain, which is crucial for maintaining the wearer's confidence in the product.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear and concise confidence indicators, the system will be able to better manage the wearer's expectations, leading to more reliable use and higher trust in the product’s ability to assist accurately.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a specific local memory threshold for face/name recall, the system ensures that new people are remembered with confidence while preventing unnecessary data storage and processing overhead. This balance enhances trust through clear reinforcement over time and faster lookup of relevant memories.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit for face/name recall objects, set to 50 entries or until memory usage hits 10 MB, whichever comes first.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach reduces the risk of privacy concerns by limiting data storage while ensuring that recently encountered people are remembered with high confidence. It also improves lookup performance and simplifies memory management on the phone, leading to a more stable and trustworthy experience for the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Set a local cache size limit for face/name recall objects at 50 entries or until memory usage hits 10 MB, whichever comes first.
