### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By defining a consistent subtitle placement rule that minimizes distraction while ensuring readability, we can enhance the wearer's trust in the system during live conversations. Stable and predictable subtitle placement helps maintain the wearer’s focus on the conversation rather than the display, reducing cognitive load and social awkwardness.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a single fixed position for subtitles at the bottom center of the glasses frame, with a slight adjustment to the side to ensure they do not block the user's view during natural head movements. This can be adjusted slightly based on user input but should remain consistent across sessions.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - A more predictable and stable subtitle placement will reduce visual distraction and improve trust in the system, allowing users to focus better on conversations without constantly adjusting their gaze or position. This enhances the overall user experience and perceived value of the product.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a fixed subtitle placement rule at the bottom center of the glasses frame with a small side adjustment for natural head movements.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving confidence display by refining the format and label style ensures that the wearer can trust the system's output. Clear, consistent, and reliable confidence indicators help build long-term user trust in the system’s memory and subtitle accuracy.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single-confidence-object format: `score + "Low/Medium/High" + short reason`. For example, a score of 0.84 would be displayed as `Score 0.84 High Seen recently`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a clear and consistent confidence display system, the wearer will better understand when to trust the system’s memory and subtitles. This reduces cognitive load by minimizing uncertainty and enhances overall user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one confidence-object format for V1: `score + "Low/Medium/High" + short reason`.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving memory cache policies will enhance the reliability of name and face recall, ensuring that the wearer can trust the system's ability to remember important details without overloading their cognitive load.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a local cache threshold for person-memory objects, setting a recency limit (e.g., 30 days) beyond which old memories are evicted from the cache.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By limiting the retention period of memory objects in the local cache, the system can reduce battery usage and thermal load while ensuring that recent interactions remain highly available for quick recall.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set a 30-day recency threshold for person-memory objects to balance memory availability with resource constraints.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a specific phone/cloud boundary for live subtitle processing, we can ensure that subtitles are generated quickly and accurately on the phone without excessive power consumption or privacy concerns. This helps maintain the real-time nature of subtitles, which is crucial for trust and usefulness.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one task-specific cloud fallback boundary for exactly one task such as live subtitle processing. For example, if the local subtitle generation falls behind due to high latency or poor conditions, fallback to a less resource-intensive version on the phone until conditions improve.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This will reduce power consumption and thermal strain by keeping real-time processing local while still ensuring that subtitles remain accurate and timely. It also helps maintain privacy by not transmitting raw audio for cloud processing unless absolutely necessary.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving subtitle placement stability helps maintain a consistent, reliable visual experience that enhances trust in real-time subtitles. This is crucial for the wearer to rely on the glasses during conversations without distraction or uncertainty.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence text (e.g., clear visibility and minimal movement) and another for low-confidence text (e.g., less prominent but still visible).

3. **Likely payoff**

This change will reduce subtitle flickering and misplacement, leading to a more stable and trusted visual experience that enhances the wearer's ability to follow conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
