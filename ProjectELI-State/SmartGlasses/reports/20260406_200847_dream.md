### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we ensure that subtitles are always visible and clear without overwhelming the wearer. This improves real-time subtitle clarity (attractor: `subtitle_clarity`) by making sure critical information is not missed in noisy or fast-paced environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement rules:
   - High-confidence text placed at the bottom of the screen.
   - Medium-confidence text placed slightly above, ensuring it does not obstruct the wearer's view but remains visible.
   - Low-confidence text temporarily highlighted and moved to a corner or center.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these rules will enhance the reliability and clarity of subtitles in different scenarios. High-confidence texts are always readable, medium-confidence texts provide continuous context without distraction, and low-confidence texts ensure critical information is not missed during uncertain moments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps build trust by clearly communicating certainty levels to the wearer, ensuring they understand when information is highly reliable and when it may be less certain. This enhances the overall reliability of the system and ensures that the wearer can make informed decisions based on the provided assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a confidence format that uses a numeric score (e.g., 0-1) combined with a label (High, Medium, Low) and a short reason phrase to explain the certainty level. For example, "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'" or "score 0.32 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'".

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly displaying confidence levels in a concise format, users will feel more confident in the system's accuracy and be better equipped to handle situations where the information may be less certain. This can reduce cognitive load and increase overall trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving memory cache policies will enhance trust by ensuring that frequently recalled entities (like names and faces) are easily accessible, while less relevant information is efficiently managed to keep local storage usage minimal.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache eviction policy that prioritizes recently interacted with individuals over other data types, such as topics or context snippets.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By optimizing the cache to retain key entities more reliably, the system will provide faster and more accurate recall for names and faces, thereby increasing user trust in the memory functionality.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary between what runs phone-local and what falls back to cloud, we ensure that critical processing happens on the device where it can be most responsive and private. This helps maintain the core mission of providing real-time, reliable support without unnecessary latency or privacy risks.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one task-specific cloud fallback boundary for exactly one task such as live subtitle processing. For example, decide that all live subtitle generation and initial confidence scoring must run phone-local to ensure near-real-time performance and privacy.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing local, we can achieve faster response times, reduce battery drain due to less frequent cloud calls, and maintain a higher level of privacy for the wearer. This also sets a precedent for other critical tasks that need similar treatment in V1.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

3. **Likely payoff**
