# Consolidated SmartGlasses Core Field Analysis

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Quality**: The glasses should provide near-real-time subtitles that are clear, concise, and unobtrusive. This enhances the wearer's understanding in noisy environments.
- **Face/Name Memory**: Effective face and name recall with confidence objects is becoming more crucial for building trust and reliability.

## Weakening Signals
- **Complex Interactions**: The reliance on frame-touch interactions over voice activation or spoken commands indicates a focus on simplicity and user experience. Complex interactions will be deferred until V2.
- **High-Latency Responses**: Any delays in subtitle delivery should be minimized to maintain trust and utility, indicating a need for continuous optimization of latency.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Always-On vs Battery Life**: The system must remain always-on but also minimize battery drain. This tension is crucial as the glasses are designed to be socially acceptable and discreet.
- **Rich Memory vs Fast Lookup**: Balancing between rich, detailed memory support and fast, relevant lookup remains a key challenge.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Memory Reinforcement**: The system should continually reinforce new-person memories through interactions and context-based reminders. This can help in building stronger confidence objects over time.
- **Visual UX**: Optimizing subtitle placement and visual hierarchy to ensure they are clear, unobtrusive, and usable across different environments.

## Concrete Tensions Around Key Areas
1. **Subtitle Quality**:
   - **Tension**: Subtitle accuracy vs. real-time processing efficiency.
   - **Recommendation**: Prioritize real-time delivery over perfect accuracy in V1 to maintain usability.
   
2. **Confidence Trust**:
   - **Tension**: Displaying confidence with labels and reasons vs. numeric scores.
   - **Recommendation**: Use label + short reason for now, as it balances clarity and simplicity.

3. **Automatic Memory Behavior**:
   - **Tension**: Continuously remembering new faces and names without overwhelming the user.
   - **Recommendation**: Implement a caching policy that keeps memory relevant but not excessive.

4. **Explicit Remember-Touch Behavior**:
   - **Tension**: Balancing automatic memory with explicit user reinforcement.
   - **Recommendation**: Allow users to explicitly confirm or correct memory entries to enhance accuracy and trust.

5. **Fast Lookup Requirements**:
   - **Tension**: Providing quick access to previously learned information vs. avoiding overloading the wearer.
   - **Recommendation**: Use one-line prompts for quick recall, while ensuring they do not disrupt the flow of conversation.

6. **Privacy Concerns**:
   - **Tension**: Collecting and storing enough data to provide useful memory support without compromising privacy.
   - **Recommendation**: Design a privacy-focused caching policy that minimizes storage but maximizes utility.

7. **Battery and Thermals**:
   - **Tension**: Always-on sensing vs. battery drain.
   - **Recommendation**: Optimize sensors for low power consumption during idle periods.

8. **Visual UX**:
   - **Tension**: Subtitle placement vs. visual hierarchy.
   - **Recommendation**: Place subtitles prominently but ensure they do not distract the user, using a clear visual hierarchy to balance clarity and unobtrusiveness.

## Open Questions
- **Subtitle Placement**: What is the optimal subtitle placement for best readability without distraction?
- **Confidence Display**: How should confidence be represented: label only, label + short reason, or score + label?

## Recent Signal Analysis

- The glasses should remain focused on live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. These features are essential for V1.
- The system must avoid becoming a gimmick by ensuring it provides real utility in daily use.

### Non-negotiables
- The glasses should not pretend knowledge the wearer does not have.
- Early hardware remains simple: display text, record audio/visual input; phone/cloud handles heavy processing.

- New faces and names should be remembered with confidence objects and reinforcement over time.

### Core Tensions

- Always-on sensing vs. battery and thermals.

- Rich memory vs. fast lookup.
- Discreet UX vs. enough visual clarity.
