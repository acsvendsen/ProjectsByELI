### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By establishing stable subtitle placement rules, V1 can ensure that subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive during live conversations. This improves the wearer's trust in the system by making sure they are always able to read subtitles without distractions, which is crucial for maintaining their focus on the conversation.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement rules: 
   - Top-left corner
   - Bottom-center of the screen
   - Outside the field of view but with a quick reveal upon touch

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing stable subtitle placement will reduce visual distractions during live conversations, enhancing the wearer's understanding and trust in the system. This clarity can lead to higher user satisfaction and more effective use of the smart glasses.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud, we can ensure that V1 remains focused on delivering essential subtitle and memory support without overwhelming battery or thermal limits. This helps maintain high real-time responsiveness and privacy while ensuring that complex processing is handled where appropriate.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific task boundary for live subtitle processing that keeps critical real-time functionality phone-local, allowing the cloud to handle transcript cleanup and memory archive sync as needed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing on-device, we can ensure fast, reliable, and low-latency support in noisy environments. This improves trust and usability without increasing battery drain or thermal load. The cloud will still be available for background tasks like face/name recall lookup and memory archive sync, providing useful reinforcement over time.

4. **Immediate next probe**
