### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the wearer's ability to follow conversations, making the subtitles more useful without becoming distracting. This refinement ensures that the subtitles are placed in a way that minimizes visual clutter while maximizing readability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence speakers and another for lower-confidence or less familiar voices. The high-confidence rule can place subtitles slightly higher on the screen to avoid obstruction, while the lower-confidence rule can position them below the frame for easier reference but with reduced visual intrusion.

3. **Likely payoff**
   This change will improve the overall user experience by ensuring that critical information is more prominent and less likely to be missed, while still maintaining a low-friction interface that does not overwhelm the wearer in real-time interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
