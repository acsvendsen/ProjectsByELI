### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain clear, readable, and unobtrusive in real-time scenarios. Stable, well-placed subtitles enhance understanding without causing distractions, aligning with the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a fixed subtitle placement rule for different confidence states: 
- Low confidence: Place subtitles at the bottom center.
- Medium confidence: Place subtitles slightly above and to one side.
- High confidence: Place subtitles directly below the speaker's face, if visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

Implementing stable subtitle placement rules will reduce visual clutter, ensuring that subtitles remain readable without causing distractions. This improves overall user experience and trust in real-time assistance, reinforcing the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

3. **Likely payoff**

4. **Immediate next probe**

   By defining a clear boundary between what runs on the phone and what falls back to the cloud for live subtitle processing, we ensure that critical real-time operations remain stable and responsive. This improves the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity, ensuring that the wearer can trust the subtitles in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**

4. **Immediate next probe**
