### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules in V1 can enhance the wearer's ability to understand live conversations by ensuring subtitles are clear, readable, and not distracting. Stable subtitle placement is crucial for maintaining trust and providing reliable support without overwhelming the user.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 fixed subtitle placement rules: 
   - Default center placement with a slight top bias.
   - Bottom edge placement when speech is highly confident (e.g., clear speaker, low background noise).
   - Center screen for less confident or ambiguous speech to avoid occluding important visual information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing these fixed subtitle placement rules, the system will provide a more stable and predictable experience for the wearer. This can lead to increased trust in the subtitles, as they will be consistently visible and not disrupt the wearer's natural flow of conversation.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the default center subtitle placement rule with a slight top bias.

3. **Likely payoff**

4. **Immediate next probe**
