### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance overall user experience by ensuring subtitles are always visible, clear, and unobtrusive. This helps maintain trust and usability, especially in noisy environments where real-time understanding is crucial.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple rule that places subtitles slightly above the center of the wearer's field of view, with a fixed vertical offset from the bottom edge of the glasses display. This ensures subtitles are always visible but do not block important visual context or become distracting.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By maintaining consistent and unobtrusive subtitle placement, users will have better real-time comprehension without cognitive strain from frequent repositioning. This can significantly enhance the product's usefulness in noisy environments, thereby strengthening the core attractors of real-time clarity and low-friction assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
