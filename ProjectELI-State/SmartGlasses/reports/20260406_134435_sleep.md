# Consolidation of Key Signals for SmartGlasses V1

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Quality:** Improving subtitle placement to enhance readability without causing distraction.
- **Memory Reinforcement:** Enhancing the confidence display with a label and short reason for memory recall, ensuring trust.
- **Fast Lookup Requirements:** Prioritizing fast access to recently seen terms or names via explicit remember-touch behavior.

## Weakening Signals
- **Voice Activation:** Reducing dependency on voice commands in V1 interaction.
- **Complex Multimodal UI:** Focusing on simpler interactions and avoiding complex multimodal interfaces for now.
- **Full On-Glasses Inference:** Limiting local inference capabilities to reduce battery drain and thermal issues.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Face/Name Memory Storage:** Balancing between caching face/name memory locally versus relying heavily on the phone for processing.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy:** Exploring optimal subtitle placement that balances clarity and non-intrusiveness.
- **Term Detection Types:** Further refining term detection to ensure it covers a broader range of useful terms without overwhelming the wearer.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Areas

### Subtitle Reasoning vs Memory Policy
- **Subtitle Quality:** Ensuring subtitles are clear, concise, and do not overwhelm the wearer. 
- **Memory Policy:** Striking a balance between maintaining a rich memory for context and ensuring fast lookup and retrieval without overburdening the system.

### Interaction Constraints vs Feature Density
- **Interaction Direction:** Focusing on frame-touch interactions to maintain simplicity while allowing for more complex features.
- **Feature Density:** Avoiding feature sprawl by prioritizing core functionalities like live subtitles, face/name recall, and conversation memory.

### Privacy vs Usefulness
- **Face/Name Memory:** Implementing robust privacy measures while ensuring the system can effectively remember new faces and names with confidence objects and reinforcement over time.
