### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain stable, readable, and unobtrusive during live conversations, thereby enhancing the wearer's ability to follow the conversation without distractions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a fixed subtitle placement mode that prioritizes stability over reactivity, ensuring subtitles remain in a consistent position regardless of head movement or gaze direction.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By maintaining stable subtitle placement, users can rely on subtitles more confidently during conversations, reducing the risk of losing context due to frequent repositioning and improving overall comprehension.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By improving the confidence display format, we can enhance the wearer's trust in the system and ensure that the assistance provided is reliable and credible.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear, concise confidence format that includes a numeric score, label, and a short reason for the confidence level. For example: "Score 0.85 + 'High' + 'Seen recently in this conversation.'"

3. **Likely payoff**

   This improvement will help users understand the reliability of the information provided by the system, leading to higher trust and better use of the assistant's support. Users will be more likely to rely on the subtitles and memory support when interacting with others.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a local cache threshold for face/name memory on the phone, we ensure that critical memory is stored and can be quickly recalled even in offline scenarios. This improves the reliability of memory support without overloading the device's storage or processing capabilities.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit for face/name recall objects on the phone, ensuring that only recently seen faces and names are cached for fast retrieval.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will enhance the reliability of name and face recall in noisy environments or when internet access is unavailable. It will also help maintain trust by providing quick and accurate support without relying solely on cloud availability.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define a local cache size limit for face/name recall objects, setting it to retain memory entries for the last 20 interactions.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

By defining a clear boundary for what runs phone-local versus falling back to cloud, we ensure that V1 focuses on delivering real-time subtitle clarity and low-friction memory support without overloading the glasses hardware or compromising privacy. This helps maintain the core mission of reducing social and cognitive friction while ensuring that essential processing stays local.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a specific task boundary for live subtitle processing where only high-confidence, recent subtitles are processed locally on the phone. For lower-confidence or older subtitles, fall back to cloud processing. This simple rule will ensure that real-time performance is maintained without unnecessary cloud load.

3. **Likely payoff**

This small change will enable V1 to deliver clear and timely subtitles while reducing battery drain and thermal stress on the glasses. It will also ensure that privacy concerns are managed by limiting local processing, thereby maintaining trust and social acceptability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support enhances the wearer's ability to focus on critical information without being overwhelmed by too much text. This balance supports real-time understanding in noisy environments, aligning with the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a visual priority rule where subtitles always appear above and take precedence over one-line support text. This ensures that crucial dialogue remains prominently displayed while less critical prompts can be suppressed or coexist minimally.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing subtitles, the system becomes more usable in real-world noisy scenarios, reducing cognitive load and enhancing trustworthiness. Clear separation also supports faster reading of important information during conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
