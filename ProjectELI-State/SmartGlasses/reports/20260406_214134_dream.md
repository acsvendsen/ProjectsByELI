### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement improves the wearer's trust in the system during live conversations. Stable subtitles reduce distractions and ensure that the wearer can rely on the displayed text, which is crucial for real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed position for subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view, ensuring they are always visible but not obstructing critical visual information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining a stable subtitle placement rule, V1 will provide more consistent and reliable subtitle delivery, enhancing trust in the system during live interactions. This stability reduces cognitive load for the wearer and ensures that subtitles remain clear and accessible at all times.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display to provide a clear, neutral format that includes both a numeric score and a short reason, we can enhance trust in the system's accuracy without overloading the wearer with too much information. This will help ensure that users understand when they should pay more attention to the subtitles or rely on them more heavily.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one confidence format candidate for V1: `score + "Low" / "Medium" / "High" + short reason`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will provide a clear and concise way to convey the system's certainty, which can significantly enhance user trust in the subtitles. Users will be able to quickly assess whether the subtitles are reliable or if they should seek additional context.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving memory cache size helps ensure that frequently encountered faces and names are quickly accessible, reducing latency in subtitle placement and name recall, thereby enhancing the wearer's understanding and trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a local cache threshold for face/name memories that evicts less frequent entries after a certain number of interactions or time without use.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - A well-tuned memory cache size will reduce latency in subtitle placement and name recall, making the system more responsive and reliable during live conversations, which directly aligns with the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a local cache threshold for face/name memories that evicts less frequent entries after 5 interactions or 1 hour without use.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a specific phone/cloud fallback boundary for transcript cleanup, we ensure that critical memory support remains available even under high-latency conditions while preserving privacy and reducing battery drain.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one cloud fallback rule for exactly one task such as live subtitle processing, ensuring that the most sensitive or real-time data is handled locally when possible but falling back to the cloud only when necessary.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This focused approach will improve daily-use trust by maintaining high-quality subtitles and memory support without sacrificing battery life or privacy, aligning with core attractors of subtitle clarity and memory trust.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one cloud fallback rule for live subtitle processing such that transcript data is primarily handled on the phone but falls back to the cloud when connectivity improves after an initial delay.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support helps maintain focus on the most critical information during live interactions. This improves the wearer's ability to follow conversations without becoming overwhelmed by additional prompts, thus enhancing their understanding and comfort.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific subtitle-vs-one-line priority rule that routes higher confidence subtitles above one-line prompts in the visual hierarchy, ensuring that the most relevant information is always prominently displayed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing high-confidence subtitles over less critical one-line support, users will be less likely to miss important details during conversations, leading to a more productive and less stressful user experience. This alignment of subtitle prominence with confidence levels ensures that the wearer's attention remains on the most relevant information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
