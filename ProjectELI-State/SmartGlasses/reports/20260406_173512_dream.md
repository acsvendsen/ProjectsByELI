### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Ensuring stable subtitle placement reduces visual distractions, allowing the wearer to focus on the conversation more effectively. Stable placement also builds trust by providing consistent and reliable information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that prioritizes the bottom center of the field of view for all confidence states, ensuring subtitles do not move excessively or disappear during dynamic interactions.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By setting a fixed position, the system will provide more consistent and predictable subtitles, reducing cognitive load and improving overall user trust in the assistive features. This stability will enhance the wearer's ability to follow conversations without visual interruptions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving confidence display ensures that the wearer can trust the system's outputs, which is crucial for maintaining the core mission of enhancing understanding and comprehension in real-world interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a simple yet effective confidence format that includes a numeric score, label, and short reason to provide clear feedback on the reliability of the displayed information.

3. **Likely payoff**

This change will enhance trust by clearly indicating when the system is more or less certain about its outputs. It will help the wearer make informed decisions based on the level of confidence shown, thereby reducing cognitive load and increasing overall usability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a local cache size limit for face/name memory, we can ensure that the system retains only the most relevant and recently encountered individuals, thereby reducing memory bloat while maintaining essential context.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a configuration option to set a maximum number of entries in the local cache for names and faces, ensuring that the cache does not grow indefinitely but remains manageable and focused on recent interactions.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Setting a clear limit on the local cache size will help reduce memory usage, improve battery efficiency, and ensure that the system remains responsive even when processing complex conversations involving many people. This will enhance the overall user experience by keeping the memory content relevant and not overwhelming the wearer with too much information.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define a maximum of 10 entries in the local cache for names and faces to balance memory retention and system performance.
