- The system should continue to focus on automatic memory reinforcement over explicit remember-touch behavior, ensuring that new faces and names are remembered with confidence objects and reinforced over time.

### Fast Lookup Requirements
- Balancing the need for fast lookups against the risk of storing low-value information is essential. The goal is to maintain rich memory while avoiding cluttering the system with irrelevant data.
