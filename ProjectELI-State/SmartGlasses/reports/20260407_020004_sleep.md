2. **Confidence Trust**: Maintaining accurate confidence levels without overconfidence or underconfidence.
3. **Automatic New-Person Memory**: Balancing the automatic recording of new faces and names with the need for reinforcement over time.
4. **Memory Reinforcement**: Ensuring that remembered information is reinforced and remains accessible.
5. **Fast Lookup**: Providing quick access to previously learned contexts without overwhelming the wearer.
6. **Privacy**: Managing data collection and usage to ensure privacy while providing useful assistance.
7. **Latency**: Balancing real-time performance with richer processing capabilities.
8. **Battery**: Avoiding heavy on-glasses computation that drains battery life.
9. **Visual UX**: Ensuring clear, unobtrusive display of subtitles and one-line prompts.
