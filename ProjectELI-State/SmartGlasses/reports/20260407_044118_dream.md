### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always clear and unobtrusive while still providing essential context. This approach helps maintain the wearer's focus without overwhelming them with too much information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states:
   - High confidence: Place subtitles at the bottom of the field of view.
   - Medium confidence: Place subtitles slightly above the center, allowing peripheral vision to notice them if needed but not blocking direct attention.
   - Low confidence: Display subtitles in a fixed position near the top-right corner of the frame, ensuring they are always visible without distracting from the main focus.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will enhance subtitle clarity and readability by adapting placement based on confidence levels. It will reduce visual clutter during high-confidence moments while ensuring critical information is easily accessible when needed. This can significantly improve user trust in the system's accuracy and reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By optimizing memory cache policies for local storage and quick lookup, we can ensure that critical name and face information is readily available to the wearer without unnecessary cloud requests or thermal battery drain. This improves the reliability and speed of memory support, aligning with the core attractors of trustworthiness and low-friction assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache eviction policy that keeps frequently recalled names and faces in the phone's cache for up to 1 hour, evicting less recently seen entries after 30 minutes. This ensures high-priority memory remains accessible while managing storage space efficiently.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will reduce latency and improve the reliability of name and face recall, enhancing the wearer’s trust in the system's ability to support them during critical interactions. It also aligns with V1 constraints by minimizing cloud usage and thermal/energy demands.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.
