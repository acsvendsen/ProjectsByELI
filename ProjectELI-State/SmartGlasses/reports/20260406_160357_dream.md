### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules help maintain a consistent user experience, reducing cognitive load and ensuring that subtitles are always legible and unobtrusive. This stability builds trust over time by providing reliable support, especially in noisy or crowded environments where clear communication is crucial.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: low (always at the bottom), medium (flexible but not obstructive), and high (more prominent but still readable).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining clear, stable subtitle placement rules, the glasses will provide a more predictable and reliable experience for the wearer. This consistency helps in reducing cognitive effort required to read subtitles, thereby enhancing overall usability and trust.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

4. **Immediate next probe**
