### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By refining subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, V1 can ensure that subtitles remain clear and stable enough to be trusted in live conversations. This improves real-time subtitle clarity (subtitle_clarity attractor), which is crucial for the wearer's trust and understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches where subtitles are placed at the bottom of the screen, and another for low-confidence matches where subtitles are slightly blurred or offset to avoid distraction while still providing useful information.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By creating distinct placement rules based on confidence levels, V1 can maintain a balance between clarity and non-distracting behavior. This will help in reducing visual overload and ensuring that the wearer focuses more on the conversation itself rather than being distracted by less certain subtitles.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving confidence display ensures that the wearer can trust the system's output by clearly indicating the level of certainty in each piece of information, which is crucial for maintaining the core mission of helping the wearer understand and respond better without overloading them with rich but potentially noisy assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear confidence format that includes a numeric score, label, and short reason. For example, use a format like "score 0.9 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'" for well-supported information and "score 0.3 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'" for less certain information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A consistent confidence display will help the wearer trust the system more, especially in noisy environments where clarity is vital. It also reinforces the core attractor of memory support by ensuring that the wearer can distinguish between reliable and uncertain information, thus maintaining their understanding and trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining clear boundaries for local processing versus cloud fallback, V1 can ensure that critical real-time subtitle processing remains fast and reliable while offloading less time-sensitive tasks to the phone or cloud as needed. This approach helps maintain high subtitle clarity and trustworthiness without overburdening the glasses' limited resources.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific task boundary for live subtitle processing that runs entirely on the phone, with no fallback to the cloud. For example, set a rule that any real-time speech-to-text conversion must run locally and not rely on network latency.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This ensures that subtitles remain clear and stable in critical real-world interactions where high reliability is essential. By keeping live subtitle processing local, V1 can avoid the risk of dropped connections or network delays impacting the wearer's experience negatively.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the rule that live subtitle processing runs entirely on the phone with no fallback to the cloud.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle vs one-line support coexistence ensures that the wearer can trust subtitles without being overwhelmed by additional information. This balance is crucial for maintaining focus and reducing cognitive load, which in turn enhances the overall usability and trustworthiness of the product.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a visual hierarchy rule that prioritizes subtitle clarity while still allowing one-line support to appear when necessary but not as prominently. For example, if confidence is high for subtitles, they should be displayed clearly with reduced priority given to one-line prompts unless contextually relevant.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By carefully balancing subtitle and one-line support coexistence, the product can maintain high trust in real-time subtitle assistance while ensuring that necessary contextual information (like one-liners) is not missed. This balance will reduce cognitive overload for the wearer and improve overall usability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a visual hierarchy rule prioritizing subtitle clarity over one-line support, ensuring subtitles are always clearly visible while one-liners appear only when contextually relevant with lower prominence.
