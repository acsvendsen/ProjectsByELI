### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Stable subtitle placement rules ensure that subtitles remain consistently readable without becoming distracting. This helps the wearer focus on the conversation, improving their understanding and engagement.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define three fixed subtitle placement rules: one for high confidence (e.g., 80%+ certainty), another for medium confidence (e.g., 60-79%), and a third for low confidence (e.g., below 60%).

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By providing consistent placement, the system reduces visual clutter while ensuring that critical information is always visible. This can lead to higher user satisfaction and trust in the subtitles' accuracy.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear and consistent confidence display helps build trust with the user. By refining how certainty is presented, we can make the system's assistance more credible and useful.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence format that combines a numeric score with a label and a short reason. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A clear and consistent confidence display will enhance the user's trust in the system’s judgments, leading to better adoption and use of its features. This clarity also helps users understand when they can rely on the assistance provided by the glasses.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Ensuring that critical live subtitles stay phone-local ensures real-time responsiveness, which is crucial for trust and effectiveness in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a rule where all subtitle processing remains on the phone until absolutely necessary to fall back to cloud.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By keeping subtitle processing local, we can ensure near-real-time delivery, reducing latency and improving trust with the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one task-specific cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing that triggers only when the phone is low on battery or overheating.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support helps maintain focus on critical information without overwhelming the wearer. This improves overall comprehension and reduces cognitive load, aligning with the goal of helping the wearer stay present and informed.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: 
   - Rule 1: Subtitles should always be visible but not obstruct primary viewing.
   - Rule 2: One-line support should appear prominently when needed and disappear quickly to avoid distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly defining these rules, the visual hierarchy will be more predictable and intuitive for the wearer, enhancing the system's usability and reducing the risk of information overload during conversations or in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
