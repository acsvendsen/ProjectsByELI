- Mission: Discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support.
- V1 Interaction: Frame touches only; no voice activation or spoken command control.
- Phone-First Architecture: Heavy processing primarily happens on the phone with optional cloud support where clearly justified.
