### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining stable subtitle placement rules that prioritize readability and avoid distracting motion, we can ensure the subtitles remain clear and trust-preserving in live conversation. This helps maintain the wearer's focus on the conversation without interrupting their natural flow.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that positions subtitles at the bottom of the frame, away from the center, to minimize visual distraction while ensuring they remain visible and readable.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will reduce eye strain and cognitive load by keeping subtitles out of the wearer's direct line of sight during conversation. It also helps maintain a clean interface that is less likely to be distracting to both the wearer and their conversational partners, thereby reinforcing trust in the assistant's reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the system's output by clearly indicating the certainty level of each piece of information, such as subtitles or memory recalls. This enhances overall reliability and builds trust in the product.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple but clear confidence label combined with a short reason for why the confidence is rated that way. For example: "High" + "Seen recently"; "Low" + "Weak match".

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing a concise and reliable confidence indicator, the system can reduce user uncertainty and increase trust in its outputs. This clarity will help the wearer better understand when to rely on the system's information versus making their own judgments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving memory cache policy will enhance the trustworthiness of name and face recall by ensuring that relevant memories are readily available during conversations, thus supporting the wearer's ability to understand and respond appropriately.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a local cache size limit for frequently encountered people based on recent interaction frequency and confidence in memory.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By setting a clear threshold for how much data is stored locally, we can ensure that the glasses provide accurate and timely recall of names and faces without overwhelming the device's storage or compromising battery life. This will improve the overall user experience by reducing latency and enhancing the reliability of memory support.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing stays on-device significantly reduces latency, which is crucial for maintaining real-time clarity and trust. By keeping this task phone-local, we can avoid any network delays or interruptions that might degrade the wearer's experience.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a rule that all live subtitle processing must run on the phone first, with only fallbacks to cloud for tasks that cannot be completed within strict latency constraints.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will ensure real-time accuracy and stability in subtitles, enhancing trust and daily-use value by providing immediate and reliable support during conversations. It also helps in maintaining a discreet UX by avoiding any network delays or interruptions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support, we ensure that the wearer can focus on the most critical information without being overwhelmed by secondary content. This enhances trust in the subtitle system by making it easier to read and understand during conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a subtitle-vs-one-line priority rule where subtitles always take precedence over one-line support, but one-line support is visible as needed without obstructing the main text.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This visual hierarchy will reduce cognitive load by ensuring that critical information (subtitles) remains the primary focus while still providing necessary context through one-line prompts when needed. It will enhance trust and usability in noisy environments where clear, reliable subtitles are essential.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
