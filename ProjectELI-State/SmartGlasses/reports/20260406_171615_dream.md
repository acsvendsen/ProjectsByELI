### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring subtitle placement is stable and unobtrusive is crucial for maintaining the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles. A fixed, well-defined placement rule ensures that the subtitles remain visible and readable without becoming a distraction, thereby enhancing the overall user experience.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 subtitle placement rules for different confidence states: high-confidence (e.g., speaker identified), medium-confidence (e.g., likely speaker but uncertain), and low-confidence (e.g., unknown speaker).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining clear, stable subtitle placement rules, the system will be less prone to distracting or confusing visual elements during live interactions. This will help maintain the wearer's focus on the conversation and improve overall usability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the system's assistance, thereby reinforcing the core mission of creating a reliable personal assistant. A well-designed confidence label and short reason will help the wearer understand the reliability of the information provided, which is crucial for maintaining trust in real-world interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear and concise format for confidence labels that includes both a numeric score and a brief description of its source. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing a clear indication of the confidence level, the system can avoid overwhelming the wearer with uncertain or potentially incorrect information. This improves trust and ensures that only reliable information is presented, enhancing the overall user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a recency-first eviction policy for local memory, we ensure that the most relevant and recent information is prioritized in the cache. This improves trust by providing the wearer with the latest and most useful context, while reducing the storage overhead on the phone.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit of 10 entries per person, with an eviction policy that prioritizes the most recently encountered entities for each face or name. This change ensures that frequently seen individuals are always available quickly, enhancing the speed and accuracy of memory recall.

3. **Likely payoff**

   The recency-first eviction policy will significantly improve the reliability and relevance of the wearer's memory support. By keeping recent interactions fresh in the cache, the system can provide faster, more accurate responses to repeated encounters with individuals, thus enhancing user trust and satisfaction.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a local cache size limit of 10 entries per person.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the phone/cloud boundary decision ensures that only essential data processes on the phone, reducing battery drain and thermal load while maintaining high functionality. This focus aligns with the project's mission of discreet, context-aware personal assistance, ensuring that the system remains lightweight and socially acceptable.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one specific task as a cloud fallback boundary in V1. For example, define when transcript cleanup tasks should fall back to the phone or remain local. This clarity will guide decisions on where processing happens, optimizing battery use while maintaining functionality.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly defining which tasks should run locally and which can be offloaded to the cloud, the system can significantly reduce its thermal load and battery consumption without sacrificing essential functionalities. This optimization will enhance user trust by ensuring that the glasses remain cool and operational for longer periods during use.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the phone/cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing to occur on the cloud if the glasses are idle for more than 10 seconds.
