### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   
   By refining subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we ensure that subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive while providing crucial support when needed most. This enhances the wearer's trust in the system by balancing visibility with real-time usability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a new subtitle placement rule where low-confidence text is shown as static subtitles at the bottom of the frame, while high-confidence text can be displayed dynamically but briefly to avoid distraction during critical moments.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This adjustment will help maintain high trust in the system by ensuring that critical information (high confidence) is presented without distraction, while less certain information remains unobtrusive yet available when needed. It also prevents potential visual overload and ensures that the wearer can focus on real-time conversation clarity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

This will make the system more reliable in critical real-time scenarios where quick memory recall is essential for understanding.

4. **Immediate next probe**
