### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles remain readable and unobtrusive while still providing accurate information when needed. This will help maintain the wearer's focus during conversations without overwhelming them with too much text.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two to three subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence scenarios where subtitles are essential, another for medium-confidence scenarios where they provide useful context, and a third for low-confidence scenarios where subtitles can be less prominent or appear only when the wearer frames their face close to the glasses.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will enhance the wearability of the SmartGlasses by ensuring that subtitles are appropriately visible in various contexts. It will reduce visual clutter, making it easier for the wearer to follow conversations without feeling overwhelmed by text. Additionally, this approach will help maintain a balance between providing useful information and keeping the glasses discreet.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Implement a simple memory cache policy where each person or name is stored in local cache with a fixed eviction threshold based on recent interaction frequency. For example, entries that have been seen within the last 30 minutes can be kept for up to 1 hour before being evicted.

3. **Likely payoff**

This approach will allow the system to maintain high-confidence face/name recall while reducing the overall memory footprint and processing load. It ensures that frequently encountered individuals are always available quickly, enhancing the wearer's experience without overwhelming the phone's resources.

4. **Immediate next probe**
