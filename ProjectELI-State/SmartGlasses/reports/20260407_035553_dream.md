### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a stable subtitle placement rule, we ensure that subtitles remain in a predictable and unobtrusive position on the screen. This stability improves trust by reducing distractions and maintaining visual consistency during live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single fixed subtitle placement mode where subtitles appear directly below the speaker's face for all confidence states. This simple rule prevents reactive adjustments that could disrupt the wearer’s focus.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A stable subtitle placement will enhance user trust and reduce cognitive load during conversations. The predictability of the subtitles' location allows the wearer to focus on listening and understanding without constantly adjusting their gaze or attention.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving the confidence display format in V1 will enhance the wearer's trust in the system by clearly indicating the certainty of information presented, such as subtitles and memory recall. This clarity can prevent misunderstandings and ensure that the wearer is aware when a piece of information might be less reliable.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a consistent confidence format that includes both a numeric score and a short label with an accompanying reason. For example, "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'". This format can help the wearer quickly understand the reliability of the information being presented.

3. **Likely payoff**

By implementing this confidence format, the system will be able to convey more nuanced information about the certainty of subtitles and memory recall in real-time. This will reduce the risk of misinterpretation and increase overall user trust, making the glasses a more reliable tool for understanding and interacting with their environment.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the numeric score presentation for the confidence display in V1.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Strengthening memory trust by ensuring that new-person memories are automatically linked to existing confidence objects, reducing the likelihood of incorrect or redundant information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a stable entity-linking rule that attaches new evidence to existing person-memory objects with high confidence scores based on recent encounters and repeated recognitions.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By consistently linking new evidence to established memory objects, the system can maintain a more accurate and reliable memory profile for individuals, reducing the risk of confusion or incorrect recall during conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing remains local to the phone minimizes latency, thereby maintaining real-time clarity and trust in noisy environments. This focus on local computation also aligns with the goal of low-friction assistance, as it reduces dependency on potentially slower cloud services.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear rule that live subtitle processing runs entirely on the phone without falling back to the cloud under any circumstances for V1.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing local, we ensure that subtitles remain crisp and timely, enhancing the wearer's ability to follow conversations in real-time. This will directly support the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity while minimizing battery drain and thermal stress on the glasses.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that subtitles are placed in a way that balances clarity with non-distracting placement improves the wearer's ability to follow conversations while reducing visual clutter. This enhances the wearer's trust in the system and their overall comfort during interactions, which is fundamental to the core mission of the SmartGlasses.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**

   By creating a stable and clear placement rule, users will experience more reliable and less intrusive subtitles, leading to higher satisfaction and trust in the system's ability to assist during real-time interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
