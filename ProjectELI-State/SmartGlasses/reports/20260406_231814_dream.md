### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are always clear and stable enough to be trusted in live conversations. This helps maintain the wearer's understanding and trust without overwhelming them with visual noise.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 2 to 3 subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: one for high-confidence matches (e.g., frequent speakers or known names), another for medium-confidence matches (e.g., new faces in a conversation), and a third for low-confidence matches (e.g., less frequently encountered names).

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these subtitle placement rules will reduce visual distraction, enhance the wearer's ability to follow conversations, and maintain trust in the system’s accuracy.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a clear threshold for local memory retention, we ensure that the system efficiently manages memory while maintaining high trust through reinforcement over time. This helps in reducing unnecessary data storage on the phone, thus conserving battery and thermal resources without compromising the quality of subtitle or memory assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache eviction policy that retains face/name memories for at least 15 minutes after they are first encountered but evicts them if not reinforced by additional context within an hour.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This policy will help in reducing the overall memory footprint on the phone, thereby improving battery efficiency and thermal management. It also ensures that frequently reused information remains accessible while clearing less relevant data to maintain a clean state.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Set a local cache eviction rule for face/name memories to retain them for at least 15 minutes but evict if not reinforced within an hour.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the subtitle-vs-one-line priority rule ensures that subtitles remain clear and stable while still providing useful one-line support. This balance helps maintain trust in real-time subtitle clarity (subtitle_clarity) by ensuring that the wearer can easily read and follow the conversation without being overwhelmed by too much information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a new visual hierarchy rule where subtitles are prioritized over one-line prompts when confidence is high, but one-line support can overlay on subtitles at low confidence levels to provide context without distraction. This rule ensures that critical real-time information remains clear while offering contextual help as needed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly defining this subtitle-vs-one-line priority rule, the system will better manage visual clutter and ensure that the wearer can trust their subtitles during live conversations. This improvement will enhance user satisfaction and maintain a low-friction assistive experience, aligning with the "low-friction assistance" attractor.

4. **Immediate next probe**
