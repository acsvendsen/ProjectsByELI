## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 design emphasizes frame-touch interactions and minimal hardware complexity to ensure lightweight, socially acceptable glasses while leveraging the phone for heavy processing tasks.
