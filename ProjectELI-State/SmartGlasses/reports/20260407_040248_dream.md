### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are always readable, stable, and appropriately positioned to enhance understanding without causing distraction. This will help maintain trust in the system's real-time capabilities and improve overall user experience.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple rule where subtitles are placed at the bottom center of the field of view for low confidence states (e.g., when names or faces are first seen) and slightly above the center for high confidence states (e.g., familiar people or well-known contexts).

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will make the subtitles more predictable and easier to read, reducing cognitive load during conversations. It will also help in reinforcing the system's reliability by consistently placing text where it is most useful without overwhelming the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By improving the confidence display format, we can enhance the trustworthiness and clarity of the subtitles and memory support, which are central to the SmartGlasses' core mission. A well-crafted confidence label and short reason will help users understand the reliability of the information presented, reducing cognitive load and increasing overall user satisfaction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear format for confidence labels that includes a numeric score, a label (such as "High", "Low", or "Medium"), and a short reason (e.g., "Seen recently" or "Weak match"). This format will provide users with a quick understanding of the reliability of the information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing a well-designed confidence display will enhance user trust in the system, leading to better adoption and daily use. It will also help in reducing cognitive overload by clearly indicating when the system is less certain about the information it presents, thus improving the overall experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a local cache threshold for face/name memory, we ensure that critical person memories are reliably stored on the phone, reducing reliance on cloud fallbacks and improving trust through stable behavior.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**

   This reduces latency and improves reliability in face/name recall, ensuring that the wearer can quickly access frequently encountered individuals even with intermittent cloud connectivity. It also enhances trust by providing consistent, fast responses.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

By carefully defining which processing stays local to the phone and which falls back to the cloud for live subtitle processing, we can ensure that subtitles remain real-time and stable enough to be trusted during conversations. This balance helps maintain the high attractor score of "Real-time subtitle clarity" while respecting battery and thermal constraints.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a specific rule for local vs. cloud subtitle processing: all short-term, low-latency subtitle tasks should run locally on the phone to ensure real-time performance. Longer or more complex subtitle refinement can fall back to the cloud when necessary.

3. **Likely payoff**

This small change will significantly enhance the core mission by ensuring that subtitles remain clear and trustworthy during live interactions. It reduces battery drain and thermal stress, maintaining a low-friction user experience that supports the primary attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity."

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle vs one-line support coexistence ensures that both types of information can be presented in a way that minimizes distraction while maximizing utility. This helps the wearer stay focused on their conversation without feeling overwhelmed, thereby enhancing the overall user experience and trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a visual hierarchy rule that gives subtitles higher precedence than one-line prompts but allows both to coexist without overlapping. Specifically, set a subtitle priority mode where subtitles are always displayed first, and one-line prompts are shown below or to the side if space permits.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining this subtitle priority rule, we ensure that critical information (subtitles) is not obscured by less essential cues (one-line prompts), which could lead to missed conversations or misunderstandings. This setup helps maintain a balance between clarity and utility, ensuring the wearer stays engaged without feeling burdened.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
