### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Stable subtitle placement rules enhance user trust by ensuring subtitles remain in a predictable, unobtrusive position, reducing cognitive load and distraction during live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three fixed subtitle placement modes: top center, bottom center, and bottom left for different confidence states (high, medium, low).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By establishing stable rules for subtitle placement, the system will maintain consistent visibility without overwhelming the wearer, thereby improving overall trust in the assistance provided.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format, we ensure that the wearer can quickly understand the reliability of the information being shown. This clarity helps in maintaining trust and reduces cognitive load by providing clear signals about the certainty of the displayed content.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific confidence object format for V1 that combines a numeric score with a label and a short reason. For example, "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'; score 0.42 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'; score 0.63 + 'Medium' + 'Name heard once in introduction'."

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will enhance the wearer's trust by providing clear and concise confidence signals. It will also simplify the visual hierarchy, making it easier for the wearer to focus on relevant information without being overwhelmed.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that face/name memory is stored with a clear and effective cache policy helps maintain trust by providing accurate and reliable support to the wearer without overwhelming them with too much information. This focus on local storage and efficient lookup ensures that when names or faces are encountered, the system can quickly provide relevant context, enhancing the wearer's ability to recall prior interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple cache eviction rule based on recency of interaction for face/name memory objects. For example, prioritize retaining recently seen and frequently recalled names over older ones that have not been accessed in some time.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a clear and efficient cache policy, the system can provide faster and more accurate name recall during live interactions, reducing the likelihood of errors or confusion. This improves overall trust by ensuring that when someone is mentioned, the correct information is quickly available without unnecessary delays.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one cache eviction rule for face/name memory objects based on recency of interaction to optimize local storage and lookup speed.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing remains phone-local for V1 reduces battery drain, keeps latency low, and maintains real-time clarity without thermal stress on the glasses. This balance is crucial for the wearer's trust in the system, as delayed or distorted subtitles can break their focus during critical interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a rule that all live subtitle processing stays phone-local until explicit user action requires cloud support, such as transcription cleanup or memory archive sync.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitle processing local, the system can provide faster, more stable, and clearer subtitles in real-time, reducing the risk of battery drain and thermal issues. This improves daily-use value and trust for the wearer, aligning with the core attractors of subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear visual hierarchy that prioritizes subtitles over one-line prompts when the wearer needs to focus on understanding can strengthen trust by making sure the most critical information is always visible and readable, even in noisy or complex social situations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single subtitle placement rule where subtitles are displayed above the one-line prompt area with a margin large enough for easy reading but not so much that it becomes distracting. This rule should be applied consistently across different confidence levels and user contexts.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By establishing a clear visual hierarchy, the wearer will trust the system more because critical information (subtitles) is always prioritized and easily readable. This reduces cognitive load and social awkwardness, enhancing the overall assistive experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
