### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles remain stable and readable, thus enhancing trust and real-time clarity during live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches (e.g., familiar names), another for medium-confidence matches (e.g., likely but not confirmed), and a third for low-confidence matches (e.g., newly encountered faces or names).

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these rules will help the wearer distinguish between different levels of confidence in real-time, leading to better trust in the subtitles and improved overall understanding during interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
