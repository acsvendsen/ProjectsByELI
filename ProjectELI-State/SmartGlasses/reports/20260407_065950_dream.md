### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules will enhance the clarity and readability of subtitles, ensuring they remain trustworthy in live conversations. This will directly support the attractor "Real-time subtitle clarity" by making sure subtitles are visible and stable enough for the wearer to understand and respond appropriately.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: one fixed at the top of the frame and another slightly below, reacting to confidence levels. This will allow for a balance between constant visibility and adaptive placement based on the certainty of the transcript.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing these two subtitle placement modes, we can ensure that subtitles are always visible enough to be trusted while minimizing distractions during high-confidence speech. This will improve overall user trust in the system's ability to provide accurate and timely information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a fixed position rule for subtitles at the top of the frame.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the local cache size for face/name memory ensures that frequently encountered people are remembered more reliably and quickly, enhancing the wearer's trust in the system’s ability to recall key individuals without delay or uncertainty.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache threshold of 10 most recently seen faces/names with frequent reinforcement over time for fast lookup. This simple policy ensures that commonly encountered people are prioritized and remembered consistently, reducing the need for cloud fallbacks and improving real-time recall accuracy.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting this threshold, the system will have a smaller set of high-confidence face/name memories readily available locally, leading to faster and more reliable lookups during live interactions. This reduces latency and enhances the overall trustworthiness of the memory support feature.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.
