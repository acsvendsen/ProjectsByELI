## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on V1 release with specific constraints and goals to keep the hardware lightweight and socially acceptable, maintain practical software, ensure reliable wireless interfaces, and deliver near-real-time subtitles. The current configuration ensures adherence to these constraints while aiming for a core mission of discreet personal assistance.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery, thermal pressure, weight, social acceptability
- progress: The project is adhering to the hardware constraints by focusing on frame-touch interactions and minimizing on-glasses processing. The chosen subtitle placement of 'More Prominent' aligns with the need for clear but non-distracting subtitles.
- next focus: Refine the specific rules for subtitle placement and ensure they do not significantly impact battery life or social acceptability.
- confidence: 0.8

## Software Stack
