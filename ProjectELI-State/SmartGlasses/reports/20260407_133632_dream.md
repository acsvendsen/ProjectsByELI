### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining clear subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we ensure that subtitles remain readable, stable, and trust-preserving in live conversation. This helps maintain the wearer's focus without overwhelming them with too much information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple two-rule placement system: 
   - High-confidence subtitles (90%+ certainty) always appear above the current line of speech.
   - Lower-confidence subtitles (60-89% certainty) appear below the current line but are slightly faded to avoid distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This clear and stable subtitle placement will improve overall usability by reducing visual clutter while ensuring that high-certainty information is easily visible. It will help maintain trust in the system's reliability, making the wearer more likely to rely on the subtitles for understanding.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By optimizing local memory thresholds for face/name recall, we can ensure that the system remembers new people effectively without overwhelming the wearer's trust in the product. This balance helps maintain a low-friction assistance experience while providing reliable support for names and prior context.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit of 5-10 recent face/name interactions to be stored on the phone before triggering cloud fallbacks or reinforcement.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing this cache size limit will help in:
   - Reducing battery and thermal load by minimizing frequent cloud queries.
   - Ensuring that new people are remembered with a reasonable level of confidence over time.
   - Maintaining a balance between rich memory support and fast lookup, enhancing the overall trustworthiness of face/name recall.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Enhancing the visual hierarchy to ensure subtitles remain readable while one-line prompts are minimally intrusive benefits the core by maintaining focus on real-time subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance. This helps the wearer stay focused on understanding conversations without unnecessary distractions, thereby reinforcing the mission of reducing social and cognitive friction.

   Define a visual priority rule that prioritizes subtitles over one-line prompts in high-concentration areas (e.g., directly above or to the side) but allows one-line prompts to appear as needed in less critical zones without overlapping or obscuring the subtitles.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting this visual hierarchy, the system will deliver more readable and actionable real-time subtitles while still providing necessary one-line support when contextually appropriate. This balance ensures that the wearer remains informed but not overwhelmed, enhancing trust and daily-use value.
