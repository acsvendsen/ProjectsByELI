### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a consistent subtitle placement rule, we ensure that subtitles remain stable and easily readable in various contexts. This stability helps maintain trust in the system's real-time assistance, which is crucial for the wearer to rely on the glasses during noisy environments or dense conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single fixed subtitle position at the bottom center of the field of view, with a slight offset to avoid direct gaze overlap and minimize distraction but still ensure visibility.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This fixed placement rule will reduce the cognitive load on the wearer by minimizing the need for visual tracking of subtitles. It will also help maintain consistency in the user experience, ensuring that subtitles remain a reliable source of information without causing distractions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the fixed position of subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a local memory threshold for storing face/name evidence, the system ensures that critical information is retained while managing battery and thermal constraints. This improves trust by reinforcing reliable memory recall and enhances the wearer's understanding of ongoing interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a recency-based cache eviction policy that keeps recently seen faces and names in local memory for up to 30 minutes, with less frequently encountered entities stored only until they are reinforced at least once within this period.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This threshold will help maintain a balance between rich memory support and limited resource usage. It ensures that the most relevant and recent information is readily accessible, thereby enhancing the wearer's trust in the system's ability to recall names and faces accurately.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

1. **Why it benefits the core**

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By keeping live subtitle processing on the phone, we can ensure minimal latency and high-quality output, which is crucial for maintaining trust during conversations. This will likely result in a more reliable and user-trustworthy experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
