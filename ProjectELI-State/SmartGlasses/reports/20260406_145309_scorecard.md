## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through V1 features such as live subtitles, face/name recall, and conversation memory. The project must adhere to strict hardware and software constraints to ensure real-time responsiveness and minimal battery drain while maintaining privacy and social acceptability.

- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery

- next focus: Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
