### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a fixed subtitle placement rule, we ensure that subtitles remain stable and predictable, which is crucial for real-time understanding and trust in noisy environments. This consistency helps the wearer focus on the conversation without being distracted by unpredictable subtitle positions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed position for subtitles at the bottom center of the frame, with a slight margin from the edges to avoid occlusion but ensuring readability.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A fixed subtitle placement will reduce visual distractions and improve trust in the real-time assistance provided by the glasses. This stability will help the wearer maintain their focus on the conversation, enhancing overall understanding and comfort during interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a fixed position for subtitles at the bottom center of the frame.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format in V1 will enhance the trustworthiness of both subtitles and memory recall, ensuring that the wearer can easily understand the level of certainty provided by the system. This clarity is crucial for maintaining a balance between assistance and social acceptability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one clear confidence format that uses a numeric score (e.g., 0.84), a label (e.g., "High"), and a short reason (e.g., "Seen recently") to convey the certainty of the information provided by the system.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By using a simple, consistent confidence format, the wearer can quickly understand how reliable the subtitles or memory recall is, which helps in making better decisions during interactions. This transparency builds trust and enhances the overall user experience without adding unnecessary complexity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what runs on the phone versus what falls back to the cloud, we ensure that V1 is focused on delivering reliable and low-friction assistance without overloading the device or compromising privacy. This clarity helps in maintaining the core attractors of real-time subtitle clarity and memory trust.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one clear task-specific fallback boundary for exactly one V1 task, such as live subtitle processing. For example, decide that all live subtitle processing must remain on the phone to ensure real-time response times, while less critical tasks like transcript cleanup can fall back to the cloud.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing local, we maintain fast and reliable performance, which is crucial for the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity. Additionally, this will help in maintaining a clear privacy boundary by not relying on the cloud for critical assistive behaviors.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle-vs-one-line priority ensures that subtitles remain clear, stable, and trusted in live conversations while ensuring one-line prompts are not overwhelming or distracting. This balance helps maintain the wearer's focus on the conversation without being overburdened by additional text.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a visual hierarchy rule where subtitles always have precedence over one-line support unless the one-line content is critical and time-sensitive, such as urgent instructions or warnings.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By ensuring subtitles remain the primary focus, the system can maintain high trust in real-time communication, reducing cognitive load and social friction for the wearer. This will enhance overall user satisfaction and confidence in the product's effectiveness during live interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support, prioritizing subtitles with a small delay to accommodate critical one-line content if necessary.
