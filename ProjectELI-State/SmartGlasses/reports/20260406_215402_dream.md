### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a small set of stable subtitle placement rules, we can ensure that subtitles are always readable and do not distract from the conversation. This will help maintain trust and improve the wearer's ability to follow the dialogue in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: one for high-confidence matches, another for medium-confidence matches, and a third for low-confidence matches. Each rule should specify a fixed position relative to the face or text box boundaries.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing stable subtitle placement rules will reduce visual distractions and ensure that subtitles are always readable. This will help maintain the wearer's focus on the conversation without being overwhelmed by too many subtitles. It also sets a strong foundation for future improvements in subtitle clarity and stability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving confidence display can significantly enhance trust in subtitle quality and memory support, making the wearer feel more confident about understanding the conversation. Clear and consistent confidence labels will help the wearer discern between highly certain and less certain information, reducing cognitive load and social awkwardness.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a simple yet clear confidence format that combines a numeric score with a label and short reason. For example: "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'; score 0.42 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'. This format provides both precision and context without overwhelming the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**
   A well-defined confidence display will help in building trust with the user, ensuring they understand when the system is certain versus uncertain about its information. This clarity can lead to more effective use of the assistant’s support features and reduced reliance on incorrect assumptions or skepticism.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
