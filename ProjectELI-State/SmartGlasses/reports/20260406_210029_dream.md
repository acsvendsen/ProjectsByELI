### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a stable subtitle placement rule that prioritizes readability over reactivity, we ensure that subtitles remain consistently visible and not distracting during live conversations. This improves real-time subtitle clarity, which is one of our primary attractors.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement mode where subtitles are shown below the current line of text for all confidence levels. This simple rule ensures that subtitles do not shift around too much and remain stable on the screen.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A fixed subtitle placement will reduce visual distraction during live conversations, making the subtitles more readable and trustworthy. This will help users stay focused on the conversation while still receiving real-time support, thereby enhancing their understanding without adding complexity to the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
