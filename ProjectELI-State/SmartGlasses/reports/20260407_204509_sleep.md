# Recent Consolidations

## Strengthening Signals
1. **Subtitle Clarity**:
   - Focus on ensuring subtitles remain clear, fast, and stable enough for live conversation.
   - Ensure that the system can handle various environmental noises without degradation.

2. **Memory Trust**:
   - Continue to develop memory support that accurately remembers names, faces, and prior context while maintaining a high level of trustworthiness.
   - Reinforce confidence objects and ensure they are updated with each interaction.

3. **Low-Friction Assistance**:
   - Deliver concise one-line prompts and visible support without overwhelming the user or introducing unnecessary command overhead.
   - Ensure that any additional visual elements do not detract from the primary function of understanding and responding in real-time interactions.

## Weakening Signals
1. **Always-On Sensing vs Battery & Thermals**:
   - Reduce reliance on always-on sensing to minimize battery drain and thermal issues.
   - Prioritize processing on the phone or cloud where necessary, but avoid unnecessary heavy lifting on glasses hardware.

2. **Rich Context vs Low-Latency Response**:
   - Aim for timely responses rather than overly rich context that could introduce latency.
   - Balance between providing enough contextual information to be helpful and maintaining a quick response time.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
1. **Privacy vs Usefulness**:
   - Ensure that the system provides useful assistive behavior without invading privacy or creating false certainty.
   - Strive for a balance where the system aids the wearer without overstepping boundaries.

2. **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**:
   - Maintain a discreet design to avoid social awkwardness while ensuring subtitles and one-line prompts are clear enough for quick understanding.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
1. **Subtitle Placement & Visual Hierarchy**:
   - Revisit the current subtitle placement strategy to ensure it remains unobtrusive yet readable.
   - Explore different visual hierarchy designs that can enhance readability without causing distraction.

2. **Phone/Cloud Boundary**:
   - Consider the best ways to leverage cloud processing for heavy tasks while keeping critical real-time functions on the phone or glasses hardware.
   - Ensure that any cloud interaction is well-justified and does not introduce significant latency.
