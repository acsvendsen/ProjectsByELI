## Project Summary
The project is currently focused on improving core mission areas such as subtitle clarity, memory trust, and low-friction assistance while maintaining a balance between privacy and functionality. Key tensions like latency vs richness and discreet UX vs visual clarity are being actively managed to ensure the system remains useful and socially acceptable.

## Hardware Stack

- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery
- progress: The glasses maintain a simple design with minimal on-glasses processing, aligning well with the goal of keeping V1 lightweight and socially acceptable. The focus remains on frame-touch interactions and light sensor inputs, reducing thermal and battery pressure.
- next focus: Ensure that any added components do not exceed minimal on-glasses compute requirements to maintain social acceptability and reduce power usage.
