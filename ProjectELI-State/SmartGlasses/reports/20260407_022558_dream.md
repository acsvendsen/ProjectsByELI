### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules helps maintain clarity and trust in real-time subtitles, ensuring that the wearer can focus on understanding the conversation without visual distractions. Stable subtitle positioning also supports the low-friction assistance attractor by making the subtitled information more reliable and unobtrusive.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that keeps the subtitle text at the bottom of the screen, centered or slightly offset from the center, to avoid overlapping with any part of the wearer's face or other important visual elements.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By establishing a consistent and unobtrusive subtitle placement, the system can maintain better trust in noisy environments where subtitles are critical for understanding. This reduces cognitive load and increases the likelihood that the wearer will rely on the glasses for assistance without feeling self-conscious about the device's presence.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a local cache size threshold for face/name memory objects, we can ensure that critical person memories are retained on the phone for fast lookup and easy reinforcement over time. This improves trust by making sure key identities remain accessible even if the cloud connection is weak or unavailable.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a policy where the local cache retains up to 10 most recently seen faces/names, with an additional buffer of 5 less-frequent encounters. This ensures that core entities are always available for quick recall and reinforcement without overwhelming the device's storage.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This local caching strategy will reduce latency in face/name recognition during conversations, allowing for faster and more reliable memory support. It also enhances the overall trustworthiness of the system by ensuring that key identities remain accessible even when network conditions are poor.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.
