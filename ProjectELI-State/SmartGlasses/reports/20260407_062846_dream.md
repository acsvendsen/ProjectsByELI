### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Stable subtitle placement rules in V1 will help ensure that subtitles remain readable and unobtrusive, reducing cognitive load during live conversations. This stability aligns with the attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity" and helps maintain the wearer's trust without becoming distracting.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule for different confidence states: 
   - High-confidence subtitles at the top center.
   - Medium-confidence subtitles slightly below the high-confidence ones, but still visible.
   - Low-confidence subtitles in a smaller font or hidden if not essential.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting clear and consistent subtitle placement rules, V1 will provide more reliable and less intrusive support to the wearer. This will enhance trust and usability without overwhelming the user with too much information at once.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format, we can ensure that the system's reliability is clearly communicated to the wearer. This clarity helps in building trust and ensuring that the wearer understands when they should rely on the subtitles or seek additional context.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple yet effective confidence object format that includes both a numeric score and a label, along with a short reason for the assessment.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A clear and consistent confidence display will help in reducing cognitive load by making the wearer trust the subtitles more. This can lead to better engagement and less need for constant confirmation of the spoken content, enhancing overall user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a local cache size limit for face/name memory, the system can balance the need for fast recall with the risk of storing potentially inaccurate or outdated information. This helps in maintaining trust by ensuring that memory objects are refreshed and reinforced appropriately over time.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific threshold for the local cache on the phone, such as "keep up to 10 face/name memories per person with a recent encounter within the last week."

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing this limit will help in managing memory efficiently without overwhelming the device, thus improving the reliability and accuracy of recall during live interactions. This can lead to more trustworthy behavior and reduced risk of displaying outdated or incorrect information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set a local cache size limit of 10 face/name memories per person for V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud, we ensure that V1 interactions remain lightweight and socially acceptable. This helps in maintaining the core mission of being a discreet personal assistant without overloading the glasses hardware or creating unnecessary privacy concerns.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one specific task boundary where the glasses will handle live subtitle processing locally. For example, keep subtitles within a 3-second delay window to ensure they are displayed nearly instantaneously.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitle processing phone-local and ensuring sub-3-second latency, we can maintain high trust and social acceptability. This will enable the glasses to provide real-time assistance without draining battery or overheating the device, thereby preserving the wearer's ability to use them comfortably in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle vs one-line support coexistence ensures that the wearer can easily distinguish between critical subtitles and contextual prompts. This clarity is crucial for maintaining focus during conversations, reducing cognitive load, and preserving trust in the system's accuracy.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a visual hierarchy rule that prioritizes subtitles over one-line prompts when the confidence of the subtitle is high but the context suggests useful supplementary information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly distinguishing between critical subtitles and contextual prompts, users will find the system more reliable and less distracting. This improvement will enhance the overall user experience, making the glasses a more valuable daily tool.

4. **Immediate next probe**
