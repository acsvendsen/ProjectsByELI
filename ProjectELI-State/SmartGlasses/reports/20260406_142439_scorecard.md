## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering a discrete, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles and memory support. The V1 version is constrained to minimal hardware requirements and phone-first processing with limited cloud usage. Current efforts are centered on subtitle placement, confidence display, memory caching, and visual hierarchy design while adhering to strict interaction constraints.

- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The hardware stack is aligned with the goal of minimal on-glasses processing. The glasses are designed to remain simple and non-distracting, focusing on essential text display capabilities.
- next focus: Define specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels to minimize visual distractions while ensuring critical information remains visible.
