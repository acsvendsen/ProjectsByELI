### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules will enhance the wearer's ability to read subtitles quickly without distraction, thereby improving real-time subtitle clarity and overall user trust. Stable subtitle placement reduces visual fatigue and ensures that important information is always in a predictable location, making the system more reliable for noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two fixed subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence situations (e.g., common speakers or known contexts) where subtitles are placed at the top of the screen to minimize distraction but remain visible, and another for lower-confidence situations (e.g., new people or less familiar contexts) where subtitles can be slightly more prominent but still avoid overwhelming the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing these fixed placement rules, the system will provide a clearer and more predictable visual experience, which can significantly enhance user trust and comfort during live interactions. This improvement will align with the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity and help maintain a low-friction user interface that does not overwhelm the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
