### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Stable subtitle placement rules enhance the wearer's trust in the system by ensuring that subtitles remain visually consistent and unobtrusive during conversations. This consistency is crucial for maintaining the wearer's focus on the conversation rather than the display itself, thereby improving overall understanding and engagement.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a fixed subtitle placement rule for V1 that places subtitles at the bottom of the field of view (FOV), centered horizontally, and slightly below the center vertically. This simple rule ensures that subtitles are always in a predictable position without causing distraction or discomfort.

3. **Likely payoff**

By setting a stable subtitle placement rule, the system can provide more reliable and less disruptive subtitle support during live conversations. This predictability will help build trust with the wearer by ensuring that they consistently know where to look for subtitles, thereby reducing cognitive load and improving the overall user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
