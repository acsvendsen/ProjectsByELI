1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are always readable and do not distract from the conversation flow. This will enhance trust by making sure the wearer can rely on the subtitles without feeling overwhelmed.

   Define a set of 3 to 5 subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: high-confidence, medium-confidence, and low-confidence states. For example:
   - High confidence: Place subtitles at the bottom center.
   - Medium confidence: Place subtitles slightly above the bottom center.
   - Low confidence: Reduce subtitle size or place them temporarily in a less prominent position.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will lead to more consistent and reliable subtitle placement, reducing distraction during conversations. It will also help in building trust by ensuring that the subtitles are always easily readable and do not interfere with natural interactions.
