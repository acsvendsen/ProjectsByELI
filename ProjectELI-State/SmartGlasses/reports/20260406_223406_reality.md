### Confidence Display Feasibility
**Confidence Display:**
- **Current Core:** Represent confidence with a label or short reason to build trust.
- **Constraints:**
  - **Battery Draw:** Avoid complex animations or high-resolution displays that could drain the battery.
  - **Privacy Perception:** Ensure the display is clear but not overly intrusive, maintaining social acceptability.

**Conclusion:** Feasible Now

- **Constraints:**

**Conclusion:** Feasible Now

**Phone/Cloud Boundary:**
- **Current Core:** Phone-first runtime for heavy processing.

  - **Battery Draw:** Minimize cloud dependency to preserve phone battery life.

**Conclusion:** Feasible Now
