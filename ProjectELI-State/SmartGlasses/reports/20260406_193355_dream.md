### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we ensure that subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive while still providing crucial information when needed.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a simple rule where subtitles are placed at the bottom of the screen for low-confidence matches but dynamically shift to the top during high-confidence speaker detection to avoid occlusion by head movements.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This placement strategy will reduce visual clutter and improve subtitle readability, especially in noisy environments or when wearing glasses. It will also enhance the wearer's confidence in the system’s accuracy without overwhelming them with too much information at once.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving confidence display ensures that the wearer can trust the system's output more reliably, reducing uncertainty and enhancing the overall user experience. Clear and honest confidence indicators help maintain the balance between helpfulness and realism, ensuring that the wearer is not misled by uncertain information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple, neutral confidence format using a score plus label with a short reason: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"` for high certainty and `score 0.42 + "Low" + "Weak match"` for low certainty.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a clear and concise confidence format, the system will gain credibility with users, reducing confusion and fostering trust. This improvement will help users understand when to rely on the system's output and when to seek clarification, thereby enhancing the overall user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving local cache size ensures that frequently accessed memory objects, such as names and recent context, are readily available to the wearer without unnecessary cloud requests, reducing latency and maintaining a smooth user experience.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a local cache threshold for face/name recall that evicts less-recently used entries after a set period of inactivity, ensuring the cache remains manageable while retaining essential memory objects.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By setting an optimal local cache size and eviction policy, the system will reduce latency during face/name recall, enhance the reliability of one-line support, and maintain clear, uninterrupted real-time subtitles, thereby improving overall user trust and daily-use value.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a local cache threshold for face/name memory that evicts entries older than 30 days to maintain a manageable cache size.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary between what runs phone-local and what falls back to cloud, we ensure that critical processing remains on-device, reducing latency and preserving battery life while maintaining high trust and usability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**

   This decision will ensure that the glasses remain lightweight and responsive, maintaining high trust through low latency while still leveraging cloud power for tasks that benefit from heavier processing capabilities.

4. **Immediate next probe**
