### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules help maintain a consistent user experience, reducing distractions and enhancing readability. This consistency is crucial for building trust and ensuring that subtitles do not shift unpredictably during conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 2 to 3 fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: high-confidence faces, medium-confidence voices, and low-confidence non-verbal cues.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining stable subtitle placement rules, the system will provide a more predictable and user-friendly experience. This predictability can lead to reduced cognitive load for the wearer and increased trust in the system's reliability during interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
