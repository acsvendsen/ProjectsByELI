### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, V1 can ensure that subtitles remain readable and stable without becoming distracting. This improves real-time subtitle clarity (subtitle_clarity attractor) while maintaining trust in live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 2 to 3 subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches where the system is very sure, another for medium-confidence matches where there is some uncertainty, and a third for low-confidence matches where the system is unsure but still wants to provide assistance.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these rules will help ensure that subtitles are appropriately placed based on their certainty level, reducing visual distractions while maintaining clarity and trust during live interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format to use a numeric score with a label and short reason, we can provide users with clear and concise certainty information that builds trust over time. This approach ensures that users understand not just if something is remembered or recalled, but also why they are seeing certain pieces of information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   To unlock this improvement, we need to define a format for the confidence object that combines a numeric score with a label and a short reason. For example, "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'" or "score 0.42 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'". This format can be applied consistently across all memory-related interactions.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing this confidence display will help build trust by providing clear and understandable certainty levels. Users will feel more confident in the system’s accuracy, which can lead to increased daily use and better overall satisfaction with the product. Additionally, it will set expectations for the wearer about when they should pay particular attention to certain pieces of information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving subtitle-vs-one-line priority rules ensures that subtitles remain clear, stable, and unobtrusive while still providing essential context. This focus enhances the wearer's understanding without overwhelming them with too much information at once, maintaining trust in real-time assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a simple subtitle vs one-line support coexistence rule: prioritize subtitles over one-line prompts when confidence is high and vice versa for low confidence states. This ensures that critical information (subtitles) remains visible while still providing necessary context (one-liners).

3. **Likely payoff**

By maintaining clear, stable, and relevant subtitle visibility, the system will be less likely to overwhelm or distract the wearer. This improves overall trust in real-time assistance, ensuring that key information is always accessible without causing cognitive overload.

4. **Immediate next probe**
