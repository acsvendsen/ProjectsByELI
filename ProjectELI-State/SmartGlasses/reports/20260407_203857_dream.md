### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules will ensure that subtitles remain stable and unobtrusive while still being easily readable. This enhances the wearer's ability to follow conversations in real-time, which is crucial for maintaining trust and utility.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: one for high confidence states where subtitles can be placed slightly lower on the screen, and another for low confidence states where subtitles should be moved higher to avoid visual clutter but remain legible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By creating more stable and unobtrusive subtitle placement rules, the system will become less distracting while still providing reliable assistance. This will enhance user trust and satisfaction, making it a core component of the assistive experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

   A well-crafted confidence display will enhance the trustworthiness of the subtitles and memory support. It will help the wearer understand when to rely on the system's information, leading to better comprehension and reduced cognitive load in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
