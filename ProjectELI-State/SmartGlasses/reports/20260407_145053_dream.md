   Ensuring stable subtitle placement helps maintain the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles. By defining fixed position rules for different confidence states, V1 can reduce distractions and ensure that critical information is always visible.

   Define two fixed subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence text (e.g., names and faces) and another for lower-confidence text (e.g., background conversations). This will help maintain a clean visual hierarchy while ensuring important details are not missed.

   Stable, predictable subtitle placement will reduce cognitive load by minimizing distractions during live interactions. The wearer can focus more on the conversation rather than constantly adjusting their gaze between the glasses and surrounding environment.
