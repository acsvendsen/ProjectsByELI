### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain readable, stable, and unobtrusive during live conversations, which is crucial for maintaining the wearer's trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule: place subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view (FOV) with minimal movement unless the speaker changes or the confidence level drops below a certain threshold.

3. **Likely payoff**
   This rule will reduce distractions and ensure that subtitles remain visible without obstructing the wearer's primary visual focus, enhancing their trust in the system’s ability to provide clear and unobtrusive assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring clear, stable, and trust-preserving subtitle quality directly supports the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity. By optimizing confidence display to accurately reflect the reliability of the subtitles, we can enhance user trust in the system's assistive capabilities.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a new confidence format that combines a numeric score with a label and a short reason for why the confidence is rated that way. For example: "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'; score 0.63 + 'Medium' + 'Name heard once in introduction'."

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this new confidence format, we can provide users with a more precise understanding of the reliability of the subtitles and other assistive features. This increased transparency will help users trust the system more, leading to better overall user satisfaction and engagement.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a robust local memory cache policy for names and faces helps maintain the trustworthiness of name recall and face recognition, thereby strengthening the SmartGlasses' ability to provide accurate and reliable assistance in real-time interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple local cache eviction rule that retains recently seen names and faces based on frequency of interaction or recent encounter timestamps, without continuously updating memory objects.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a well-defined local cache policy, the system can maintain accurate name recall for frequently encountered individuals, ensuring that the wearer receives timely and relevant assistance while reducing the risk of privacy concerns associated with continuous memory updates.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a recency rule that retains recently seen names and faces in local memory based on interaction frequency or timestamps.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining clear boundaries for what runs on the phone versus the cloud, we ensure that V1 remains focused and efficient. This clarity helps in maintaining a low-friction user experience while ensuring privacy and utility are balanced correctly.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define explicit rules for which tasks run locally on the phone and which ones fall back to the cloud for memory archive syncs, specifically focusing on live subtitle processing versus conversation snapshot uploads.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly defining these boundaries, we can:
   - Reduce latency in critical real-time interactions like subtitles.
   - Ensure privacy by keeping sensitive data (like full transcripts) off the phone and on the cloud.
   - Streamline the architecture for easier maintenance and optimization as the project evolves.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving the visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support ensures that the wearer can easily read critical information while minimizing distractions. This enhances both subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance, maintaining trust in real-time communication.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a priority rule where one-line prompts are suppressed when high-confidence subtitles are active, but allow one-line support to reappear when subtitles are less clear or lower confidence.

3. **Likely payoff**

This prioritization will ensure that the wearer remains focused on critical information by reducing visual clutter and distraction. It will enhance the overall user experience by making it easier for the wearer to follow the conversation while providing necessary context through one-line prompts when needed.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
