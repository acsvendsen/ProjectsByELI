### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement stability enhances trust in the system's real-time performance, ensuring that the subtitles do not distract the wearer or cause cognitive strain during conversations. Stable, predictable subtitle placement allows the wearer to focus on the conversation without being interrupted by rapidly shifting text.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule for each confidence state, such as "always below the conversation area" for high-confidence matches and "slightly above the bottom of the screen" for low-confidence matches.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing stable subtitle rules, users will experience more consistent and reliable subtitles, leading to increased trust in the system's accuracy and reliability during live conversations. This stability also reduces cognitive load on the wearer, making the product feel more polished and professional.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
