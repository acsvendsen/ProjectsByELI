### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement helps maintain the wearer's trust in the system by making subtitles easily readable and distraction-free during live conversations. A consistent placement strategy reduces cognitive load, allowing the wearer to focus on the conversation rather than constantly adjusting their gaze or reading position.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that prioritizes stable visibility over dynamic adjustments based on confidence levels. For example, place subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view and adjust only if the confidence score drops below a certain threshold.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a stable subtitle placement rule, the system will be less distracting to the wearer during live conversations. This improves the overall user experience by reducing cognitive effort required for following the conversation, thereby enhancing the core mission of making the wearer understand and respond better in real-world interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud, we ensure that V1 focuses on delivering essential real-time subtitle and memory support without overloading the glasses hardware. This clarity helps maintain low latency, preserves battery life, and enhances the discreet UX.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single task boundary for live subtitle processing as phone-local, with all other tasks falling back to cloud. Specifically, frame touches for subtitles should trigger local processing, while cloud processing handles transcript cleanup, face/name recall lookup, memory archive sync, and conversation snapshot upload only when necessary.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing entirely on the phone, we can ensure that subtitles are always available with minimal latency, enhancing real-time clarity and trust. This local processing also reduces battery drain, allowing for longer continuous use without recharging.

4. **Immediate next probe**
