### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Enhancing subtitle placement rules helps maintain clear, stable subtitles that are easy to read and trust in live conversations, which is critical for the wearer's understanding and engagement.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define three specific subtitle placement rules: 
   - **Fixed Position:** Subtitles appear at a fixed location on the screen, ensuring they do not move or animate.
   - **Confidence-Based Adjustment:** Subtitles adjust their position slightly based on confidence levels (e.g., lower confidence subtitles may be slightly larger and more prominent).
   - **Context-Sensitive Overlay:** Subtitles overlay key information such as speaker names in a less intrusive manner.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By defining clear subtitle placement rules, we can ensure that the wearer remains focused on the conversation without being distracted by inconsistent or overly dynamic subtitles. This will enhance the overall user experience and trust in the system’s ability to provide real-time assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the fixed position rule for subtitles to appear at a consistent screen location.

This helps maintain the core mission of providing discreet, context-aware personal assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
