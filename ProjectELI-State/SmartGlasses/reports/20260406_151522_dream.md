### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain a consistent user experience, reducing cognitive load and improving overall trust in the system's reliability. Stable placement ensures that subtitles remain visible and readable without constant movement or distraction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: "High", "Medium", and "Low". For instance, high-confidence subtitles can be placed in a more prominent but non-distracting position, while low-confidence ones might be slightly less visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By establishing stable subtitle placement rules, the system will provide a predictable and reassuring experience for the wearer. This consistency will help build trust over time as users become accustomed to where they can find subtitles without constant adjustments, enhancing overall user satisfaction and the perception of reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
