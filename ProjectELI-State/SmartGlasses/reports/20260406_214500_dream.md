### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are always clear and usable without becoming distracting. This helps maintain real-time clarity (attractor: subtitle_clarity) and supports low-friction assistance (attractor: low_friction_assistance), ensuring the wearer can focus on the conversation without visual interruptions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three distinct subtitle placement rules:
   - **High Confidence Subtitles:** Place subtitles above the speaker's face for high-confidence matches.
   - **Medium Confidence Subtitles:** Position subtitles slightly off-center to the right of the speaker’s face for medium-confidence matches.
   - **Low Confidence Subtitles:** Display these as a brief text block at the bottom of the screen with low opacity, ensuring they do not obstruct the primary view.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will enhance user trust in subtitle accuracy by reducing visual distractions during high-confidence matches while still providing critical information when needed. It will also maintain a clean and unobtrusive interface, supporting the core mission of discreet assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
