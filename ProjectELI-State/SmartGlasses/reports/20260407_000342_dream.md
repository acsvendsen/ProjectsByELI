### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules under different confidence states ensures that subtitles remain clear, readable, and stable enough to be trusted in live conversations. This helps maintain the wearer's focus and understanding without causing distractions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed position for high-confidence subtitles (90%+ certainty) placed above the conversation feed, with lower confidence subtitles (70-89%) below or next to the speaker’s name. This simple rule ensures that highly certain information is prominently displayed while less certain information is easily accessible but not intrusive.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By establishing clear and stable subtitle placement rules, users will have a consistent experience where high-confidence subtitles are always visible and easy to follow, enhancing their trust in the system’s ability to provide accurate and timely support during conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format can enhance trust in subtitle accuracy and memory support. A clear and concise confidence label with a short reason will help the wearer understand when to rely on the subtitles or memories, thus reducing cognitive load and increasing overall usability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence format that includes a numeric score (e.g., 0-1), a confidence label (e.g., High, Medium, Low), and a short reason (e.g., "Seen recently", "Weak match"). This can be integrated into the subtitle rendering process to provide real-time feedback on reliability.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing a clear and concise confidence format, users will better understand when to trust the subtitles or memories. This reduces uncertainty and improves the overall user experience by ensuring that the assistive behavior is reliable and transparent.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By optimizing local memory thresholds for face/name recall, we ensure that the system can quickly access relevant memories without overloading the phone's limited resources. This improves real-time subtitle clarity and trustworthiness by allowing the glasses to provide immediate support when needed.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit of 5-10 recent face/name encounters for quick lookup on the phone, with a fallback to cloud if necessary but only after an explicit touch-based reinforcement.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will reduce latency in memory recall, ensuring that common interactions are smoother and more responsive. It also preserves battery by reducing continuous data syncs, leading to longer usable sessions without interruptions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle clarity by ensuring real-time subtitles remain stable and fast enough to trust in live conversations directly supports the primary attractor of Real-time subtitle clarity (subtitle_clarity). By optimizing subtitle placement rules, we can reduce visual distraction while maintaining readability, thereby enhancing the wearer's ability to understand and respond effectively.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single subtitle placement rule that prioritizes lower corners for subtitles during high-confidence matches, ensuring they do not interfere with face recognition or text reading but still remain easily readable. This rule can be implemented without affecting other visual elements, keeping the design simple and focused.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining this subtitle placement rule, we reduce visual clutter and improve the wearer's ability to focus on the conversation while simultaneously receiving necessary subtitles. This enhances trust in the system’s capability to provide real-time assistance and supports the core mission of reducing social and cognitive friction.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By optimizing the visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support, we can ensure that critical information remains clear and prominent while minimizing distractions. This improves the wearer's ability to focus on essential cues without overloading their attention.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single subtitle placement rule for high-confidence interactions where subtitles must be clearly visible but not intrusive. For lower confidence or one-line support, prefer a coexistence mode that slightly de-emphasizes these elements without hiding them entirely.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will enhance the wearer's trust in the system by ensuring that key information stands out when it matters most, while reducing visual clutter during less critical times. It will also help maintain the product’s low-friction and discreet UX, which are critical attractors.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
