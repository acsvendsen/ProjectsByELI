### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the real-time subtitle clarity, making them more readable and stable during live conversations. This ensures that subtitles do not become distracting or break the wearer's trust in the assistive system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule based on confidence levels: 
   
   - High confidence (seen recently): place subtitles at the bottom of the display.
   - Medium confidence (name heard once): center the text horizontally and vertically, with subtle animation to catch attention without distraction.
   - Low confidence (weak match): show as a brief tooltip or in a corner.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will reduce distractions by placing subtitles appropriately according to their certainty level. It will also help maintain the wearer's focus on live conversation, improving overall understanding and trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
