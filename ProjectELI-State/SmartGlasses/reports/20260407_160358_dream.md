### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By defining a fixed subtitle placement rule that prioritizes readability and stability over reactivity, we ensure that subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive during live conversations. This enhances trust in the system's ability to provide accurate and timely information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a single fixed subtitle position at the bottom center of the field of view (FOV), with an adjustable margin from the edge that allows for text to be read easily without causing distraction during movement or interaction.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - A fixed and stable subtitle placement will reduce cognitive load by ensuring subtitles are always in a predictable location, enhancing trust and usability. This stability will also minimize visual distractions, making it easier for the wearer to focus on the conversation while still having access to the necessary information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
