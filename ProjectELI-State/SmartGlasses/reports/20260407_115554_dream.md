### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules helps ensure that subtitles remain readable and stable, which directly supports the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity. By defining clear placement rules, we can reduce visual distractions and maintain the wearer's focus on the conversation.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a minimal set of subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels. For example, low-confidence subtitles could be placed at the bottom right corner with a slightly smaller font size to avoid distraction, while high-confidence subtitles could be centered at the top for better readability and ease of following.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing clear and minimal subtitle placement rules, we can ensure that subtitles remain readable and stable during live conversations. This will help maintain the wearer's trust in the system’s ability to provide accurate and timely information without overwhelming their visual field.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
