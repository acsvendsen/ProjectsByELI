## Concrete Tensions Around Core Decision Areas
1. **Subtitle Quality and Memory Trust**:
   - **Subtitle Clarity**: Ensuring subtitles are clear, fast, and stable.
   - **Memory Trust**: Reliable memory support for names, faces, and prior context without overconfidence or corruption.

2. **Low-Friction Interaction**:
   - **Interaction Constraints**: V1 should stick to frame-touch interaction only, avoiding voice commands initially.

3. **Privacy and Usability**:
   - **Privacy vs. Usefulness**: Striking the right balance between providing useful assistance and respecting privacy.
   - **Latency vs. Richness**: Balancing timely responses with efficient processing.

4. **Visual UX**:
   - **Subtitle Placement**: Ensuring subtitles are placed in a way that minimizes distraction while still being readable.
   - **Confidence Display**: Deciding on the best format for confidence (label, score, reason) to build trust without overloading the user.

5. **Battery and Thermal Management**:
   - **Always-On Sensing**: Managing always-on sensing with minimal impact on battery life.
   - **Thermal Constraints**: Ensuring hardware remains within thermal limits, especially in V1.

6. **Phone/Cloud Boundary**:
   - **Phone-First Processing**: Keeping heavy processing on the phone while allowing cloud assistance where justified.
