### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Subtitle placement rules that are stable and predictable help the wearer trust the subtitles more by ensuring they are always in a reliable position, which is crucial for real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement rules: one for high confidence states (like familiar faces), one for medium confidence states (like likely new people or names heard once), and one for low confidence states (like unlikely new people).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear, stable subtitle positions based on the confidence level of the speaker or name, the wearer can better focus on the conversation without constantly adjusting their gaze. This improves overall understanding and reduces cognitive load.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format ensures that the wearer can trust the subtitles and memory support more effectively. By clearly indicating certainty levels, the system maintains a balance between providing useful information and avoiding overconfidence which could lead to misunderstandings.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a consistent confidence label with a short reason for each confidence state. For example, use labels like "High" or "Low" along with reasons such as "Seen recently" or "Weak match".

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly showing the certainty of the information (e.g., "High: Seen recently"), users can better understand when to rely on the system's suggestions and when they might need to seek additional context or verification. This enhances trust in the system without overwhelming the user with too much detail.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

3. **Likely payoff**

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the phone-cloud boundary for live subtitle processing, we can ensure that critical real-time behavior is handled on-device to maintain low latency and stability. This enhances the trust and usability of subtitles in noisy environments, aligning with our core attractors of real-time subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache eviction policy for live subtitle processing that keeps the most recent and relevant transcriptions on-device while minimizing memory use. This ensures that critical subtitles are available immediately without relying on cloud latency.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing this local caching strategy will significantly reduce lag in subtitle delivery, making the system more reliable and responsive during real-time interactions. This improvement directly supports our core mission of providing clear and trust-preserving subtitles.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear distinction in visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support helps maintain the wearer's focus on essential information without overwhelming them with too much text. This balance enhances trust by ensuring that critical subtitled conversations are easily readable, while contextual prompts remain unobtrusive.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a subtitle-vs-one-line priority rule where subtitles always take precedence in visual prominence and placement over one-line support. This ensures that the most important information is always at the forefront without distracting from the main conversation.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing subtitles, the system can maintain clarity during critical interactions while keeping non-essential prompts subtle. This reduces cognitive load and enhances the overall user experience by ensuring that the wearer focuses on the essential information first.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a visual hierarchy rule where subtitles always have higher prominence and are placed more prominently than one-line support.
