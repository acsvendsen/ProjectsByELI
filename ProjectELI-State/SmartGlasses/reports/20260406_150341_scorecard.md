## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering V1 of SmartGlasses with a frame-touch-first interaction model, emphasizing near-real-time subtitles, face/name recall, and memory support. The main constraints are keeping the glasses lightweight, discreet, and socially acceptable while managing battery and thermal issues.

## Hardware Stack

- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The focus remains on a simple glasses hardware setup with minimal local processing, aligning with the current V1 architecture direction.
- next focus: Validate subtitle placement and visual hierarchy through user testing to ensure optimal usability without being distracting.
- confidence: 0.8
