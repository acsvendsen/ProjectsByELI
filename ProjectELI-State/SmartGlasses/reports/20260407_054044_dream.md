### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring subtitle placement is stable and unobtrusive enhances the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles. A fixed position minimizes distractions, allowing the wearer to focus on the conversation without visual disruptions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that positions subtitles below the speaker’s face for V1, ensuring they are always visible but do not obscure facial expressions or other important visual cues.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitles in a consistent and predictable location, the wearer can rely on them without being distracted by their movement or placement. This stability improves overall trust in the system and reduces cognitive load during interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a local memory threshold for face/name recall, we can ensure that critical information is always available to the wearer without overloading their phone's cache or draining its battery. This improves the reliability and speed of memory lookup, enhancing the overall trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local memory threshold where face/name memories are cached on the phone with a recency limit of 10 days, and automatically evict entries older than this period to keep the cache size manageable.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing a clear recency threshold for memory caching will reduce the risk of storing outdated or irrelevant information while ensuring that recently encountered faces/names remain readily accessible. This will lead to faster lookup times and more reliable face/name recall during conversations, thereby increasing trust in the system’s ability to support the wearer effectively.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Set a local cache eviction policy to evict face/name memories older than 10 days to maintain a manageable cache size for V1.
