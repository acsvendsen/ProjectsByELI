## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project aims to create a discreet personal assistant focused on real-time subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 will use frame-touch interaction, rely heavily on the phone for processing, and limit on-glasses compute to ensure social acceptability and battery efficiency.
