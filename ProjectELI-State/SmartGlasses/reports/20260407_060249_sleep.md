# Consolidation of Project Signals for SmartGlasses

## Strengthening Signals
1. **Subtitle Clarity**: Enhance subtitle placement to ensure they remain readable and stable during live conversations, maintaining trust.
2. **Memory Support**: Improve name and face recall by ensuring confident memory objects are accurately reinforced over time.

## Weakening Signals
1. **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Reduce the tendency to quietly slide into invasive behaviors that compromise privacy for perceived utility.
2. **Latency vs Richness**: Avoid adding richer but slower processing until it is justified, focusing on timely and relevant assistance.
3. **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**: Ensure visual clarity does not come at the cost of social acceptability.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
1. **Always-on Sensing vs Battery and Thermals**: Balance always-on functionality with battery conservation to avoid overheating issues.
2. **Rich Context vs Low-latency Response**: Prioritize quick responses over overly rich context that could slow down real-time interactions.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- Explore subtitle placement options that balance readability and social acceptability without becoming distracting.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Aspects
1. **Subtitle Quality**:
   - Ensure subtitles are clear, fast, and stable enough to maintain trust in live conversations.
2. **Memory Trust**:
   - Maintain the balance between remembering names, faces, and prior context while not creating false certainty.
3. **Low-friction Assistance**:
   - Provide one-line help without overwhelming the user or making the product appear like a cumbersome command interface.

## Core Deepening vs Feature Sprawl
- **Core Deepening**: Prioritize improvements in subtitle quality, memory trust, and visual UX over broad feature expansion until V1 is solid.
- **Feature Sprawl**: Avoid adding new command surfaces and hardware complexity that do not align with the current V1 interaction constraints.

## Reality Check Early Surface
- Continuously surface real-world limitations such as battery, latency, privacy, and usability to avoid wasting time on impossible or low-value directions.

## Open Questions for Future Exploration
- **Subtitle Placement**: Determine the most usable subtitle placement without becoming overly distracting.
- **Confidence Display**: Decide on the best format for confidence—label only, label + short reason, or score + label—to build trust.
- **Face/Name Memory Cache Policy**: Define how much face/name memory should be cached locally versus relying on cloud storage.

## Core Mission and Real-world Usefulness
- Ensure all work strengthens the core mission of reducing social and cognitive friction for the wearer in noisy environments and dense multi-person contexts.

## Evolving Project Direction
- Allow evolution that improves real-world usefulness, increases trust, clarity, and assistive value without deviating from the core mission.
