### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we ensure that subtitles are always clear and readable while minimizing distraction. This improves daily-use value by maintaining focus during conversations without overwhelming the wearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: 
   - A fixed position for high-confidence subtitles to remain in a constant, easily accessible location.
   - A dynamic mode that slightly shifts low-confidence subtitles out of direct sight but still within view upon request or when needed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that the wearer can always focus on the conversation without being distracted by less certain subtitles. It also reduces the cognitive load by making high-confidence information more prominent, thereby enhancing overall understanding and trust in the assistive behavior of the glasses.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display by using a numeric score format with labels and short reasons will enhance trust in the system's reliability and accuracy. This approach allows users to understand the level of certainty behind each piece of information provided, thereby reducing social awkwardness and increasing the overall credibility of the assistive behavior.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence object format that includes a numeric score between 0 and 1, a label indicating high, medium, or low confidence, and a short reason for why this level of confidence was assigned. This can be achieved by adding a new `confidenceFormat` enum in the relevant codebase.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a clear and concise confidence format, users will feel more informed and less unsure about the system's accuracy, which can lead to higher daily-use value without increasing complexity or social friction. This clarity also helps in building trust over time as the user sees consistent and reliable behavior from the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving memory cache policies ensures that the glasses provide more relevant and timely support to the wearer. By defining a robust local cache threshold, the system can store frequently encountered names and faces, enhancing the recall accuracy and reducing latency during live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache eviction policy based on recency and frequency of encounters with people's names and faces. This means that recently seen or frequently recalled individuals are kept in memory for a longer duration, while less relevant information is evicted to make space for new data.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this small change, the system can maintain a more accurate and up-to-date memory cache, reducing the need for frequent cloud lookups and improving the reliability of real-time subtitle assistance. This enhances user trust in the product's ability to provide relevant and timely information during conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the recency rule to keep names and faces seen within the last 24 hours active in local memory.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By clearly defining what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud for live subtitle processing, we can ensure real-time subtitle clarity is maintained without unnecessary battery drain or latency issues. This focus helps balance the need for immediate assistance with practical constraints.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear policy where face and name recall lookup primarily happens on the phone but falls back to cloud only for rare, heavy-processing tasks like memory archive syncs or extensive background processing.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This will ensure that subtitle clarity remains high in real-time interactions, reducing the risk of breaking trust due to delayed processing. It also optimizes battery use and privacy by keeping sensitive memory operations local where possible.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one phone/cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that subtitles remain stable and clear during live conversations is crucial for maintaining trust and understanding. A well-defined subtitle placement rule can prevent flickering or jumping text, which would otherwise distract the wearer and reduce the quality of assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single subtitle placement rule that prioritizes stability over dynamic adjustment based on visual context. This rule should ensure that subtitles remain in a fixed position on the screen until explicitly updated, reducing jittery or unpredictable behavior.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a stable subtitle placement rule, we can reduce visual distractions and improve overall trust in the system’s real-time subtitle capabilities. This will enhance the wearer's ability to follow conversations more effectively without being pulled away by inconsistent text movement.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
