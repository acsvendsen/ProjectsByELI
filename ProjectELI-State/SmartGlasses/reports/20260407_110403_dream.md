### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the wearer's ability to focus on the conversation while reducing distraction. Stable, predictable subtitle placement ensures that subtitles are always in a visible and unobtrusive position, which is crucial for maintaining trust and ensuring clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence scenarios where the system is certain about who is speaking, and another for low-confidence scenarios where additional context or reinforcement might be needed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By establishing clear placement rules based on confidence levels, we can reduce visual clutter during conversations, making it easier for the wearer to focus on understanding the spoken words. This will enhance the overall user experience and trust in the system's ability to provide accurate subtitles.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can quickly understand the reliability of the subtitle information provided by the system. A clear and consistent confidence indicator will reduce cognitive load and enhance trust in the subtitles, making them more useful in noisy or hearing-challenged environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple numeric score + label + short reason format for confidence objects. For example, a score of 0.95 with "High" and "Seen recently" would indicate high certainty based on recent interaction history.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly displaying the confidence level in this format, the system can help the wearer better understand when to trust the subtitles more or less, reducing frustration and enhancing overall reliability of real-time communication support.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving memory cache thresholds ensures that likely new-person names are quickly remembered with confidence objects and reinforced over time, thereby enhancing the wearer's trust in face/name recall capabilities while maintaining low-friction interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit for face and name memories on the phone, set to 50 entries per person, with a refresh threshold based on recent interaction frequency.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting clear limits and thresholds, the system will efficiently manage memory resources, ensuring that likely new-person names are remembered accurately and consistently without overwhelming the local cache, thereby improving overall trust in face/name recall.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a local cache size limit for face and name memories on the phone at 50 entries per person.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what runs locally on the phone versus what falls back to the cloud, we can ensure that critical real-time subtitle processing happens locally, maintaining low latency and stability. This focus ensures that the wearer's experience remains smooth and reliable without introducing delays or uncertainty.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific task boundary for live subtitle processing: all live subtitle text should be processed on the phone in near-real time before being transmitted to the glasses.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This decision will ensure that subtitles remain fast and stable, providing reliable support during live interactions without introducing delays or jittery behavior. It also aligns with the core attractors of real-time clarity and low-friction assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving the visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support ensures that the wearer can focus on critical information without being overwhelmed by less important details, thereby enhancing overall clarity and reducing cognitive load.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a clear precedence rule where subtitles have priority over one-line prompts in fast-moving conversations, with one-line prompts appearing only when there is sufficient free visual space or low confidence in the current subtitle.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By prioritizing subtitles and reducing distractions from one-line prompts, wearers will be more likely to trust the system during critical interactions, leading to a better user experience and increased daily-use value.

4. **Immediate next probe**
