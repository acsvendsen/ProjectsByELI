### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring clear, stable subtitle placement helps maintain trust in the system during live conversations. By defining a fixed position for subtitles that remains unobtrusive yet visible, the wearer can rely on real-time text without distraction or confusion.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for confident matches (e.g., names and faces recognized with high certainty) and another for less certain matches (e.g., new people or weaker audio contexts). For confident matches, place subtitles at the bottom of the field-of-view; for less certain matches, use a semi-transparent overlay in the peripheral vision.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will reduce subtitle-related cognitive load by providing clear visual cues for different levels of confidence. It ensures that the wearer can focus on the conversation while still having crucial information when needed, enhancing overall trust and usability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define two subtitle placement rules: one for confident matches placing subtitles at the bottom of the field-of-view, and another for less certain matches using a semi-transparent overlay in the peripheral vision.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the system's memory and support without overloading them with unnecessary details or creating uncertainty. Clear, concise, and reliable confidence indicators will enhance the overall trust in the system’s ability to recall names, faces, and prior context accurately.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple yet effective confidence format that includes both a numeric score and a label, along with a short reason for the confidence level. For example, "Score 0.95 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'".

3. **Likely payoff**

   By adopting this format, users will quickly understand the system’s certainty in its memory recall without feeling overwhelmed by too much information. This clarity can lead to more confident interactions and a stronger overall trust in the system's capabilities.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
