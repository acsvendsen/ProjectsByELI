### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules, we can ensure that subtitles remain stable and unobtrusive while still being easily readable. This improves overall user trust and understanding during live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule for V1 that places subtitles below the speaker’s face with a slight offset to avoid occluding facial features but ensuring they are clearly visible in various lighting conditions and head movements.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This will enhance the wearer's ability to follow conversations in real-time without distractions, thereby increasing trust and utility of the product during noisy or crowded environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the local memory threshold ensures that frequently encountered faces and names are stored with higher confidence, reducing the need for cloud processing while maintaining high trust in memory recall. This strengthens the core mission by ensuring that the system remains reliable and useful even when connectivity is limited or absent.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit of 10 entities (faces/names) with a priority based on recency, where more recent encounters are stored longer to reinforce memory over time.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By limiting the local cache to 10 entities and prioritizing recency, we ensure that the system remains efficient in terms of battery usage while still providing high-confidence memory recall for frequently encountered people. This reduces cloud dependency, enhances privacy by minimizing data storage, and improves overall trust through consistent and reliable face/name recognition.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one local-cache size limit of 10 entities with a priority based on recency for face/name memory.

3. **Likely payoff**

4. **Immediate next probe**

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear visual hierarchy rule between subtitles and one-line prompts, we can ensure that the wearer remains focused on essential information without feeling overwhelmed by too many display elements at once.    Define a subtitle-vs-one-line priority rule where one-line prompts are shown only when they provide critical context, such as introducing new people or key information that cannot be conveyed through subtitles alone. 3. **Likely payoff**

This balance improves trust in the system as it remains unobtrusive while still providing necessary support when needed, aligning with the core attractors of real-time subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.
