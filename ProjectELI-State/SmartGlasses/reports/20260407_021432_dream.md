### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain consistent visual clarity and readability, which is crucial for real-time subtitle assistance in noisy environments. Stable placement ensures that subtitles do not flicker or move erratically, reducing cognitive load on the wearer and maintaining their trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a set of fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: 
   - High confidence (e.g., 0.8+): Fixed at the bottom center.
   - Medium confidence (e.g., 0.5-0.8): Slightly above, centered but allowing for some movement.
   - Low confidence (e.g., <0.5): Dynamic but with a fallback to fixed placement if too dynamic.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining these stable rules, the system can reduce visual distractions and ensure that subtitles remain readable even when there is uncertainty in the recognition. This will enhance the overall user experience and trust in the subtitle assistance provided by the glasses.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving confidence display can enhance trust by clearly indicating the system's certainty level in its responses. A well-designed confidence format helps users understand when they should rely more heavily on their own judgment and when the system is providing high-confidence information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple, yet informative confidence format that includes both a numeric score and a label with a short reason. For example, use "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'".

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly indicating the level of certainty in the system's responses, users will have a better understanding of when to trust the displayed information. This can lead to more effective and less stressful interactions, reducing cognitive load for the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement for live subtitles enhances the wearer's trust in the system by ensuring that subtitles are always clear and stable enough to be read comfortably during conversations. This consistency builds confidence and ensures that the wearer can rely on the system even in noisy or challenging environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a set of three subtitle placement rules for different confidence levels: 
   - High-confidence subtitles placed directly under the speaker's face.
   - Medium-confidence subtitles slightly above the speaker’s head, with a slight margin to avoid cluttering the view.
   - Low-confidence subtitles displayed in a small box near the bottom of the frame, not obstructing the main view.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By implementing these rules, the system will provide clear and unobtrusive subtitle placement that enhances readability without causing visual distraction. This improvement will increase trust in the system during live conversations, making it more useful for the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line prompts, we ensure that the wearer can easily distinguish which information is most relevant at any given moment. This helps in maintaining focus during conversations without overwhelming the user with too much text, thereby reducing cognitive load and social friction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for confidence scores above 0.75 where subtitles take precedence over one-line prompts, and another for lower confidence scores where one-line prompts are more visible to prevent missed context clues.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This visual hierarchy will improve the wearer's ability to follow the conversation by ensuring that critical information is easily visible while less important details do not distract from understanding. It will also enhance the system’s usability, making it more intuitive and user-friendly.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
