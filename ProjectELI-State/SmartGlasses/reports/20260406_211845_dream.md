### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the clarity and stability of subtitles, ensuring they are always in a fixed, readable position that minimizes distraction while maximizing comprehension during live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence faces/names (e.g., "Always above the face") and another for lower confidence entities (e.g., "Floating 50% of time, remaining static 50%").

3. **Likely payoff**
   This change will help in reducing visual distraction during conversations by keeping important information always visible while maintaining a clean interface that does not overwhelm the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   By providing a clear confidence display, the wearer can better understand when to trust the subtitles more or be cautious. This will enhance the overall user experience by reducing misunderstandings and increasing trust in the system's accuracy.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
