### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules improves the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles, enhancing their ability to follow conversations without distraction. This stability is crucial for maintaining clarity and avoiding cognitive overload.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that centers subtitles at the bottom of the frame, ensuring they do not move or flicker unless necessary. This minimizes distractions and keeps important information always visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this stable subtitle placement rule, the wearer will experience more consistent and reliable subtitle visibility, reducing cognitive load during conversations and enhancing overall understanding. This consistency builds trust in the system's reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format to use a clear, concise label with a short reason for the match, we can improve the wearer's trust in the system's reliability and accuracy. This approach ensures that the confidence level is not only conveyed numerically but also contextually, helping the wearer understand when they can rely on the displayed information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one specific confidence format candidate for V1: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"; score 0.42 + "Low" + "Weak match"; score 0.63 + "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction"`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This clear and concise confidence format will enhance the wearer's trust in the system by providing a quick, easily understandable indication of how reliable the displayed information is. It aligns with the project’s core mission to provide honest support without faking certainty or creating corrupted recall.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a local cache threshold for name recall, we ensure that frequently encountered faces and names are quickly accessible on-device, reducing latency and improving trust in real-time interactions. This directly supports the `memory_trust` attractor by ensuring accurate and timely memory assistance without overloading the system with heavy cloud requests.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache threshold for name recall that is set to store frequently encountered names for fast lookup on-device. This threshold should be tuned to balance between minimizing latency and avoiding excessive data storage.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing this local cache will significantly reduce the delay in recalling common faces and names, enhancing the wearer's trust in the system during live conversations. It will also free up cloud resources for more complex tasks without compromising on real-time responsiveness.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Set a local cache size limit of 10 frequently encountered people for fast name recall on V1 SmartGlasses.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary between what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud for live subtitle processing, we ensure that subtitles remain fast and reliable. This reduces the risk of latency-induced issues during real-time conversations, which is critical for maintaining trust and usability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one task-specific fallback policy for live subtitle processing: if the phone's local processing capacity drops (due to battery or thermal constraints), automatically fall back to cloud processing only for high-priority contexts like real-time conversation subtitles, while keeping lower-priority text rendering on-device.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This refined boundary will ensure that critical subtitles remain crisp and up-to-date during live interactions, enhancing the wearer's understanding and trust in the system. It also prevents unnecessary cloud traffic for less urgent text, conserving resources and improving overall efficiency.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support ensures that the wearer can focus on essential information without being overwhelmed by too much data. This balance enhances the wearer's ability to follow conversations in real-time, making the product more useful and less distracting.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single rule for subtitle-vs-one-line precedence based on confidence level: subtitles should be prioritized over one-liners when confidence is high (e.g., >0.7), and one-liners can coexist with subtitles at lower confidence levels without obscuring them.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly defining the visual hierarchy, V1 will maintain a balance between providing enough contextual support and not overwhelming the wearer. This will lead to better user satisfaction and trust in the system's reliability during real-world interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
