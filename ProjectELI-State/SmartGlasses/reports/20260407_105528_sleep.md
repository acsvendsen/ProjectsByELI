# Consolidated Signals

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Clarity**: Focus on maintaining high subtitle clarity to ensure real-time readability and trust.
- **Memory Trust**: Prioritize reliable memory support for names, faces, and context without overconfidence or false assurance.
- **Low-Friction Assistance**: Ensure one-line prompts are helpful without adding unnecessary complexity or social awkwardness.

## Weakening Signals
- **Always-On Sensing**: Reduce the emphasis on always-on sensing to minimize battery drain and thermal issues.
- **Rich Context vs. Low-Latency Response**: Shift focus away from rich context if it significantly increases latency, affecting real-time trust.

## Contradictions that Must Stay Alive
- **Privacy vs. Usefulness**: Balance assistive behavior with privacy concerns; avoid invasive data collection.
- **Discreet UX vs. Visual Clarity**: Maintain a balance between social acceptability and readability in fast-moving situations.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Subtitle Placement & Visual Hierarchy**: Re-evaluate subtitle placement to ensure it remains unobtrusive yet readable, especially during motion.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Areas
1. **Subtitle Quality**: Ensure subtitles are clear, stable, and trustworthy in live conversations.
2. **Memory Trustworthiness**: Avoid overconfidence in memory recall; maintain honest support without pretending certainty.
3. **Low-Friction UI**: Deliver assistance that is helpful but minimally intrusive.

## Open Questions and Tensions
- **Subtitle Placement & Visual Hierarchy**: What subtitle placement best balances readability with minimal distraction?
- **Confidence Display**: How should confidence be represented (numeric score, label, short reason)?
- **Phone/Cloud Boundary**: Should certain interactions work offline or require cloud support?

### Summary

1. **Subtitle Clarity and Memory Trust** remain critical core attractors.
2. **Always-on Sensing** is being downweighted due to real-world constraints.
3. **Discreet UX vs. Visual Clarity** tension remains but will be managed carefully.
4. **Subtitle Placement & Visual Hierarchy**, **Confidence Display**, and **Phone/Cloud Boundary** are key open questions that need further exploration.

By focusing on these areas, the project can continue to deepen its core mission while respecting current constraints and maintaining balance across critical tensions.
