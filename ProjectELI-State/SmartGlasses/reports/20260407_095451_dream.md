### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules for different confidence states enhances the wearer's trust in the system by providing consistent and reliable subtitles, which are crucial for real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple two-tier subtitle placement rule: 
   - For high-confidence matches (e.g., seen recently), place subtitles directly above the speaker.
   - For lower-confidence matches or new faces, use a semi-transparent overlay in a less prominent position to avoid distraction but still be visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will reduce visual clutter and improve subtitle readability, thereby enhancing user trust and the overall assistive value of the system without overwhelming the wearer with too much information at once.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
