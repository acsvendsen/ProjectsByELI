### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

4. **Immediate next probe**

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving the stability and reliability of face/name recall ensures that the wearer can trust their memories over time, reducing social friction and increasing confidence in interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a local cache eviction policy based on recency and frequency to ensure frequently encountered names are kept in memory longer than infrequently seen ones.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By prioritizing more recent and frequent encounters, the system can maintain a smaller but highly relevant cache of names, reducing the risk of outdated or irrelevant memories being recalled and improving overall trust in the memory system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
