## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The current V1 is constrained to frame-touch interactions and primarily relies on the phone for heavy processing while keeping hardware simple to manage battery and thermal constraints.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The glasses remain simple with focus on frame-touch interactions. The current design respects the constraints of battery and thermal limits by keeping heavy processing off-glasses.
- next focus: Optimize subtitle generation algorithms for real-time performance while minimizing energy usage.
