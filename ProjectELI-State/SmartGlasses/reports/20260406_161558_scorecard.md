## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 is focused on delivering discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, and conversation memory. The glasses are designed to be lightweight, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute. Progress is being made in defining subtitle placement, confidence display, memory caching policies, phone/cloud boundaries, and visual hierarchy, all while respecting hardware constraints.
