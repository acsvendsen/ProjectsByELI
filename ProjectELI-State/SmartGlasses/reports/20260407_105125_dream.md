### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules to ensure they remain stable and unobtrusive, we can enhance the wearer's trust in the subtitles. Stable placement reduces visual distractions during live conversations, making the subtitles more reliable for the wearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed position rule that places subtitles at the bottom center of the screen with minimal vertical movement, ensuring they do not flicker or shift unexpectedly.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A consistent and stable subtitle placement will reduce cognitive load by minimizing the need for the wearer to constantly adjust their gaze between text and conversation participants. This stability can lead to higher trust in the system's assistance and improved overall user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear and consistent confidence display format helps maintain trust in the system's accuracy and reliability. A well-designed confidence object can help the wearer understand when to rely more heavily on the subtitles, thus improving overall understanding without creating false certainty.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence format that combines a numeric score with a label and a short reason. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"; score 0.42 + "Low" + "Weak match"; score 0.63 + "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction".

3. **Likely payoff**

   By using a clear and concise confidence format, the system can better manage expectations and reduce cognitive load on the wearer. This will help maintain trust and ensure that the subtitles are seen as reliable assistance rather than guesses or incorrect information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the local memory threshold for face/name recall helps ensure that relevant context is available even when internet connectivity is limited or unavailable, thereby maintaining trust and usability without overloading the device.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit for person-memory objects based on recent encounters to prioritize frequently seen individuals while still allowing new people to be remembered automatically by default.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will ensure that the system can maintain reliable memory of key individuals even in low-signal environments, reducing the risk of false negatives and maintaining trust with the user.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what runs locally on the phone versus what falls back to the cloud, we can ensure that V1 remains focused on providing real-time, reliable subtitle assistance and memory support without overloading the hardware or compromising privacy.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define explicit rules for when live subtitle processing should be done on-device (phone) versus when it should fall back to the cloud due to resource constraints.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This decision will allow us to maintain a consistent and reliable user experience by ensuring that subtitles are always available in real-time, even during periods of high demand or limited battery life. It also helps manage privacy concerns by keeping sensitive audio processing off-device where possible.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support, we ensure that the wearer can focus on essential information without cognitive overload or distraction. This improves both subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance by making critical information more prominent while reducing unnecessary clutter.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a rule where subtitles are always displayed prominently, while one-line support is shown minimally unless explicitly triggered by the wearer through frame touches.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing subtitle visibility over one-line prompts, we enhance the wearer's ability to follow live conversations without distraction. This improves trust and understanding in noisy environments, aligning with the core attractors of real-time subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a visual hierarchy rule where subtitles are always displayed prominently, while one-line support is shown minimally unless explicitly triggered by the wearer through frame touches.
