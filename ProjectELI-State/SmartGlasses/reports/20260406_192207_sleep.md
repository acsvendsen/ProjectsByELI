# Consolidation of SmartGlasses V1 Core Signals

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Clarity** (high strength): Ensure subtitles are clear, fast, and stable to maintain trust in live conversation.
- **Trustworthy Memory Support** (high importance): Implement name and face memory that avoids overconfidence or incorrect recall.
- **Low-Friction Assistance** (medium importance): Provide concise one-line support without adding unnecessary command overhead.

## Weakening Signals
- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Reduce the inclination to prioritize privacy concerns over useful functionality, ensuring both are balanced but not at the expense of effectiveness.
- **Always-On Sensing vs Battery and Thermals**: Minimize always-on sensing that drains battery or generates excessive heat, focusing on real-time performance.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**: Strive for a balance where display behavior remains socially acceptable while still providing clear and readable subtitles.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy**: Revisit the current subtitle placement to ensure it is both readable and non-distracting, especially in dynamic environments.
- **Confidence Display Format**: Evaluate different confidence formats (label only, label + short reason, score + label) for their ability to build trust without overwhelming the wearer.
