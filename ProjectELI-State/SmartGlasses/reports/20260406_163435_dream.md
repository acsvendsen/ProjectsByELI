### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules enhances the wearer's understanding by making critical information more predictable and less distracting. This consistency helps the wearer focus on conversations without being disoriented by constantly shifting text.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: fixed position for high-confidence matches, flexible positioning for medium-confidence matches, and hidden or low-visibility for low-confidence matches.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining these rules, the system can provide a more consistent and less intrusive experience, reducing cognitive load and improving the wearer's ability to follow conversations accurately.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear confidence format that includes both a label and a short reason, we can build trust through transparent signaling of the system's certainty level. This helps the wearer understand when they should rely more on their own judgment or fully trust the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a new confidence object format: score 0.85 + "High" + "Seen in recent conversations".

3. **Likely payoff**

   This format will help users quickly assess the reliability of the subtitles and memory, enhancing their trust in the system without overwhelming them with too much information. It also aligns well with the project's mission to be assistive but not deceptive.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving the phone-cloud boundary ensures that critical processing happens locally when necessary, reducing latency and preserving battery life. This balance is crucial for maintaining the real-time nature of subtitles, which are essential for the wearer's understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a specific phone-cloud fallback rule for live subtitle processing. For example, if the phone loses connection, fall back to cached or previously synced data on the glasses instead of pausing or degrading performance.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By ensuring that subtitles can continue uninterrupted even with intermittent connectivity, the system will maintain high trust and reliability, which are critical for real-time assistance in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.
