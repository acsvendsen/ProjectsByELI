### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a stable subtitle placement rule based on confidence levels, we ensure that subtitles remain consistently readable and unobtrusive while still providing necessary clarity in different contexts. This improves real-time subtitle quality and trust, aligning with the core attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity."

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule where subtitles appear at the bottom center of the frame for low confidence states (e.g., when faces or names are uncertain) and slightly shifted to one side for high confidence states (e.g., well-known speakers or familiar environments). This small change ensures that the subtitles remain stable and unobtrusive while still being easily readable.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a stable subtitle placement rule, we reduce visual distraction and improve the wearer's ability to focus on the conversation. This enhances the overall user experience and aligns with the core mission of reducing social and cognitive friction during interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that subtitles are stable, clear, and fast enough to be trusted in live conversations helps the wearer stay informed without feeling overwhelmed or distracted.
