### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules helps ensure that subtitles remain clear, readable, and unobtrusive, which directly supports the primary attractor of real-time subtitle clarity. By defining specific placement modes or fallback strategies based on confidence levels, we can enhance the wearer's ability to trust the subtitles in live conversations without them becoming distracting.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple yet effective subtitle placement rule that prioritizes stable and readable placement over reactive behavior. For example, setting a fixed position for high-confidence subtitles (e.g., above the speaker’s face) and allowing lower-confidence subtitles to fade out after a few seconds if no visual correction is needed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By establishing consistent and unobtrusive subtitle placement, the system will maintain higher trust levels during conversations. This stability reduces cognitive load on the wearer, making it easier for them to focus on understanding the conversation without being distracted by fluctuating subtitle positions. The payoff includes improved user experience and sustained confidence in the assistive nature of the glasses.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
