   By refining the confidence display format, we can ensure that the system's certainty about a name or face is clearly communicated to the wearer, thereby enhancing trust and reliability. 2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple numeric score alongside a label and short reason for each confidence object. This format provides a clear and concise way to convey the system's level of confidence.

   A well-defined confidence display will help users understand when they can rely on the system's information, especially in critical situations where misrecognition could be costly. It also builds trust by showing that the system is not overconfident and acknowledges its limitations, which is crucial for maintaining user acceptance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
