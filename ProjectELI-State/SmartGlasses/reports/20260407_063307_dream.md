### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules will enhance the stability and clarity of subtitles, which is crucial for the wearer's trust in the system. By ensuring subtitles remain fixed in a prominent but unobtrusive position, the system can provide consistent and reliable support during conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single fixed subtitle placement rule that positions subtitles at the bottom center of the display area, ensuring they do not move or shift during speech, even as the wearer's gaze changes.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this fixed subtitle placement rule, the system will provide more predictable and reliable subtitle behavior. This stability will enhance the user's trust in the system, making it a more valuable tool for understanding conversations in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a single fixed subtitle placement rule that positions subtitles at the bottom center of the display area.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format will ensure that the wearer can trust the system's assistance more confidently, especially during live conversations. A well-designed confidence label and short reason will help reduce uncertainty and enhance the perceived reliability of the system, aligning with the core attractors of real-time subtitle clarity and memory support.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple yet effective confidence format for V1: use a numeric score (0-1), a label (Low, Medium, High), and a short reason that provides context on why the system is showing a particular level of confidence.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear, concise, and contextually relevant confidence information, the wearer will be better informed about the reliability of the subtitles and memory support. This clarity can lead to increased trust in the system, which is crucial for real-time interactions where immediate understanding is essential.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By setting a clear threshold for local memory caching, we ensure that the system only retains information relevant to the wearer's current context without overwhelming their cognitive load or violating privacy concerns.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a recency-based eviction policy where face and name memories are cached locally but automatically expire after a set period of inactivity (e.g., 30 minutes).

3. **Likely payoff**
   - This approach maintains the wearer's trust by ensuring that memory content is relevant and up-to-date, reducing the risk of outdated or unnecessary information cluttering their experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a recency threshold for local caching of face and name memories, setting it to expire after 30 minutes of inactivity.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the local caching and fallback rules for memory recall enhances the trustworthiness and reliability of name and face recognition. By keeping frequently recalled entities locally, we reduce latency and improve the speed with which names are displayed during live conversations. This ensures that the system remains responsive even under high thermal or battery constraints.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache eviction policy that retains recently seen and highly confident names for a certain duration (e.g., 30 minutes) on the phone, with a fallback to cloud storage only if there is no recent interaction within this timeframe.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping commonly recalled names locally, we can reduce latency during live conversations, ensuring that names appear faster and more reliably. This improves the overall trust in the system’s memory recall capabilities and reduces the need for frequent cloud requests, thereby saving battery life and maintaining a smoother user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By carefully defining the visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support, we ensure that the wearer remains focused on the most critical information while minimizing distractions. This enhances the overall clarity and trustworthiness of the system, aligning with our primary attractors for subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear visual priority rule where subtitles take precedence over one-line prompts in areas of high visual activity or when confidence is high. This ensures that the most relevant information remains prominent without overwhelming the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a clear subtitle vs. one-line priority, we can reduce visual clutter and ensure that the wearer stays focused on the essential context. This improves the overall user experience by maintaining clarity and reducing cognitive load, which is critical for real-time communication support.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
