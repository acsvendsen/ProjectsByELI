### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence states, we ensure that subtitles remain stable and non-distracting while still providing timely assistance. This helps in maintaining trust by not overloading the wearer with too many visual elements, especially during high-confidence interactions where immediate clarity is most needed.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Implementing a simple rule set that places subtitles at the bottom of the screen for high-confidence interactions and slightly above or below for medium and low confidence levels can unlock this improvement. This fixed placement ensures consistent visual behavior, reducing cognitive load and distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   The payoff includes reduced eye strain from constantly shifting gaze, enhanced trust in the system by providing clear, stable subtitles, and improved overall user experience during live interactions. Consistent placement also aligns with the core tension of balancing discreet UX with sufficient visual clarity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

   By standardizing the confidence display format, V1 can achieve more reliable and trustworthy interactions, enhancing the wearer's trust in the system's memory support and subtitle accuracy. This clarity will reduce cognitive load by making it easier for users to understand when they should pay attention or confirm information manually.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a specific threshold for local memory retention, we can ensure that the glasses prioritize storing information that is most relevant and useful to the wearer. This helps in maintaining a balance between rich memory support and fast lookup, thereby enhancing the overall trustworthiness and usability of the product.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit for person-memory objects based on recency and frequency of interaction. For example, retain top 5 most frequently seen people with high-confidence memories, while lower confidence or less frequent interactions are stored locally but with reduced priority.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that the glasses can provide quick access to names and faces that are highly relevant to the current context, reducing latency and improving the wearer's trust in the system. It also helps in managing memory storage efficiently without overwhelming the local cache, thus maintaining a good balance between rich memory support and fast retrieval.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a local cache size limit of 5 for person-memory objects based on recency and frequency of interaction.
