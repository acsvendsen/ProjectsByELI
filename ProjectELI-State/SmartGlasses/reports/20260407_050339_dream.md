### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a stable subtitle placement rule that prioritizes readability over reactivity, we can ensure subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive during live conversations. This stability helps in maintaining the wearer's trust and focus on the conversation.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed position for subtitles at the bottom center of the frame, with a slight offset to the side if needed, ensuring they do not move frequently or block the wearer’s view.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A stable subtitle placement will reduce eye movement and cognitive load, making the subtitles more reliable and less distracting during conversations. This can significantly enhance the user's trust in the system and improve overall understanding of the spoken content.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format will enhance the trustworthiness of the system by ensuring that the wearer can easily understand the certainty level behind the displayed subtitles and memory recall. This clarity in confidence signals will help the wearer better integrate the assistant's output into their understanding, reducing cognitive load and improving overall interaction quality.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a new confidence format for V1 that combines a numeric score with a label and a short reason. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently";` This simple but informative format will provide the wearer with immediate context about the reliability of the displayed information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this confidence format, the system can better manage expectations and avoid creating false certainty or unnecessary doubt. The improved transparency will lead to a more natural and trusting interaction experience, which is crucial for the core mission of helping the wearer understand and respond better in real-world interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving memory cache policies will ensure that the system retains relevant information more effectively, reducing the likelihood of memory gaps during conversations and enhancing overall confidence in real-time assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache eviction policy based on recency to keep the most recently used names and contexts in memory while expelling less recent data.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing recency, the system will be better equipped to provide accurate and relevant support during conversations, reducing the need for cloud fallbacks and improving overall trust in the wearer's memory assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the phone/cloud boundary for live subtitle processing ensures that critical real-time information is handled on the phone, reducing latency and preserving battery life. This enhances the wearer's trust in the system by providing near-real-time subtitles without overloading the glasses or cloud infrastructure.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local subtitle processing rule where all speech-to-text transcription happens on the phone for live conversations. This involves setting up a robust offline transcriber that can handle real-time audio input and produce stable, readable subtitles quickly.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing on the phone, the system reduces latency, which is crucial for maintaining trust and ensuring that subtitles are displayed in near-real time. This improves the overall user experience and aligns with the core mission of providing clear, reliable assistance in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.
