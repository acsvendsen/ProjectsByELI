# Consolidation

## Strengthening Signals
1. **Subtitle Clarity (high)**
   - The system must maintain clear, fast, and stable subtitles to ensure the wearer can trust them in live conversations.

2. **Memory Trust (high)**
   - Names, faces, and prior context should be remembered accurately without creating a false sense of certainty or corrupted recall.

3. **Low-Friction Assistance (medium)**
   - One-line prompts should enhance understanding without adding unnecessary command overhead or social awkwardness.

## Weakening Signals
1. **Always-On Sensing vs Battery and Thermals (high pressure)**
   - The system must balance always-on sensing with battery constraints to avoid overheating and drain.

2. **Privacy vs Usefulness (high pressure)**
   - Helpful assistive behavior should not lead to invasive capture or retention of private data.

3. **Latency vs Richness (high pressure)**
   - Timely help is crucial, but the system must avoid overloading with richer processing that could introduce latency issues.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
1. **Privacy vs Usefulness (high pressure)**
   - The tension between providing useful assistance and maintaining privacy must be managed carefully to ensure the product remains both helpful and respectful of user data.

2. **Latency vs Richness (high pressure)**
   - The system should prioritize low-latency responses over richer, but slower processing that could degrade real-time performance.
