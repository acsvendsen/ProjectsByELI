### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By establishing clear subtitle placement rules based on confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles remain readable and stable, enhancing trust and usability without becoming distracting. This helps in maintaining a clean visual hierarchy that supports real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches where subtitles should be displayed prominently at the top of the screen, and another for lower-confidence matches where subtitles can be placed slightly lower to avoid distraction but remain visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This setup will improve overall readability and trust in live conversation by ensuring that critical information is clearly presented without overwhelming the wearer with too much text. It also helps in managing visual clutter, making the SmartGlasses more discreet yet effective.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

   Improving the confidence display format will enhance the trustworthiness of both subtitles and memory recall, ensuring that the wearer can distinguish between high-confidence and low-confidence information. This will reinforce the wearer's belief in the system's reliability without overwhelming them with too much detail.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a new confidence format for V1: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"; score 0.63 + "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction"`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly showing the certainty level of information, the wearer will be able to better trust the system's suggestions and understand when they are high or low confidence. This will reduce cognitive load by providing a quick glance at the reliability of each piece of information, leading to improved user experience and trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By clearly defining which tasks fall back to the cloud versus staying local on the phone, we can optimize for both real-time subtitle quality and memory trust while respecting battery and thermal constraints. This ensures that critical subtitle processing happens fast and locally, maintaining trust in live conversation.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear threshold for transcript cleanup where low-latency requirements exceed the local processing capabilities of the phone. For tasks like live subtitle processing, keep everything on the phone to ensure clarity and speed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing local, we can achieve smoother, more reliable real-time subtitles that the wearer can trust in noisy environments. This reduces battery drain from continuous cloud requests and ensures that critical memory support remains fast and accurate without unnecessary cloud round trips.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.
