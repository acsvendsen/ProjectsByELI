### Confidence Display
- **Confidence Display**: Implementing confidence as a label + short reason (e.g., "likely", "uncertain") could help users understand the reliability of the subtitle and face/name recall. This approach balances clarity with minimal visual clutter.

  - **Feasibility Now**: The `TranscriptLab` app can be used to prototype different confidence displays, ensuring that the labels are clear and concise enough for the wearer to quickly understand their meaning. This is feasible now given the existing test setup.

### Memory/Cache Policy
- **Memory Cache Policy**: Implementing a caching policy on the phone where low-confidence face/name memories are stored temporarily before being reinforced over time can help manage battery and thermal constraints while maintaining user trust.

  - **Feasibility Now**: The `TranscriptLab` app can be used to simulate memory storage and retrieval, ensuring that the implementation respects battery limits. This is feasible now but may require further optimization for real-world use.
