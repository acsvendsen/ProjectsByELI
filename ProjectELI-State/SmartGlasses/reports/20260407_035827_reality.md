### Recommendations:
1. **Subtitle Clarity**: Continue refining subtitle placement and readability while ensuring they do not become distracting.
2. **Memory Support**: Focus on clear and consistent memory recall behavior without overloading the user with too much information.
3. **Interaction Design**: Ensure that all interactions remain low-friction and intuitive, sticking to frame touches for now.
4. **Privacy Considerations**: Conduct user research to understand privacy concerns and establish clear guidelines.
5. **Latency Management**: Ensure that any cloud interactions are fast enough to maintain trust and usability.

By maintaining a focus on these core areas and continually testing with users, we can ensure the SmartGlasses project stays aligned with its mission while addressing real-world constraints.
