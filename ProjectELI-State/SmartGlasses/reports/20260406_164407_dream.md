### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always readable but do not become distracting. This improves user trust by maintaining a clear visual hierarchy, which is crucial for real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence speech (always visible), one for medium-confidence speech (conditional visibility based on context and ambient noise level), and one for low-confidence speech (visible only under certain conditions).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear and unambiguous subtitle placement, the system can maintain a balance between clarity and non-distracting behavior. This enhances user trust as they are always able to read subtitles when needed without visual clutter.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
