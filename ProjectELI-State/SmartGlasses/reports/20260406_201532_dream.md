### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always in a clear and unobtrusive position, thereby enhancing the wearer's ability to focus on the conversation while maintaining trust through consistent and stable placement.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches (e.g., names or faces seen multiple times) and another for low-confidence matches (e.g., first-time encounters). For each rule, specify a fixed position relative to the speaker's face that minimizes distraction but ensures readability.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will lead to more reliable and less distracting subtitle placement, improving the wearer’s trust in the system by reducing visual clutter during high-confidence matches while still providing critical support for low-confidence ones. It will also set a clear, stable behavior pattern that reduces cognitive load and enhances overall user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear local cache eviction policy for face/name memory, we ensure that the system can efficiently manage its memory footprint while maintaining robust recall capabilities. This helps in preserving battery and thermal resources without compromising on the quality of live subtitle assistance or memory support.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   The small change is to set a specific recency threshold for local caching of names and faces, ensuring that only recently encountered entities are kept in cache while older entries are evicted based on age.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing this policy will lead to more efficient memory usage, reducing the risk of overheating or battery drain from unnecessary data storage. It also ensures that the wearer's interactions with new people are always remembered and recalled accurately, enhancing trust in the system’s memory capabilities.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define a local cache eviction rule that evicts entries older than 24 hours to balance memory retention and resource management for face/name recall.
