### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a fixed subtitle placement rule that ensures subtitles remain in a consistent, unobtrusive position throughout various confidence states, we can enhance the wearer's trust in the system. A stable and predictable subtitle location reduces cognitive load by eliminating the need for the wearer to constantly adjust their gaze or focus.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a single fixed subtitle placement rule that positions subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view, regardless of the confidence level. This rule would apply uniformly across all interactions and contexts.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing this fixed subtitle placement will reduce visual distractions during conversations, leading to higher trust in the system's real-time capabilities. The wearer can focus more on the conversation without worrying about whether subtitles might move unexpectedly or become difficult to read due to varying confidence levels.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that positions subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By clearly defining which transcript processing tasks should run locally on the phone versus falling back to cloud, we ensure that V1 remains focused on providing real-time, high-quality subtitles and contextually relevant one-line support without overloading the device's resources or compromising privacy.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific task boundary for live subtitle processing: all subtitle-related tasks that require immediate response should be handled locally on the phone to maintain stability and speed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This decision will ensure that subtitles remain clear, fast, and stable enough in real-time interactions, thereby maintaining user trust and satisfaction with the core functionality of the glasses. It also allows for more reliable memory and confidence handling, as these processes can leverage local storage and processing capabilities without cloud delays.

4. **Immediate next probe**
