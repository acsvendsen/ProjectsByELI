### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Ensuring consistent subtitle placement rules helps maintain a stable and reliable user experience, reducing visual distractions and enhancing trust in the system's ability to provide real-time assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define two fixed subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence situations where subtitles should be placed at the bottom of the field of view (FOV) with minimal overlap, and another for medium-to-low confidence situations where subtitles can be slightly more prominent but still avoid distracting visual elements.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By implementing consistent placement rules, users will experience a more stable and predictable display of subtitles, reducing visual noise and enhancing their trust in the system's reliability during conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format, we can ensure that the wearer understands the reliability of the information provided by the glasses. This clarity in certainty will enhance trust and reduce social friction, aligning with our core mission to help the wearer understand better without faking understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a simple numeric score along with a label and short reason for each confidence object. For example, display "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'". This format will provide immediate context to the wearer about how certain they can be of the displayed information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing this change will likely lead to a more transparent and trustworthy user experience. The wearers will understand when the glasses are highly confident versus when the information is less certain, leading to better overall interaction and reduced frustration.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a local cache size limit for person-memory objects, we ensure that the memory system retains only the most relevant and frequently accessed information, reducing the risk of storing low-value or outdated data while maintaining fast lookup performance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed threshold for the number of recent encounters before caching a new person-memory object locally on the phone.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will help maintain trust by ensuring that memory objects are not needlessly stored, which could lead to confusion or perceived invasive behavior. It also optimizes local storage space and improves lookup performance without overwhelming the cache with less relevant data.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define a threshold of 5 recent encounters before caching a new person-memory object locally on the phone.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing stays on the phone localizes heavy computational tasks, reducing latency and preserving battery life. This localization allows for faster response times and more stable performance in real-time scenarios, which is crucial for maintaining trust and providing timely assistance to the wearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear boundary where live subtitle processing runs on the phone only, with no fallback to the cloud under any circumstances until further optimization is needed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitle processing local, we can achieve more reliable and faster real-time performance, which directly benefits the core mission of providing timely and accurate subtitles in noisy environments. This reduces latency and ensures that the wearer receives assistance quickly, enhancing trust and user satisfaction.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one phone-local vs cloud fallback boundary for exactly one task: live subtitle processing.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support helps maintain low-friction assistance, allowing the wearer to focus on the conversation without being overwhelmed by too much information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple coexistence rule that prioritizes subtitle visibility over one-line prompts when both are active, ensuring subtitles remain prominent and readable.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By maintaining clear visibility of subtitles while keeping one-line prompts secondary, the system can provide just-in-time support without disrupting natural conversation flow or causing visual clutter.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support, prioritizing subtitles in the primary display area during active conversations.
