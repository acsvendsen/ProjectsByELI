### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules can significantly enhance the wearer's trust in the system by ensuring that subtitles are always visible and clear, even during dynamic interactions. Stable subtitle placement helps maintain readability and reduces visual distractions, making the subtitles more useful in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high confidence states (e.g., known speakers or highly likely matches) and another for low confidence states (e.g., new faces or uncertain matches). This differentiation ensures that critical information is always placed optimally, reducing the chance of misreading.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing these placement rules, V1 can deliver more reliable subtitles, which will enhance user trust and satisfaction. Users will find it easier to follow conversations in noisy environments, leading to a better overall experience without adding complexity or distraction.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
