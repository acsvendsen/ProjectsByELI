### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are always clear, readable, and unobtrusive, thereby enhancing real-time subtitle clarity (subtitle_clarity attractor). This will help the wearer maintain trust in the system during live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a new subtitle placement rule for low-confidence matches where subtitles appear at the bottom of the screen to avoid distracting the wearer while still providing critical information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will reduce visual distractions and improve overall trust in the system, making it more usable in noisy environments. It will also ensure that only high-confidence matches are prominently displayed, enhancing the clarity of key information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing remains local on the phone minimizes latency and battery drain, enhancing real-time clarity and trust. By keeping this task entirely local, we avoid the risk of cloud delays impacting the wearer's ability to follow conversations in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific boundary where transcript cleanup tasks that involve heavy processing can fall back to the cloud only if battery levels are above 80% and there is no immediate latency concern from the network.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing local, we ensure consistent and timely subtitle delivery, which directly benefits the core mission of real-time clarity and trust in noisy environments. This also aligns with the constraint that always-on sensing should not excessively drain the battery or introduce latency issues.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.
