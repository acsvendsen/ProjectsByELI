# Sleep cycle

## Core Field: SmartGlasses Core Focus

### Mission
Create smart glasses that act as a discreet, context-aware personal assistant focused on live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support to help the wearer understand and respond better in real-world interactions.

### Strengthening Signals
1. **Context Persistence**: Ensure context across sessions remains robust.
2. **Face/Name Memory**: Enhance algorithms for fast, confident recall of new faces and names through consistent reinforcement learning.
3. **Real-Time Subtitles**: Optimize real-time subtitle delivery in noisy environments without compromising battery life or user comfort.

### Weakening Signals
1. **Gimmick Risk**: Avoid adding non-essential features that may distract from core functionality.
2. **Feature Sprawl**: Prioritize core features over expanding the feature set to maintain focus on essential assistive value.
3. **Latency Management**: Ensure responses are timely and do not break trust.

### Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
1. **Privacy vs. Usefulness**: Balance providing helpful information without compromising privacy.
2. **Always-on Sensing vs. Battery Life**: Optimize hardware for low-power operation while maintaining necessary functionality.
3. **Local Processing vs. Cloud Intelligence**: Utilize the cloud for heavy processing where needed but keep critical operations local to reduce latency and ensure reliability.

### Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
1. **Confidence Indicators**: Implement a simple, effective confidence score or label to help users understand the reliability of prompts.
2. **Visual Clarity vs. Discreet UX**: Explore subtle UI elements that enhance user experience without drawing excessive attention.

### No Fluff
- Focus on real-world usability and effectiveness.
- Avoid features that do not significantly improve daily use value.
- Ensure all developments are aligned with the core mission of reducing social and cognitive friction.

## Evolution Rule
The project is allowed to evolve beyond its original form if it strengthens the core mission, improves real-world usefulness, or increases trust, clarity, and assistive value. Expansion should deepen the core functionality rather than distract from it.

## Reality-Check Rule
Continuously surface and address real-world limitations early, including hardware constraints, battery management, latency issues, privacy concerns, and usability challenges to avoid wasted development time on impractical directions.

## Open Questions
1. **V1 Split**: Determine the optimal allocation between glasses, phone, and cloud for V1.
2. **Memory Storage & Reinforcement**: Develop a strategy for storing and reinforcing face/name memory efficiently.
3. **Confidence Representation**: Design an intuitive method to represent confidence in prompts and memories.
4. **Subtitle/Prompt Coexistence**: Find a balance where subtitles and one-line prompts complement each other without overwhelming the user.

This consolidation focuses on key areas that need attention while ensuring alignment with the core mission of enhancing real-world interactions through discreet, context-aware assistance.
