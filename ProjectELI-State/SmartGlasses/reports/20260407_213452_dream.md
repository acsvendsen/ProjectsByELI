### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules helps ensure that subtitles remain stable, readable, and unobtrusive, which is crucial for maintaining trust in real-time conversations. Better placement can reduce distractions and enhance the wearer's focus on the conversation, thereby increasing the overall effectiveness of the smart glasses.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that places subtitles at the bottom center of the display, with a slight offset to the side, ensuring they are always visible but not overly intrusive. This rule can be adapted based on confidence levels: higher confidence placements might allow for slightly more space between lines, while lower confidence placements might require closer proximity to reduce latency issues.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting a consistent subtitle placement policy, the glasses will provide stable and readable subtitles that do not distract from the conversation. This stability will enhance user trust in the system, leading to better overall performance and acceptance of the product.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

This balance enhances subtitle clarity, memory support, and low-friction assistance by leveraging local processing where it’s most beneficial.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   By focusing V1’s local processing capabilities on live subtitle rendering, we can ensure real-time, high-quality subtitles that remain stable and clear even in noisy environments. This enhances the wearer's trust in the system without compromising on battery life or performance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
