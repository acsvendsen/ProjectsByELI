### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear subtitle placement rule that prioritizes stability over reactivity, we can ensure subtitles remain consistently readable and trustworthy in noisy environments. This stability helps maintain the wearer's trust and focus during conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a fixed subtitle position setting for V1, where subtitles are always displayed at a consistent location on the glasses' display, regardless of head movement or confidence levels.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A stable subtitle placement will reduce visual distractions and ensure that the wearer can trust the subtitles without constantly adjusting their gaze. This consistency will enhance overall user experience and reinforce the core mission of providing reliable real-time assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
