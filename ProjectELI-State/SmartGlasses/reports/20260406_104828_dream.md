### Adjacent-Value Improvements

#### 1. Enhanced Confidence Display Design

**Why it benefits the core:**
Improving how confidence is displayed can significantly enhance the wearer's trust in the system and improve their overall experience by providing clear, actionable information.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Introduce a simple visual indicator next to subtitles and prompts that shows a high-level confidence score (e.g., "High", "Medium", "Low"). This could be a colored dot or bar that appears for a brief moment before fading away, ensuring the wearer is aware of the system's confidence level.

**Likely payoff:**
Increased user trust in the system’s accuracy and reliability. Users will feel more confident relying on the system during critical interactions.

**Immediate next probe:**
Run an A/B test with different confidence indicators (e.g., green dot for high, yellow dot for medium, red dot for low) to see which visual style is most effective at improving user trust without overwhelming them.

#### 2. Optimized Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy

**Why it benefits the core:**
Proper subtitle placement ensures that subtitles are easily readable while minimizing distractions, thereby enhancing the wearer's ability to follow conversations in noisy environments.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Adjust the position of subtitles based on head movements and visual focus. For instance, if the user looks away from a particular area, move the subtitle slightly closer to their line of sight.

**Likely payoff:**
Improved readability and reduced cognitive load for the wearer, leading to better understanding and engagement in conversations.

**Immediate next probe:**
Implement an algorithm that tracks eye movements and subtly adjusts subtitle placement based on where the user is looking. Test this with real users in noisy environments to see if it improves their comprehension of spoken words.

---

#### 3. Memory Reinforcement for New Faces/Names

**Why it benefits the core:**
Effective memory reinforcement helps build a robust database of names and faces, ensuring that the system can provide accurate and timely recall during conversations.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Develop a dynamic ranking system where newly recognized faces or names are prioritized in memory lookup. This could involve increasing the confidence score of recently encountered individuals over time.

**Likely payoff:**
Faster and more reliable face/name recall, reducing the number of instances where the system falls back on less certain recognitions.

**Immediate next probe:**
Create a ranking policy that assigns higher initial confidence scores to newly recognized faces/names. Monitor how this affects memory retention and accuracy over several days of use.

---

#### 4. Low-Friction Visual UX for One-Line Prompts

**Why it benefits the core:**
One-line prompts need to be presented in a way that is clear, brief, and unobtrusive, ensuring they add value without disrupting the user’s focus.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Implement a fade-in/fade-out mechanism for one-line prompts. This ensures the prompt appears quickly and disappears just as fast, minimizing disruption to the conversation flow.

**Likely payoff:**
More natural and unobtrusive delivery of contextual cues, reducing cognitive load on the user during conversations.

**Immediate next probe:**
Experiment with different durations for fade-in/fade-out effects (e.g., 1 second in, 3 seconds out) to find the optimal balance between visibility and non-intrusiveness. Conduct a usability test with real users to gather feedback.

---

#### 5. Battery/Thermal-Conscious Architecture

**Why it benefits the core:**
Ensuring efficient power consumption is crucial for maintaining an always-on system that can provide continuous assistance without compromising on battery life or performance.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Optimize the glasses’ processing and display logic to minimize unnecessary computations and data transfers, especially when no significant context changes occur.

**Likely payoff:**
Extended battery life and improved thermal management, ensuring the glasses remain usable throughout the day without frequent charging breaks.

**Immediate next probe:**
Introduce a power-saving mode that reduces processing intensity during periods of low activity (e.g., when not in use for 10 seconds). Monitor how this affects overall system performance and user satisfaction.
