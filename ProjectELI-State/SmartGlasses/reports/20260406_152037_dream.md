### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are always placed in a manner that maximizes readability while minimizing distraction. This will help maintain the wearer's focus on their immediate environment and reduce cognitive overload.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 subtitle placement rules based on the confidence level of the detected speaker or context, such as:
   - Always place subtitles at the bottom center when the confidence is high.
   - Place subtitles slightly lower than center when the confidence is medium.
   - Use a more prominent but non-distracting position (like edge corner) for low-confidence speakers.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will enhance the wearer's ability to read subtitles without being distracted by them, leading to improved comprehension and less cognitive strain during interactions. It also helps in maintaining the subtlety of the glasses, making them more socially acceptable.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By defining clear phone-local vs cloud boundaries for specific tasks, we can optimize both privacy and utility. For instance, live subtitle processing should be done locally to ensure real-time responsiveness, while less critical background tasks like face/name recall lookup can leverage the cloud.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a clear rule that live subtitle processing runs on-device and only falls back to the phone or cloud if local processing fails due to extreme latency constraints.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - This approach will ensure the system remains highly responsive, maintaining real-time subtitle quality without compromising privacy by offloading non-critical tasks. It also simplifies the architecture, making it easier to manage and optimize.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one task-specific cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing that prioritizes local processing but allows remote help if absolutely necessary due to extreme latency.
