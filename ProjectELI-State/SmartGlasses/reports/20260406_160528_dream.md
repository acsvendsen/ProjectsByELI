### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always visible when needed but do not distract when they are less certain or unnecessary.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define three subtitle placement modes: default prominent (high confidence), fallback hidden (low confidence), and intermediate visibility for medium confidence.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - This will improve the balance between clarity and distraction, ensuring that subtitles are always useful without overwhelming the wearer. It will also help in maintaining focus during less certain moments, thereby enhancing overall trust in the system's reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving the confidence display format enhances user trust by clearly indicating the reliability of the system's outputs. This is crucial for maintaining the wearer's confidence, especially in high-stakes interactions like business meetings or medical appointments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a numeric score range and corresponding label with a short reason for each confidence level. For example:
     - High (0.85+): "High" + "Seen recently"
     - Medium (0.65-0.84): "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction"
     - Low (<0.65): "Low" + "Weak match"

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By providing a clear, concise, and consistent confidence format, the system will be more transparent about its reliability, helping to build trust with the wearer over time. This clarity can reduce misunderstandings and improve overall user satisfaction.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
