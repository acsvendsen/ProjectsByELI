## Core Field Summary
The SmartGlasses project remains focused on delivering discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The system must prioritize subtitle clarity, reliable memory support, and low-friction interaction while managing real-world constraints like privacy, battery, latency, and visual UX.

### Decision Areas
- **Subtitle Quality**: Ensure subtitles are clear and fast.
- **Memory Trust**: Provide reliable name and context recall without overpromising on certainty.
- **Confidence Display**: Represent confidence clearly to build trust.
- **Automatic Memory Support**: Remember new faces and names with confidence objects.
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Design for social acceptability while providing useful information.

### Core Tensions
1. **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Maintain a balance that respects user privacy while providing helpful assistance.
2. **Latency vs Richness**: Deliver timely, clear responses without overwhelming the wearer with too much information.
3. **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**: Ensure the system remains socially acceptable while still being readable in motion and noise.

### Real-World Constraints
- Glasses should remain lightweight and socially acceptable.
- Early versions should not depend on heavy on-glasses compute.
- Battery and thermals are major risks for always-on features.
- Subtitles must feel near-real-time or trust breaks.
- Face/name recall must expose uncertainty clearly.
- First versions should rely heavily on phone processing, with cloud optional where useful.

### Interaction Constraints
- V1 interaction should be controlled through frame touches only.
- Do not assume voice activation or spoken command control for subtitle management, mode switching, or confirmations in V1.
