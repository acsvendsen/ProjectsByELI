# Sleep Cycle Consolidation

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Clarity**: The subtitle display must remain clear, fast, and stable in live conversations to ensure the wearer can trust them.
- **Memory Trust**: The system should remember names, faces, and prior context accurately without overcommitting or introducing errors.

## Weakening Signals
- **Low-Friction Assistance**: Current efforts are focusing more on subtitle clarity and memory support at the expense of one-line prompts. This means the emphasis on low-friction assistance is being reduced in favor of core mission improvements.

## Contradictions that Must Stay Alive
- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: The system must balance providing useful assistive features without invading privacy.
- **Latency vs Richness**: The system should provide timely help while avoiding overloading with richer but slower processing.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy**: These ideas are worth revisiting to ensure the subtitles are readable in motion and noise, enhancing usability without overwhelming the wearer.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Mission

### Subtitle Quality
- **Real-time subtitle clarity** remains a high-priority attractor. Ensuring subtitles are clear, fast, and stable is critical for trust.

### Confidence Trust
- **Memory support that helps the wearer recall names, people, and prior context without pretending certainty** continues to be a strong attractor. The system should reinforce this by ensuring memory is reliable and not overconfident.

### Visual UX
- **Discreet UX vs visual clarity**: The glasses should remain socially acceptable while still being readable in motion and noise. This tension will continue to guide design decisions on subtitle placement and visual hierarchy.
