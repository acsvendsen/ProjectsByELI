### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By establishing fixed subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, V1 can ensure that subtitles remain clear, stable, and unobtrusive during live interactions. This will enhance the wearer's trust in the system by providing consistent and reliable support without causing distraction or discomfort.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a set of fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: for high-confidence matches, place subtitles at the bottom center; for medium-confidence matches, position them slightly lower to avoid overlap; and for low-confidence matches, show them in a less prominent area, such as near the edge or bottom-right corner.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will create a more predictable and stable subtitle experience, reducing the risk of subtitles shifting or becoming too intrusive during conversations. It will also help maintain visual clarity by ensuring that the most important information is always easily readable, thereby improving overall usability and trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format will enhance the wearer's trust in the system by clearly indicating the certainty of the information provided. This clarity helps reduce social and cognitive friction, ensuring the wearer can rely on the subtitles without feeling that they might be missing critical details.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence format for subtitle text that includes a numeric score, a label (e.g., High, Medium, Low), and a short reason (e.g., "Seen recently", "Weak match"). This format will help the wearer understand the reliability of the information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this confidence format, the system can provide more reliable subtitles that the wearer can trust in real-time conversations. This improvement will increase overall user satisfaction and trust in the product, reducing social awkwardness and cognitive load during interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud, we can ensure that critical real-time subtitle processing and face/name recall remain stable and low-latency on-device. This enhances trust and reduces dependency on unreliable network conditions or latency issues.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one task-specific cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing. For example, explicitly set a rule that subtitles are generated locally using the phone's runtime, but fall back to the cloud if local processing fails due to thermal constraints or battery limits.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle generation on-device, we can provide near-real-time responses with minimal latency, ensuring that subtitles remain clear and stable during conversations. This improves user trust in the system's ability to help them understand what is being said, especially in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one task-specific boundary for live subtitle processing, where subtitles are generated locally using the phone's runtime but fall back to the cloud if local processing fails due to thermal constraints or battery limits.
