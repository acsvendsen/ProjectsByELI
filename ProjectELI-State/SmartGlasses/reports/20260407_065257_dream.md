### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain readable, stable, and trust-preserving in live conversation. By defining specific subtitle placement modes based on confidence levels, we can ensure that critical information is always visible and not obstructed by less important text.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence content (e.g., names and key phrases) that should be displayed prominently at the top of the screen, and another for lower-confidence content (e.g., supporting information or background noise) that can be placed near the bottom to avoid distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly distinguishing between high- and low-confidence content, we enhance the wearer's ability to focus on critical information without being overwhelmed by less important text. This improves overall comprehension and reduces cognitive load during live conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format will enhance the trustworthiness of the system, ensuring that the wearer can rely on the subtitles and memory support provided by the glasses. A clear and concise confidence display helps in building a strong relationship between the glasses and the user, making the assistive behavior more acceptable and useful.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one confidence format for V1 that includes a numeric score, a label, and a short reason. This will provide a clear indication of the reliability of the displayed information without overwhelming the wearer with too much detail.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a simple yet informative confidence display, the system can maintain high levels of trust among users. Users will be more likely to accept the glasses as a reliable assistant, which in turn can lead to higher daily use and better integration into their lives.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a clear local cache threshold for face and name memory, we ensure that the system retains only the most relevant and frequently encountered information, reducing memory overload while maintaining essential recall capabilities.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache eviction policy that removes entities from memory if they have not been interacted with or seen in recent sessions, but retain key figures like those repeatedly encountered in conversations.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will help manage the complexity of memory storage, ensuring that the system remains light and responsive without compromising on the trustworthiness and relevance of name recall during live interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set the local cache size limit for face and name recognition to 50 entries.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary between what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud for live subtitle processing, we ensure that subtitles remain fast and reliable in real-time. This decision will help maintain the core attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity" by reducing latency and ensuring that the glasses can handle critical processing locally.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single task-specific phone/cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing, where all live subtitles are processed on-device unless explicitly necessary to use cloud resources due to constraints like high speaker diversity or complex background noise.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing local, we can reduce latency and ensure that subtitles remain clear and stable in real-time, which is crucial for the wearer's understanding in noisy environments. This decision will directly support the core attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity" by maintaining a consistent and reliable display of subtitles.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one phone/cloud fallback boundary for exactly one task such as live subtitle processing.
