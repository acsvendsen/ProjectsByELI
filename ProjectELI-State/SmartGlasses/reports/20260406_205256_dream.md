### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining fixed subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we ensure that subtitles remain stable and unobtrusive while still providing clear assistance. This helps maintain trust by avoiding distracting reactive behavior and ensuring that subtitles are always readable and reliable.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches where the text is placed in a fixed, non-distracting position on the screen, and another for low-confidence matches where the text is slightly more prominent but still unobtrusive.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that subtitles are always readable and stable, reducing the risk of breaking trust with sudden repositioning or flickering. It also simplifies the UI design, making it easier for users to focus on the conversation without distractions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format, we can ensure that the wearer has a clear and trustworthy understanding of the system's certainty in providing information. This improves overall trust in the assistant’s ability to help without overloading the user with complex or uncertain data.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a consistent confidence format for all subtitle and memory-related interactions, using a numeric score (e.g., 0-1) combined with a label and short reason. For example: "score 0.85 + 'High' + 'Name heard multiple times recently'".

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will enhance the wearer’s trust in the system by providing clear, concise, and consistent certainty indicators. It helps prevent confusion and ensures that the wearer can easily understand when they should rely on the system's help.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the confidence score presentation as "score 0.85 + 'High' + 'Name heard multiple times recently'".

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   To deepen the core mission of providing trustworthy memory support, we need to ensure that new faces and names are remembered accurately over time. This involves setting up a robust cache policy that reinforces memory objects based on repeated encounters while keeping local storage light enough to avoid battery drain.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local memory threshold for attaching new evidence to an existing person-memory object, which triggers reinforcement when a face or name is seen multiple times in quick succession.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this local memory threshold, the system will build stronger confidence objects over time, reducing false negatives and improving overall trustworthiness. This leads to more reliable memory recall for names and faces during critical conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one reinforcement rule for repeated face/name encounters with a recency threshold of 10 touches within an hour.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what runs on the phone versus the cloud, we ensure that V1 remains focused on delivering reliable and low-friction assistance without unnecessary complexity. This will help maintain the system's privacy and usability while allowing the glasses to remain lightweight and socially acceptable.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one specific task boundary for what runs locally on the phone versus what falls back to the cloud. For example, define a clear rule for when live subtitle processing should run on the phone and when it should use cloud fallbacks.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly defining this boundary, we can optimize battery usage, ensure low latency, and maintain privacy. This will help in creating a more reliable and usable product that respects real-world constraints like battery life and social acceptability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring clear, stable, and timely subtitle placement enhances the wearer's ability to follow conversations in noisy environments. By maintaining a consistent and readable visual hierarchy, the system reduces cognitive load and keeps the user focused on the conversation at hand.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one subtitle placement rule that prioritizes readability over distraction: for high-confidence subtitles, place them above the conversation text; for medium-confidence subtitles, allow a slight overlap with the conversation but ensure they do not obscure critical words.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will improve the wearer's trust in the subtitles by making them more readable and less distracting, thus enhancing overall comprehension during live interactions. It will also reduce the risk of missing important information due to overlapping text or poor placement.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
