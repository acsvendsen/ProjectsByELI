# Consolidation of SmartGlasses Core Field for V1

## Strengthening Signals
1. **Subtitle Quality and Real-Time Performance**: Subtitles should be real-time, accurate, and easily readable without causing distraction. This will enhance the user's understanding in noisy environments and during fast-paced conversations.
2. **Memory Reinforcement**: The system must effectively reinforce memory of new faces and names to build trust and reliability over time.

## Weakening Signals
1. **Complex Interaction Methods**: Avoid incorporating voice activation or complex multimodal UIs for V1. Stick to frame-touch interactions only.
2. **Fancy AR Visuals and Heavy Autonomous Behavior**: These features are not prioritized in the current V1 architecture, as they add unnecessary complexity without immediate utility.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
1. **Discreet vs Useful UX**: The system must balance discretion with usefulness to avoid social friction while providing essential assistance.
2. **Battery Constraints vs Always-On Sensing**: Battery limitations should guide the always-on features and sensing capabilities, ensuring that resource-intensive operations are optimized.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
1. **Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy**: These ideas should be revisited to ensure subtitles and prompts are placed optimally for clarity without distraction.
2. **Confidence Display Formats**: Explore different confidence display formats (label only, label + short reason, score + label) to find the best balance between trust and usability.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Areas
1. **Subtitle Quality vs Battery Usage**:
   - Ensuring real-time subtitle quality requires significant processing power that can drain battery life.
2. **Memory Trust vs Privacy Perception**:
   - Storing and using memory for face/name recall must be balanced with privacy concerns to maintain user trust.
3. **Always-On Sensing vs Social Acceptability**:
   - Continuous sensing may conflict with social acceptability, so it should be carefully managed.
4. **Rich Memory vs Fast Lookup**:
   - The system needs a balance between storing detailed contextual information and providing quick access to relevant data.

## Open Questions
1. **Subtitle Placement**: What subtitle placement is most usable without becoming distracting?
2. **Confidence Format**: How should confidence be represented: numeric score, label, and short reason?
3. **Phone/Cloud Boundary**: Which interactions should work offline versus requiring cloud support?

## Core Field Consolidation

### Mission
Create smart glasses that act as a discreet, context-aware personal assistant focused on live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support to help the wearer understand and respond better in real-world interactions.

### Non-negotiables
- The glasses help the wearer; they do not pretend knowledge the wearer does not have.
- Early hardware remains simple: display text, record audio/visual input; phone/cloud handles heavy processing.
- V1 interaction is frame-touch only; do not assume voice activation or spoken command control.
- Context must persist across sessions.
- New faces and names should be remembered with confidence objects and reinforcement over time.
- Real-world constraints must be surfaced early: latency, battery, privacy, social acceptability, on-device limits.

### Core Attractors
- Real-time subtitle assistance in noisy environments
- Personal memory support for names and prior conversations
- One-line contextual prompts rather than overwhelming paragraphs
- Low-friction human experience; assistive, not deceptive

### Core Tensions
- Privacy vs usefulness
- Always-on sensing vs battery and thermals
- Local processing vs cloud intelligence
- Rich memory vs fast lookup
- Discreet UX vs enough visual clarity

### Current Open Questions
1. **Subtitle Placement**: What is the best V1 split between glasses, phone, and cloud?
2. **Confidence Display**: How should face/name memory be stored and reinforced for fast lookup and trust?
3. **Memory Policy**: How should confidence be represented: numeric score, label, and short reason?
4. **Subtitle Coexistence**: How should subtitles and one-line prompts coexist without overloading the wearer?
5. **Visual Hierarchy**: What is the best subtitle placement and visual hierarchy for fast reading with low distraction?
