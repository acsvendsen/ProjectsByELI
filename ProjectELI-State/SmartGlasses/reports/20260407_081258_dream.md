### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules based on confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles remain clear and stable enough to be trusted in live conversations. This approach helps maintain the wearer's focus and understanding without overwhelming them with too many cues or distractions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence situations where subtitles are shown at a fixed position on the screen, and another for medium-low confidence situations where subtitles can be slightly more dynamic to attract attention when needed but return to a stable position quickly.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This refined approach will lead to more consistent and reliable subtitle placement that enhances trust in critical moments of conversation while avoiding visual overload. It supports the core mission by ensuring the wearer remains engaged without being distracted or overwhelmed.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the system's assistance, which is crucial for maintaining their presence and understanding in real-world interactions. A clear and consistent confidence indicator allows the wearer to quickly assess the reliability of the information provided.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a specific confidence label format: `score 0.85 + "High" + "Seen recently"` for high-confidence items, and `score 0.40 + "Low" + "Weak match"` for low-confidence items. This format combines a numeric score with a qualitative label and a short reason to give the wearer clear feedback on the system's confidence in its information.

3. **Likely payoff**

This change will enhance trust by providing transparent and clear indicators of the system’s confidence, allowing the wearer to make better-informed decisions based on the reliability of the provided information. This will reduce cognitive load and increase the overall effectiveness of the assistant.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing remains on-device reduces latency, which is crucial for maintaining trust and clarity in real-time conversations. By keeping this task phone-local, we can provide subtitles that are near-real-time and less prone to network interruptions or delays.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific rule that any live subtitle processing must be handled by the phone's runtime, with no fallbacks allowed unless explicitly required for memory-intensive operations or context lookup tasks.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing phone-local, we ensure faster and more reliable subtitles, which will enhance the wearer’s confidence in using the system during conversations. This stability is key to building trust without introducing additional latency that could degrade the user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
