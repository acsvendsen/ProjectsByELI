### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By defining a clear phone/cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing, we ensure that critical real-time subtitles are available even if cloud access is delayed or unavailable. This improves trust and reliability by maintaining continuity of assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define one specific task as the boundary for live subtitle processing: "live subtitle processing" should primarily occur on-device with a fallback to the cloud only in cases where local processing cannot meet latency requirements.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By keeping subtitles local, we can ensure they are always available and stable, reducing the risk of dropouts or delays that could break trust. This also allows for more efficient use of battery and thermal resources on-device.

4. **Immediate next probe**
