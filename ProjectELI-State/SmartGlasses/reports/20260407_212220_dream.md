### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement that remains readable in motion and noise is crucial for maintaining trust in the system. Stable subtitle placement helps the wearer focus on the conversation without being distracted by shifting text, which aligns with the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that prioritizes readability over dynamic adjustment based on confidence levels. For example, place subtitles at the bottom center of the frame, ensuring they are always visible and stable, regardless of head movement or context changes.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining a fixed subtitle placement, V1 can ensure that the wearer has a consistent visual experience, reducing distraction and enhancing trust in the system's ability to provide clear and reliable subtitles. This stability also allows for faster lookup and reinforcement over time, as the subtitle location is predictable and unambiguous.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
