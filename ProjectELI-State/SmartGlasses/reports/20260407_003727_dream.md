### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules helps ensure that subtitles remain clear and stable enough for the wearer to trust them in live conversation. By defining fixed subtitle positions, we can reduce visual distractions and maintain a consistent experience across different confidence levels.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: one fixed position at the bottom of the screen and another slightly offset but still visible from the center. This allows for both high clarity and minimal distraction, optimizing readability without overwhelming the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing these fixed subtitle positions, we can ensure that subtitles remain consistently readable and do not become a source of visual strain or distraction during live conversations. This will enhance the wearer's trust in the system and improve their overall understanding of spoken content.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format to use a simple label with a short reason for uncertainty, we can enhance trust in the system's memory and recall capabilities. This approach ensures that when names or faces are recognized but not fully certain, the wearer is informed clearly without overloading them with technical details.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   The small change involves defining a new confidence format: `score 0.63 + "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction"`. This format uses a numeric score to indicate certainty (in this case, medium), a label (`Medium`), and a short reason (`Name heard once in introduction`) that provides context without being overly verbose.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing this confidence format will help build trust with the wearer by clearly indicating the level of certainty in memory recall. This transparency can reduce confusion and enhance the reliability of the system, making it more useful in real-world interactions where accuracy is crucial.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting a clear threshold for local caching, the system can prioritize frequently encountered individuals, reducing latency in recall during conversations and increasing overall trustworthiness of name and face recognition.

4. **Immediate next probe**
