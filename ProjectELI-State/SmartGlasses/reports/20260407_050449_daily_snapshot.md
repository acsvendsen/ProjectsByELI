**Subtitle Placement**
- **Current Status:** The glasses should ensure subtitles are readable, stable, and do not distract the wearer.
- **Constraints:** Glasses must remain lightweight and socially acceptable. Early versions cannot depend on heavy on-glasses compute.

- **Low-Distraction Design:** Ensure subtitles stay within the wearer's peripheral vision and do not block primary visual focus. A subtle text color against a dark background could help achieve this.

### Feasible Later
- **Advanced Techniques:** Explore microdisplays or transparent OLED technology that can integrate subtitles directly into the wearer’s field of view without requiring a separate screen. This would enhance readability but might involve significant hardware and battery constraints.
- **Dynamic Positioning:** Develop algorithms to dynamically position subtitles based on head movement, ensuring they remain stable even when the user looks around.

### Likely Waste of Time
- **Extensive VR/AR Integration:** Early versions should focus on simple, fixed placements rather than exploring complex AR solutions that are not yet feasible given current hardware constraints. Such features might introduce unnecessary complexity early in development.
