### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Stable subtitle placement rules enhance user trust by ensuring that subtitles remain in a predictable, unobtrusive position during live conversations. This consistency helps the wearer focus on the conversation without being distracted by moving or flickering text, thereby improving overall understanding and engagement.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two fixed subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence states (e.g., when the speaker is known or the context is clear) and another for low-confidence states (e.g., when the speaker is new or the environment is noisy).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing consistent, predictable subtitle placement based on confidence levels, the system can significantly reduce visual distraction during conversations. This clarity allows the wearer to focus more on the interaction itself, enhancing the overall user experience and trust in the assistant's reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear, concise, and reliable confidence format for memory support helps build trust in the system's accuracy and reliability. This clarity is crucial for the wearer to understand the system's level of certainty without overloading them with complex or uncertain information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple numeric score + label + short reason format for confidence objects, where the label indicates high, medium, or low certainty, and the short reason provides a brief context for the confidence level (e.g., "Seen recently," "Weak match," "Name heard once").

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a clear and concise confidence format, users can better understand when to trust memory recall and how certain they should be of the information presented. This enhances the overall trust in the system's reliability and accuracy, which is critical for its primary mission of aiding understanding in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the local cache size and eviction policy for face/name memory will ensure that frequently encountered individuals are always quickly accessible, maintaining trust and reducing friction in social interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a basic LRU (Least Recently Used) cache eviction rule that keeps a fixed number of top-recently-seen faces and names locally on the phone while evicting older entries to make room for new evidence.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will enhance the memory support by ensuring that recently seen individuals are always quickly accessible, reducing the need for cloud queries and improving overall performance in noisy environments where quick face/name recall is crucial.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.
