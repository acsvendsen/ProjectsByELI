### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules under different confidence states enhances the wearer's trust in the system by making subtitles more predictable and less distracting. This improves overall understanding, especially in noisy environments where consistency is crucial.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules for low-confidence and high-confidence states: 
   - For low confidence (e.g., 0.5 or below), center the subtitle to minimize visual clutter.
   - For high confidence (e.g., above 0.7), place subtitles slightly higher on the screen, allowing for easier reading without distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing stable placement rules based on confidence levels, the system will reduce visual distractions during low-confidence scenarios while maintaining readability in high-confidence contexts. This balance enhances user trust and overall usability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
