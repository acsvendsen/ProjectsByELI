### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a fixed subtitle placement rule that prioritizes stability over reactivity, we ensure that subtitles remain clear and consistent in live conversation, aligning with the attractor of real-time subtitle clarity. This helps maintain trust without overwhelming the wearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single default subtitle placement mode where subtitles appear at the bottom center of the field of view, regardless of confidence level or speaker identity.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This fixed placement rule simplifies the user experience by reducing cognitive load and ensuring that subtitles remain visible and readable in any context. It also aligns with the low-friction assistance attractor by minimizing visual distraction while still providing critical information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a fixed default subtitle placement mode where subtitles appear at the bottom center of the field of view.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format enhances trust in the system's understanding and memory support. By clearly showing the certainty level and reason behind memory recall or subtitle accuracy, the wearer can better judge the reliability of the information provided, thus reinforcing their confidence in the smart glasses' assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence label format that includes a numeric score, a high-level label like "High," "Medium," or "Low," and a short reason for the confidence level. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a clear and concise confidence format, users can better understand when they should trust the information provided by the glasses. This transparency helps in building user trust over time as the system consistently reinforces its reliability through accurate and timely feedback.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving local memory thresholds for face/name recall ensures that frequently encountered individuals are remembered accurately and quickly, which enhances trust in the system's ability to support real-world interactions effectively.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit of 50 recently seen people or names on the phone, with a recency threshold of 30 days, to ensure that common individuals are remembered reliably while avoiding excessive memory usage.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting a clear and manageable local cache size, the system will be able to provide accurate and timely face/name recall for frequently encountered people without overwhelming the phone's limited resources, thus improving overall trust and utility.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the phone/cloud boundary for live subtitle processing ensures that the core mission of providing real-time, clear subtitles is maintained without undue battery or thermal strain on the glasses. By carefully defining what stays local and what falls back to the cloud, we can optimize both the wearer's experience and the hardware constraints.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific task boundary for live subtitle processing: only critical parts of the transcription pipeline that require real-time accuracy should run on the glasses, while less time-sensitive steps fall back to the phone or cloud.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By optimizing the local vs. cloud split for live subtitles, we can ensure that the system remains responsive and clear in noisy environments, maintaining trust with the wearer. This will also help in managing battery usage more efficiently without compromising on subtitle quality.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support, we ensure that the wearer can focus on critical information without distraction. This enhances trust in real-time subtitles while maintaining the utility of brief contextual prompts.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a priority rule that shows subtitles as the primary content and suppresses or minimizes one-line support text when subtitles are active, but allows one-line support to coexist with subtitles when there is additional context needed without overwhelming the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will improve the overall UX by ensuring that critical information (subtitles) remains prominent while still providing necessary contextual assistance in a non-disruptive way. It aligns with the goal of low-friction assistance and helps maintain trust by not overloading the user's visual field.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
