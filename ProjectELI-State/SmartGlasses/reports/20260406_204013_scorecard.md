## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The current V1 interaction remains frame-touch only with minimal on-glasses compute to ensure social acceptability and battery efficiency.
