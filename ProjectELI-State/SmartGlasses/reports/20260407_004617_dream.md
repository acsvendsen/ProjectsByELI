### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules to prioritize stable, unobtrusive positioning, we ensure that subtitles remain readable and trustworthy during live interactions. This improves the wearer's confidence in understanding spoken content without drawing unnecessary attention to the glasses.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a new subtitle placement rule that prioritizes fixed position over dynamic adjustments based on head movement or speaker proximity. For instance, default subtitles to a fixed bottom center display area, only adjusting for extreme head tilts or significant changes in speaking volume.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will reduce visual distraction during conversations and improve the wearer's trust in the subtitle system. A stable placement rule ensures that subtitles remain visible without jumping around, which can be particularly disruptive in noisy environments where accurate understanding is crucial.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the subtitles and memory support provided by the glasses. A clear, concise, and accurate confidence label and short reason will reduce uncertainty and build trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific confidence format that uses a numeric score, label, and short reason to convey certainty and source of information. For example: "score 0.85 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'; score 0.34 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'; score 0.62 + 'Medium' + 'Name heard once in introduction'."

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly indicating the certainty of each piece of information (subtitles, names, etc.), the glasses will reduce user uncertainty and build trust. This clarity will help the wearer make better decisions based on the real-time context provided by the glasses.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

3. **Likely payoff**

4. **Immediate next probe**
