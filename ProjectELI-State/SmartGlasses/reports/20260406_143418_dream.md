1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining fixed subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are consistently visible and readable without becoming distracting. This helps maintain a balance between providing clear assistance and avoiding visual overload.

   - **High Confidence:** Subtitles placed directly below the speaker's face.

   This approach will provide a more predictable and user-friendly experience, reducing visual clutter and ensuring that the wearer can easily find subtitles when needed. It also supports the core mission of making real-time assistance as clear and unobtrusive as possible.
