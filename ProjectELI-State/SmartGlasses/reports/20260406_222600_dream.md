### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always readable and stable, which enhances real-time understanding without becoming distracting. This improves both the subtitle clarity attractor and the low-friction assistance attractor by providing clear, reliable information to the wearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a set of fixed subtitle placement rules for different confidence states:
   - High confidence: Place subtitles at the bottom center.
   - Medium confidence: Place subtitles slightly above eye level with a slight leftward offset.
   - Low confidence: Display subtitles in a smaller, less prominent position, possibly in the lower-right corner.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these fixed placement rules will reduce subtitle distraction and ensure that critical information is always visible. This will lead to better user trust and satisfaction, as the subtitles remain readable even during high-activity conversations or when the confidence levels are uncertain.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
