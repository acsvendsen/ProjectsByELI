### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain clear and stable enough for the wearer to trust them in live conversations. This enhances real-time subtitle clarity, which is a primary attractor. Stable subtitle placement reduces visual distractions and maintains focus on the conversation, thereby improving the wearer's understanding and confidence.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence situations where subtitles should be clearly visible but not distracting (e.g., when the speaker is known), and another for low-confidence situations where subtitles can be slightly less prominent to avoid overloading the visual field (e.g., during introductions or ambiguous contexts).

3. **Likely payoff**

Implementing these rules will ensure that subtitles are appropriately placed based on the confidence level, reducing distractions and maintaining clarity. This will help in creating a more seamless and natural user experience, which is crucial for real-time understanding and memory support.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the subtitle information in real-time interactions. By clearly indicating the certainty level of the subtitles, the system maintains a high level of reliability and credibility, which is crucial for the core mission of enhancing understanding without pretending knowledge.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a confidence display format that includes both a numeric score and a short reason for the certainty level. For example, "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'" or "score 0.35 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'". This simple change enhances the clarity of the subtitle's reliability.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing a clear and concise confidence display, the system can reduce ambiguity in live interactions, leading to better trust from the wearer. This improvement will also help in reinforcing memory recall by clearly indicating when the system is certain or uncertain about the information it presents.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
