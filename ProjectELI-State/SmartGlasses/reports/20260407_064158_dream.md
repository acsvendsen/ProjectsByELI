### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, V1 can maintain a balance between clarity and distraction. This ensures that high-confidence subtitles are more prominent and easily readable while low-confidence ones remain unobtrusive but still visible.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence subtitles to be shown prominently at the bottom of the display, and another for low-confidence subtitles to be shown slightly above the line but with reduced opacity or size.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will enhance the user experience by reducing visual clutter while ensuring that important information is clearly visible. It will also help maintain the wearer's focus on the primary conversation without being distracted by less certain text, thus improving overall trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving the confidence display format enhances trust in subtitle quality by clearly indicating the reliability of the subtitles and the memory support. This helps ensure that the wearer understands when to rely on the subtitles or seek clarification, thus maintaining high-quality assistive behavior.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a confidence format for both subtitle confidence and memory recall that uses a numeric score + label + short reason. For example: "score 0.95 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'; score 0.38 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'. This provides clear, concise, and contextually relevant information to the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

This change will allow the wearer to better understand the reliability of the subtitles and memory support in real-time. By providing a clear indication of confidence, it reduces uncertainty and increases trust, ensuring that the wearer can make informed decisions about how to respond in social interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
