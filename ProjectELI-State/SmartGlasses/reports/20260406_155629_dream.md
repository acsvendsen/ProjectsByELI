### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain a consistent and predictable user experience, which is crucial for building trust and ensuring that subtitles are always easily readable without becoming distracting.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: high-confidence subtitles placed more prominently (e.g., 50% screen width), medium-confidence subtitles slightly less prominent (e.g., 30% screen width), and low-confidence subtitles barely visible or hidden entirely unless explicitly requested by the user.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining clear subtitle placement rules, users will experience a more consistent and predictable interface. This reduces cognitive load and ensures that high-confidence subtitles are always easily readable, while lower confidence ones do not distract from the conversation. Users can focus on understanding the content without constantly adjusting their gaze or scrolling through less certain information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
