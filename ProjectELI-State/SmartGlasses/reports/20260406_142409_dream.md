### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always visible where they are most needed while minimizing distractions. This improves the overall user experience by balancing clarity with social acceptability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple three-tier subtitle placement system: fixed position at eye level for high-confidence matches, dynamic adjustment to gaze direction for medium-confidence matches, and minimal or no subtitles for low-confidence matches.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will reduce visual distractions by limiting the display of subtitles when the confidence is low, thus preventing information overload. It also ensures that critical information is always prominently visible, enhancing the wearer's understanding without causing social discomfort.

4. **Immediate next probe**
