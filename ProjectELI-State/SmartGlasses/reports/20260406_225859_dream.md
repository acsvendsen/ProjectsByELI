### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Subtitle placement that is stable, predictable, and minimally intrusive helps maintain a consistent user experience without adding cognitive load or distracting from the conversation. Stable subtitle rules ensure that subtitles do not shift unexpectedly, which can cause confusion or reduce trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: 
- Low confidence (e.g., 0.5 and below) should place subtitles at the bottom of the screen.
- Medium confidence (e.g., 0.6 to 0.8) should place subtitles slightly above center but still within a quick glance range.
- High confidence (above 0.8) can use a slightly higher position or even center placement.

3. **Likely payoff**

By defining clear and consistent subtitle placement rules, the system will provide more stable and predictable subtitles that do not distract from the conversation. This will enhance trust in the system by making the experience smoother and less intrusive. Users will be able to focus on their interactions without worrying about constantly adjusting their gaze or attention.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

3. **Likely payoff**

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud, we can ensure that critical subtitle processing and memory lookup happen on the device, maintaining real-time responsiveness and privacy. This alignment helps in delivering the best possible user experience while respecting privacy concerns.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one task-specific cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing, such as deciding when to send audio inputs to the cloud for improved accuracy but prioritizing local processing under low-latency constraints.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By optimizing the live subtitle processing workflow, we can maintain high-quality, real-time subtitles without overloading the device with complex or resource-intensive tasks. This ensures that the core mission of providing clear and timely assistance is met effectively, enhancing trust and user satisfaction.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support helps maintain focus on key information, reducing cognitive load and social awkwardness. This balance enhances the wearer's ability to follow conversations while minimizing distraction.

   Define a rule that prioritizes subtitle visibility over one-line prompts when both are present. This can be achieved by setting a default precedence for subtitles in the visual hierarchy, with one-line prompts taking secondary display space or being suppressed if the subtitle content is dense and critical.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly separating and prioritizing the most crucial information (subtitles), this change will improve the wearer's ability to follow conversations, thereby enhancing their understanding and engagement in real-world interactions without overwhelming them with additional text.
