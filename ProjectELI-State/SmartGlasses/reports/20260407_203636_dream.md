### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules based on confidence states, we ensure that subtitles remain clear and stable enough to be trusted in live conversations, while also reducing visual distractions during moments of lower uncertainty. This helps maintain focus and trust without overwhelming the wearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence situations (e.g., well-known speakers) where subtitles can be placed lower or less frequently, and another for low-confidence situations (e.g., new faces) where subtitles should be more prominent to ensure clarity.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will reduce visual clutter during familiar conversations while ensuring that critical information is always visible when needed, thus enhancing the wearer's trust in the system without compromising on real-time readability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
