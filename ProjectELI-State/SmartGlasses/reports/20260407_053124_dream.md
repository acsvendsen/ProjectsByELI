### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the clarity and stability of subtitles, making them more reliable for real-time conversations. This ensures that the wearer can trust the subtitles, which is crucial for maintaining high attractor scores in real-time subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed position rule for subtitles that keeps them at a consistent height on the display, with minimal vertical movement to avoid distracting the user. This ensures that subtitles remain visible and stable without causing visual fatigue or distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitles in a fixed position, users can focus more on the conversation rather than constantly adjusting their gaze between the speaker and the subtitles. This leads to reduced cognitive load and increased trust in the system, contributing positively to the core attractors of real-time subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a fixed position rule for subtitles at a consistent height on the display, with minimal vertical movement.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a clear local memory threshold for face/name recall, the system ensures that only recently encountered or frequently relevant entities are stored locally. This prevents the cache from becoming cluttered with infrequently used information, thereby maintaining fast lookup and reducing the risk of privacy breaches.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local memory threshold based on recency and frequency of interaction for each person's name and face. For example, store names and faces seen in the last 7 days, with frequent interactions having higher priority than infrequent ones.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will improve the overall performance by ensuring that memory is optimized for real-time recall without overwhelming the local cache. It also aligns with the core tension of balancing privacy vs. usefulness by limiting the amount of data stored locally and reducing the likelihood of retaining outdated or irrelevant information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
