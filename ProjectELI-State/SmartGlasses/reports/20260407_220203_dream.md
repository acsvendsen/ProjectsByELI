### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules to ensure they remain stable and unobtrusive, we can enhance the wearer's trust in the subtitles without causing distraction. Stable and predictable subtitle placement helps the wearer focus on the conversation rather than the display, which is crucial for a low-friction assistive experience.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: one for stable placement at the bottom of the field of view (FOV) and another for dynamic repositioning if there's visual obstruction or high confidence in speaker identification.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these placement rules will lead to a more consistent and less distracting experience, which can significantly boost trust and usability. This stability also ensures that subtitles remain accessible during quick glances and head movements, further enhancing the wearer’s ability to follow conversations in real-time.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the system's memory and recall without creating false certainty or unnecessary doubt. Clear, consistent confidence labels and short reasons provide the necessary context to make the assistive behavior reliable and trustworthy.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple yet informative confidence format: use a numeric score with a label (high, medium, low) and a short reason (e.g., "seen recently," "weak match," or "name heard once in introduction").

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear and concise confidence information, the system will build trust over time as the wearer consistently sees reliable behavior from the glasses. This trust can lead to better engagement with the assistive features and more accurate memory recall.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By setting a clear threshold, the system can prioritize frequently encountered individuals, reducing latency in recall and improving overall trust in memory assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific phone/cloud boundaries for live subtitle processing versus transcript cleanup tasks, we can ensure that subtitle generation remains fast and reliable, which directly supports the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity. This focus on subtitle quality will enhance user trust and understanding in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear rule where subtitles are processed locally on the phone to maintain low latency, while transcript cleanup tasks fall back to the cloud for more robust processing but with increased potential for delays. This separation ensures that live subtitle generation remains fast and reliable.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping real-time subtitles local and offloading heavier processing like transcript cleaning to the cloud, we can maintain high subtitle clarity and speed. This will enhance the wearer's trust in the system during critical conversations, aligning with our core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.
