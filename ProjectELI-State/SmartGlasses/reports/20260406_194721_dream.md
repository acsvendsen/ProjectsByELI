### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improved subtitle placement can enhance the wearer's ability to read subtitles quickly and accurately, reducing cognitive load and improving overall understanding in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that keeps subtitles at the bottom of the screen with minimal movement, ensuring they remain visible and readable without distracting the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By setting a stable subtitle position, we can ensure that subtitles do not flicker or move unnecessarily, which will reduce eye strain and improve the overall user experience. This stability also enhances trust in the system's reliability during live conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format, we ensure that the wearer receives clear and trustworthy signals about the reliability of the subtitles and memory support. This improves the overall trust in the system, which is crucial for daily use in noisy environments or when understanding complex conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple but effective confidence format: a numeric score combined with a label and a short reason. For example:
   - Score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"
   - Score 0.42 + "Low" + "Weak match"
   - Score 0.63 + "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction"

3. **Likely payoff**

   A well-designed confidence format will help the wearer understand the reliability of the subtitles and memory support more intuitively. This reduces cognitive load by providing clear feedback, leading to increased trust and better use of the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
