### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Subtitle placement that minimizes distraction while ensuring readability can significantly enhance the wearer's ability to focus on live conversation. By defining a fixed subtitle position, we reduce the need for constant repositioning and visual adjustments, which can disrupt the natural flow of interaction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a default subtitle placement rule at the bottom center of the field of view (FOV), with an option to move slightly upward or downward based on head tilt but never more than 10 degrees away from this baseline position.

3. **Likely payoff**

This fixed placement will ensure that subtitles remain in a consistent, easily readable location without causing distraction. The wearer can focus on the conversation while still having critical information readily available. This stability will enhance trust and reduce cognitive load during interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format to be clear and concise, we can ensure that the wearer always has a reliable indicator of the system's certainty in its memory assertions. This clarity helps build trust over time by reducing ambiguity and reinforcing the reliability of the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**

   A clear and unambiguous confidence display will enhance the wearer's trust in the system, making them more likely to rely on the smart glasses for memory support. This clarity can reduce cognitive load by providing immediate feedback on the reliability of any given piece of information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving memory cache policy enhances the trustworthiness of face/name recall by ensuring that relevant memories are kept fresh while less critical data is discarded to avoid overloading local storage.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a simple LRU (Least Recently Used) eviction rule for local phone-based face/name memory caches, keeping only the most recently interacted-with entities.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By using an LRU policy, V1 can maintain high-confidence and frequently-used memories while freeing up space for less critical information, thereby reducing the risk of corrupted recall and ensuring that important names and faces are readily accessible during interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By defining a clear boundary for what runs phone-local versus what falls back to cloud, we ensure that V1 focuses on delivering essential subtitle and memory support with minimal latency. This helps maintain trust by providing real-time assistance without overloading the battery or thermal capacity of the glasses.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a specific task boundary for live subtitle processing: all live subtitle generation should run phone-local, with no fallback to cloud under any circumstances in V1.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By keeping live subtitle processing on-device, we ensure real-time and stable subtitle delivery without draining the battery or overheating the glasses. This enhances trust and reliability, aligning closely with our core attractors of subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support ensures that the most critical information is prioritized and displayed in a manner that does not overwhelm the wearer while still providing essential assistance. This enhances the overall user experience by maintaining clarity and reducing cognitive load, which aligns with the core mission of helping the wearer understand better without creating command overhead.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear visual hierarchy rule where subtitles are given higher precedence than one-line support during busy or noisy interactions. For example, if there is an increase in background noise or multiple speakers, subtitles should remain visible and readable while one-line prompts are temporarily suppressed to avoid distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By ensuring that the most critical information (subtitles) remains prominent during high-noise or multi-person interactions, users will have a clearer understanding of what is being said, leading to improved comprehension and reduced cognitive load. This also enhances the trust in the system by showing it as a reliable aid rather than an intrusive one.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
