### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Consistent subtitle placement rules help ensure that subtitles are always visible and readable without being overly distracting. This improves the wearer's ability to follow conversations in noisy environments, enhancing their overall understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define three fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: high-confidence (always visible), medium-confidence (conditional visibility based on context), and low-confidence (temporarily hidden).

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By establishing clear rules for subtitle placement, the system can reduce visual clutter while ensuring that critical information is always accessible. This will enhance user trust by providing a reliable visual aid without overwhelming them with too much text.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining how confidence levels are displayed, we can enhance the trustworthiness of the system. A well-chosen confidence format will help the wearer understand when a name or face recognition is certain versus uncertain, which is crucial for maintaining their trust and engagement.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear, concise confidence label with a short reason. For example, use "High" + "Seen recently", "Low" + "Weak match", and "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction". This will provide the wearer with immediate context on the reliability of each recognition.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear confidence labels and reasons, we can reduce user frustration when names or faces are incorrectly recognized. This clarity will help users trust the system more, leading to better overall engagement and utility. It also aligns with the core mission of helping the wearer understand and respond appropriately in real-world interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
