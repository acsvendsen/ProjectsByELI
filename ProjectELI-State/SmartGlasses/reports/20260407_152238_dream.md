### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles remain stable and unobtrusive while providing clear visual cues for important information. This helps maintain focus during conversations and enhances trust in the system's real-time assistance without overwhelming the wearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two to three fixed subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence text (like names or key phrases), another for medium-confidence text, and a third for low-confidence text that can be placed further below the line of sight.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these fixed rules will reduce visual clutter and ensure that the most critical information is easily readable without causing distraction. This approach helps maintain a clear and focused visual hierarchy, which is crucial for the wearer's trust in the system’s real-time assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining clear phone-local versus cloud fallback boundaries for live subtitle processing, we ensure that subtitles remain fast and reliable in real-time, which is critical for maintaining trust during conversations. This focus on local processing helps manage battery and thermal constraints while ensuring that the system can still leverage cloud capabilities when necessary without compromising on the core mission of providing timely, accurate, and useful subtitles.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one task-specific phone/cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing. Specifically, set a rule where critical real-time subtitle rendering happens locally, with fallbacks to the cloud only in cases where high latency or unreliable local conditions are detected.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This decision will enable V1 to deliver fast, reliable subtitles that remain stable and accurate even under challenging environmental conditions, thereby strengthening the core mission of providing real-time assistance without breaking trust due to delays or performance issues.

4. **Immediate next probe**
