1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules across different confidence levels helps maintain a consistent user experience, reducing cognitive load and enhancing the wearer's ability to follow conversations smoothly without being distracted by subtitles that move or appear at inappropriate times.

   Define 2 to 3 subtitle placement rules for different confidence states: high confidence (score > 0.8), medium confidence (score 0.5-0.8), and low confidence (score < 0.5).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By establishing clear, stable placement rules based on confidence levels, the system can provide more reliable subtitle support, reducing user frustration and improving overall understanding in noisy or complex environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
