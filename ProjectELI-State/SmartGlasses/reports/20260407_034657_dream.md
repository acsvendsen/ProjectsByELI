### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain clear, stable, and unobtrusive during live conversations. By optimizing subtitle position and stability, we can enhance the wearer's trust in the system without overwhelming their visual field or causing distractions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two fixed subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence speakers and another for lower-confidence situations. For high-confidence speakers, place subtitles at a fixed position slightly below the speaker’s face; for lower-confidence situations, allow slight adjustments to follow the speaker's movement but maintain stability.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing specific subtitle placement rules, we can ensure that the subtitles are always in a clear and unobtrusive position, enhancing the wearer's ability to understand the conversation while minimizing distraction. This will directly improve the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity and reduce cognitive load on the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
