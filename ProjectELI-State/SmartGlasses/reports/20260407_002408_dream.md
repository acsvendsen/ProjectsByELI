### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement enhances the wearer's trust in the system, making live subtitles more reliable and easier to follow. Stable placement prevents distractions and ensures that subtitles remain visible and readable without shifting or flickering, which is crucial for maintaining focus during conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed position for the subtitles at the bottom of the display screen, slightly above the horizon line, with minor adjustments for gaze direction but no significant repositioning unless there's a clear visual cue (like text overlap).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By maintaining a consistent subtitle placement, users will experience less distraction and increased trust in the system. This stability reduces cognitive load and ensures that subtitles remain visible even during rapid head movements or conversations with multiple speakers.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the fixed position for subtitles at the bottom of the display screen, slightly above the horizon line.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving confidence display in V1 will enhance trust by ensuring that subtitles are presented with clear and reliable certainty signals. This is crucial for the wearer to understand when the system is certain about its recognition and when it is less confident, thus maintaining their faith in the system's accuracy.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a confidence format that uses a numeric score, label, and short reason. For example, "score 0.85 + 'High' + 'Seen recently';" this clear and concise format will allow users to quickly understand the reliability of each subtitle without overwhelming them with too much detail.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly displaying confidence scores and reasons, the system can maintain or even increase user trust during live conversations. This transparency ensures that the wearer is aware when they should be more cautious about a statement’s accuracy, thereby enhancing their overall experience and reliance on the glasses for understanding.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary between what runs on the phone locally and what falls back to cloud processing, we ensure that real-time subtitle clarity is maintained without unnecessary latency or data usage. This helps in preserving trust by delivering timely and accurate subtitles.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one specific task for which the phone should handle local processing and only fallback to the cloud when absolutely necessary. For example, live subtitle processing can be handled entirely on the phone, while more complex memory recall or archive syncing may require occasional cloud assistance.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping real-time subtitle processing localized, we reduce latency, battery usage, and thermal stress, ensuring that subtitles remain clear and reliable in noisy environments. This enhances trust and daily-use value by making the product more dependable without compromising on speed or clarity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear visual hierarchy that prioritizes subtitles over one-line prompts, we ensure that the wearer's primary focus remains on understanding and interacting with the conversation in real-time. This strengthens the core mission of making the wearer more present and informed during live interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a subtitle-vs-one-line priority rule where subtitles always take precedence over one-line prompts, but one-line prompts are displayed when needed to provide context or support without overwhelming the user.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This visual hierarchy will ensure that the wearer can focus on understanding the conversation through clear and stable subtitles while still receiving essential contextual information from brief one-line prompts. The payoff is improved real-time understanding, reduced cognitive load, and a more seamless user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
