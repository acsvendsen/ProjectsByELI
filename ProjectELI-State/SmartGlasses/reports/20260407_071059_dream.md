### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the overall user experience by ensuring that subtitles remain clear and readable without becoming distracting. This is crucial for maintaining trust in real-time conversations, which aligns with the primary attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity."

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence speech (where subtitles are displayed prominently but not obstructive) and another for low-confidence speech (where subtitles are less prominent to avoid visual distraction).

3. **Likely payoff**
   By implementing these rules, the glasses can provide more reliable and non-intrusive subtitle support during conversations. This will help in maintaining the wearer's focus on the conversation while ensuring that important information is not missed.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format in V1 will enhance trust by clearly indicating the certainty level of memory recall or subtitle placement. This will help the wearer understand when to rely on the system and when to maintain skepticism, thereby reducing social awkwardness and increasing overall reliability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence format that combines a numeric score with a label and a short reason for its certainty level. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"` or `score 0.42 + "Low" + "Weak match"`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By using a clear, concise confidence format, the system will maintain high trust levels among wearers who rely on accurate and reliable information. This will also help in reducing false positives and negatives, ensuring that the wearer can make informed decisions based on the system's output.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a local cache threshold for name recall, we ensure that frequently encountered faces and names are stored on the phone for faster lookup and more reliable memory support without compromising privacy or battery life.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific threshold in seconds of recency or number of encounters before a face/name is considered frequent enough to be cached locally.

3. **Likely payoff**

   The local cache will reduce latency for frequently encountered names and faces, leading to more reliable memory support and faster subtitle placement during live interactions. This improves the core attractor of "memory_trust" while maintaining privacy and battery constraints.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a recency threshold of 5 seconds for caching frequently encountered faces and names locally.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By clearly defining what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud for live subtitle processing, we can ensure that subtitles are generated quickly and accurately without overloading the device's battery or thermal limits. This decision helps maintain real-time clarity, which is crucial for the wearer’s trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific rule: For live subtitle processing, any speech input that requires less than 500ms of latency should be processed locally on the phone. All other inputs will fall back to cloud processing.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By handling short-latency subtitle requests locally, we can ensure that subtitles remain clear and timely. This reduces battery drain from constant network activity and helps maintain the wearer’s trust in the system by providing reliable real-time assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support, we ensure that the most critical information (subtitles) remains prominent while still allowing useful but less urgent prompts to be displayed without overwhelming the wearer. This balance enhances the core mission of providing reliable subtitle clarity while ensuring low-friction assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a simple visual precedence rule: subtitles always take priority over one-line support, and one-line support is suppressed when subtitles are active but dimmed or moved to an auxiliary location if space allows.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This visual hierarchy will enhance the wearer's ability to focus on critical information during conversations, reducing cognitive load and improving overall understanding without creating distracting UI elements that could reduce trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
