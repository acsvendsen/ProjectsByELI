### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain clear, stable, and unobtrusive during live conversations. This enhances trust in the system by making the subtitles easy to read and understand without causing distraction or discomfort.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence speakers (e.g., known faces or frequently encountered names) and another for lower-confidence scenarios (e.g., less familiar voices or background noise). This allows the system to adjust its visibility based on certainty, reducing distraction while maintaining readability.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By optimizing subtitle placement, the system will be more usable in a wider range of environments, reducing cognitive load during conversations and improving overall user satisfaction. The ability to quickly locate and understand subtitles will also enhance trust in the system's reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the system's memory and support in real-world interactions, which is crucial for building trust and reliability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple yet clear confidence label plus short reason format to describe certainty and source. For example: score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"; score 0.42 + "Low" + "Weak match"; score 0.63 + "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction".

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly indicating the certainty and source of memory support, the wearer can better understand when to trust the system's assistance, leading to more effective interactions and reduced cognitive load.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the local cache size and eviction policy for face/name memory ensures that relevant contextual information is readily available to the wearer without overwhelming their cognitive load. This helps in maintaining trust by providing timely and accurate support, while also ensuring efficient use of battery and thermal resources.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple two-tier local cache policy: a primary cache with higher hit thresholds for frequently encountered faces/names and a secondary cache with lower thresholds for less frequent interactions to minimize memory churn.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this tiered caching strategy, the system will maintain a balance between having enough contextual information available and keeping the memory footprint manageable. This improves the reliability of face/name recall without significantly impacting battery life or thermal management.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one local-cache size limit for frequently encountered faces/names that balances memory usage with quick lookup times.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing remains phone-local helps maintain real-time performance, which is critical for trust and usability in noisy environments. By keeping subtitle processing on the phone, we can minimize latency and ensure subtitles are displayed with minimal delay, reinforcing their reliability and clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear threshold where live subtitle processing should remain local to the phone. For example, set a rule that any text that needs immediate display for clarity purposes (e.g., within 100 milliseconds of transcription) is processed locally on the phone.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitle processing phone-local, we can achieve near-real-time subtitle delivery, which is essential for trust and understanding in live conversations. This will help maintain high-quality, reliable subtitles that do not introduce lag or delay, thereby enhancing the wearer's ability to follow spoken content.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.
