### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a consistent subtitle placement rule that remains stable across different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are always readable and do not distract from the conversation. This will help maintain trust in the system by providing clear and reliable assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement rules for different confidence states: high (0.8+), medium (0.5-0.8), and low (<0.5). For each state, specify a fixed position on the screen that minimizes visual disruption while ensuring subtitles are always visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing consistent subtitle placement rules, we can improve user trust in the system's reliability. This will help ensure that the wearer does not miss important information and can focus more on the conversation at hand. Stable placement rules also reduce cognitive load by minimizing the need to constantly adjust attention between the conversation and subtitles.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
