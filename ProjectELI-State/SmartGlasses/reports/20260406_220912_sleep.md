# Consolidated Insights for SmartGlasses Project

## Strengthening Signals

### Core Attractors
- **Subtitle Clarity**: Enhance real-time subtitle clarity, ensuring they remain clear, fast, and stable during live conversations.
- **Memory Support Trustworthiness**: Improve the reliability of name and face recall to prevent any false certainty or corrupted recall.
- **Low-Friction Assistance**: Optimize one-line prompts for quick, easy-to-understand support without overwhelming the wearer.

### Core Tensions
- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Strive to provide useful assistive behavior while maintaining high privacy standards.
- **Latency vs Richness**: Balance between timely responses and richer processing capabilities that might introduce delays.
- **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**: Ensure the display is socially acceptable yet readable in noisy environments.

### Core Deepening
- Focus on improving subtitle quality, memory trustworthiness, and visual UX to enhance daily-use value without adding unnecessary complexity.

## Weakening Signals

### Interaction Constraints
- V1 interaction should remain strictly frame-touch only; avoid introducing voice commands or other complex interactions for now.
- Heavy reliance on phone processing with minimal local computation is essential to manage battery and thermal constraints.

### Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy
- Optimize subtitle placement without becoming too distracting, and ensure visual hierarchy supports fast reading and low cognitive load.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive

### Drift Risks
- Avoid evolving the project into a gimmick or adding broad features before core functionalities are solidified.
- Do not allow complexity to overshadow the core mission of providing essential, discreet assistance.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating

### Confidence Display
- Consider different formats for confidence display (label only, label + short reason) and test their effectiveness on trust and user satisfaction.

## Concrete Tensions Around Specific Areas

1. **Subtitle Quality**:
   - Ensure subtitles are clear, stable, and trusted in noisy environments.
   
2. **Memory Reinforcement**:
   - Develop effective strategies for remembering new faces and names without creating false certainty or privacy issues.

3. **Fast Lookup vs Privacy**:
   - Balance the need for quick memory recall with maintaining user privacy to ensure trust is not compromised.

4. **Visual UX**:
   - Optimize subtitle placement and visual hierarchy to provide clear, unobtrusive support that does not distract from the wearer’s natural interaction.

## No Fluff

- Focus on concrete improvements in subtitle clarity, name and face recall accuracy, one-line prompt effectiveness, and privacy management.
- Surface real-world constraints early to avoid optimizing for impossible or low-value directions.
