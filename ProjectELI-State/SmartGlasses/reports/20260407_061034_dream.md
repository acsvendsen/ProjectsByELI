### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels will enhance the wearer's understanding and trust in the system. By ensuring subtitles are placed in a manner that does not distract but still provides clear, relevant information, we can maintain a balance between usability and non-intrusiveness.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 2 to 3 subtitle placement rules for different confidence states: high (0.9+), medium (0.6-0.8), and low (0.4-0.5) confidence levels. For high-confidence subtitles, place them directly in the center of view; for medium-confidence, place them slightly offset but still visible; for low-confidence, show them at the bottom corner.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will ensure that the wearer can easily read subtitles without being distracted by their placement, especially when they are unsure about the accuracy of the transcriptions. It will also help in maintaining a consistent and intuitive user experience across different confidence levels.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
