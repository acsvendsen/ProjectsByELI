## Concrete Tensions Around Core Areas
### Subtitle Quality vs Visual UX
- The balance between ensuring subtitles are clear and prominent enough for readability versus not overwhelming the wearer with too much text.
  
### Confidence Trust vs Automatic Memory Behavior
- Ensuring that confidence displays build trust without overwhelming the user, while automatic memory behavior provides accurate and timely recall of names and faces.

### Fast Lookup vs Privacy
- Striking a balance between quickly accessing stored information about people (for face/name recall) and respecting privacy concerns by not storing unnecessary or sensitive data.
