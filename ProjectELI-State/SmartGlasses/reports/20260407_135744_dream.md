### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining clear subtitle placement rules that ensure subtitles remain stable, readable, and unobtrusive, we can enhance the wearer's trust in the system. Consistent and predictable subtitle placement helps maintain a reliable visual cue without distracting from the conversation.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states: 
   - High-confidence subtitles should be placed at the bottom of the screen with minimal animation.
   - Low-confidence subtitles should be placed in a slightly less prominent area, perhaps on the side or partially obscured by text boxes.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will lead to more stable and unobtrusive subtitle delivery, reducing cognitive load and increasing trust. The wearer can rely on the system's consistency without constant distraction from moving or flickering text.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
