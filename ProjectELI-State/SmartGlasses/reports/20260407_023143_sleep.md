# Consolidation of Recent Signals

## Strengthening Signals

- **Subtitle Clarity**: The core mission emphasizes maintaining real-time subtitle clarity that is readable, stable, and trustworthy in live conversation.
- **Trustworthy Memory Support**: Ensuring names, faces, and prior context are remembered accurately without overconfidence or false recall.
- **Low-Friction Assistance**: Delivering concise, one-line support to enhance understanding while minimizing interaction overhead.

## Weakening Signals

- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: The tension of providing helpful assistive behavior while respecting privacy is becoming less prominent as the project focuses more on core functionality.
- **Always-On Sensing vs Battery and Thermals**: The drive for always-on features is being adjusted to minimize battery strain, aligning with V1's frame-touch-only interaction constraint.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive

- **Real-Time Subtitle Clarity vs Battery Drain**: There remains a critical tension between providing timely, high-quality subtitles and avoiding excessive battery consumption.
- **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**: The visual design must balance the need for subtlety with the requirement to remain readable in noisy environments.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating

- **Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy**: Further exploration of subtitle placement and visual hierarchy could enhance readability without becoming distracting, though this is currently not a top priority.
- **Memory Reinforcement and Confidence Display**: Investigating more advanced methods for reinforcing memory and improving confidence display mechanisms remains important but has been on the back burner.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Areas

1. **Subtitle Quality**:
   - **Real-time Clarity**: Ensure subtitles remain clear, stable, and trusted during live conversations.
   - **Latency vs Richness**: Balance between fast, reliable subtitle delivery and richer contextual information that might delay response times.

2. **Memory Trust**:
   - **Automatic Memory Support vs Confidence and Correction Safety**: Strive for a balance where memory support is accurate but not overly certain to avoid false recall.

3. **Low-Friction Assistance**:
   - **Interaction Mode**: Focus on frame-touch interactions while exploring minimal UI elements that do not disrupt the wearer's experience.
   - **Prompt Display**: Ensure one-line prompts are visible and helpful without overloading the user interface.

4. **Privacy vs Usefulness**:
   - **Data Handling**: Prioritize data minimization and transparency to maintain trust without compromising on core functionality.

5. **Battery and Thermals**:
   - **Phone-First Processing**: Continue to leverage phone processing for heavy tasks while minimizing thermal and battery strain.
   - **Always-On Sensing**: Manage always-on features with V1's frame-touch-only interaction constraint in mind.

6. **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**:
   - **Subtitle Placement**: Find a balance where subtitles are visible but not overly intrusive, especially during movement or in noisy environments.
   - **Visual Hierarchy**: Ensure the visual hierarchy supports quick reading and understanding while maintaining social acceptability.

## Open Questions

- **Subtitle Placement**: What is the best V1 split between glasses, phone, and cloud?
- **Confidence Display**: How should confidence be represented: numeric score, label, and short reason?
- **Memory/Cache Policy**: How should face/name memory be stored and reinforced for fast lookup and trust?
- **Phone/Cloud Boundary**: What is the best balance between local processing on glasses and cloud support?
- **Visual Hierarchy**: How can subtitles and one-line prompts coexist without overloading the wearer?

## Key Decisions

- **Subtitle Clarity**: Ensure real-time, readable, stable subtitles.
- **Memory Trust**: Implement accurate memory support without overconfidence.
- **Low-Friction Assistance**: Provide concise, helpful prompts through minimal UI.

By focusing on these areas, the project can continue to strengthen its core mission while addressing critical tensions and maintaining a balance between usability, privacy, and functionality.
