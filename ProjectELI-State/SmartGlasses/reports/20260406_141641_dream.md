### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear subtitle placement rule based on confidence levels, we ensure that the most important information is always visible while minimizing distractions. This helps maintain focus and trust in the system's ability to provide accurate and timely assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple subtitle placement rule: For high-confidence matches (e.g., >0.85 confidence score), display subtitles directly below the speaker; for medium-confidence matches (e.g., 0.6 to 0.85), show them above or beside the speaker but avoid direct overlap; for low-confidence matches, limit their duration and visibility.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach reduces visual clutter while ensuring that critical information is always clear and prominent. It also aligns with real-world expectations, where high-confidence matches are more urgent and require immediate attention. This can enhance the overall user experience by balancing clarity with minimal distraction.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
