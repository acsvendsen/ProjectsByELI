## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on creating discreet, context-aware glasses with live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The current version (V1) adheres strictly to frame-touch interactions and minimizes local hardware processing while relying heavily on the phone for most heavy computation tasks.
