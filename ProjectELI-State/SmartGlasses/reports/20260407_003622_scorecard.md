## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on core deepening while maintaining balance in key tensions such as privacy vs usefulness and discreet UX vs visual clarity. The hardware stack must remain lightweight, ensuring the glasses are socially acceptable and do not rely heavily on on-glasses compute. Progress against these goals is being assessed through structured signals and recent cycle reports.
