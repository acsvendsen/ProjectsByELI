### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring consistent subtitle placement rules in V1 helps maintain a stable and predictable user experience, which is crucial for trust and reliability. Stable subtitle placement reduces cognitive load by making the interface more intuitive and less distracting, allowing the wearer to focus on the conversation.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence speaker names and another for lower-confidence non-speaker mentions. This separation ensures that critical information is always visible while minimizing distractions from less certain content.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing stable and predictable subtitle placement, the system will reduce cognitive load and maintain a more natural flow of conversation. Users will be able to trust the subtitles without constantly adjusting their gaze, leading to a smoother and more integrated assistive experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By improving how confidence is displayed, we can enhance trust in both subtitle accuracy and memory recall. Clear and consistent confidence indicators will help the wearer understand when information is highly certain versus less reliable, thus reducing cognitive load and increasing overall user trust.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple yet informative confidence format that combines a numeric score with a label and short reason. For example, "High: Seen recently" for well-remembered names or "Low: Weak match" for less certain identifications.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A clear confidence format will help the wearer interpret information more accurately, reducing misunderstandings and enhancing overall trust in the system. This can lead to more effective use of memory support and subtitles, improving the core mission of aiding understanding in real-world interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing is handled locally on the phone while offloading less time-critical tasks like transcript cleanup to the cloud helps maintain real-time responsiveness and trust in subtitles, which is crucial for the wearer's understanding in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific boundary where live subtitle processing runs exclusively on the phone without falling back to the cloud unless absolutely necessary, such as when local resources are strained or there’s an explicit user action that triggers remote processing.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that subtitles remain fast and stable in real-time conversations, reducing latency issues and maintaining high trust levels. It also allows for more efficient use of limited thermal and battery resources on the glasses.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a rule where live subtitle generation runs exclusively on the phone without falling back to the cloud, except when local resources are exhausted.
