## Project Summary
The project is focusing on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through smart glasses with a strong emphasis on live subtitles, face/name recall, and conversation memory. The current focus areas include optimizing subtitle placement and confidence display, defining memory caching policies, maintaining the phone-first architecture, and ensuring low-friction interactions. Real-world constraints such as battery life and social acceptability are being managed through careful design decisions.
