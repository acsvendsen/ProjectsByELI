### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules will enhance the stability and readability of subtitles, ensuring they remain clear and trustworthy in live conversations. This is crucial for maintaining the wearer's trust and understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define three fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: 
   - High Confidence: Fixed position at the bottom right corner.
   - Medium Confidence: Centered but slightly below center.
   - Low Confidence: Top left with a small, subtle animation to draw attention without distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By defining clear and consistent subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure subtitles are always visible and understandable, reducing cognitive load and maintaining trust in the assistant's reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

By defining a clear boundary between what stays phone-local and what falls back to cloud for live subtitle processing, we ensure that critical subtitle data remains on-device, reducing latency and ensuring real-time accuracy. This helps maintain the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity while respecting the constraint pressure from battery and thermal considerations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a local cache policy where subtitle text and associated confidence objects are stored locally for a fixed duration before falling back to cloud. This involves setting a time threshold for how long subtitles should remain on-device, with explicit frame touches triggering cloud fallback only when necessary.

3. **Likely payoff**

This approach ensures that critical subtitles are available instantly without relying on potentially slow network connections. It also helps in maintaining the real-time clarity and stability of subtitles, which is essential for the wearer's trust and daily-use value. By keeping this core functionality local, we can avoid latency issues and provide a more reliable user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.
