### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring consistent subtitle placement helps maintain a stable and predictable user experience, reducing cognitive load and visual distractions. This stability is crucial for building trust and ensuring that subtitles are always easily readable, which aligns with the core mission of making real-time communication clearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule where subtitles appear at the bottom center of the frame, regardless of head or gaze position, but adjust text size and spacing to optimize readability without overlapping.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this fixed placement rule, users will experience more consistent and predictable subtitle visibility, which can enhance their confidence in relying on the smart glasses for understanding conversations. This stability helps maintain a discreet and unobtrusive design while ensuring that subtitles remain easily readable even during rapid head movements or when focusing on other objects.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

4. **Immediate next probe**
