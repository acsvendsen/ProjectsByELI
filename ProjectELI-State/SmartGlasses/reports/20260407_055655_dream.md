### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format, we can ensure that the wearer receives clear and trustworthy information about the reliability of the displayed subtitles and memory support. This enhances trust by providing honest feedback, which is crucial for maintaining the system's utility in real-world interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a new confidence label format: "score [0-1] + 'Confidence' + (Low, Medium, High)". For example, "0.84 Confidence High" or "0.35 Confidence Low". This format clearly communicates the level of certainty to the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A clear and consistent confidence display will help the wearer understand the reliability of the subtitles and memory support. This clarity can significantly reduce cognitive friction by ensuring that the wearer is aware when they should pay more attention or when the system is less certain. It also reinforces trust in the system, making it more likely to be used regularly.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the confidence score presentation format as "score [0-1] + 'Confidence' + (Low, Medium, High)", such as "0.84 Confidence High" or "0.35 Confidence Low".
