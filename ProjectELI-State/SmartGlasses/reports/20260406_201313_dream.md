### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By defining a consistent subtitle placement rule that prioritizes readability and stability, the system ensures that subtitles are always visible and clear in noisy environments, thereby enhancing the wearer's trust in real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a fixed subtitle placement mode that places subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view (FOV), with a slight offset to avoid visual overlap but remain within the natural gaze range.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - A consistent and unobtrusive subtitle placement will reduce eye strain, improve readability, and maintain focus on the conversation. This ensures that subtitles are always accessible without distracting the wearer's attention from the speaker or context.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

3. **Likely payoff**
