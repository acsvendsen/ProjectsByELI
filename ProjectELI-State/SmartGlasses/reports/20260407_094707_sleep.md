# Recent Consolidation for SmartGlasses Project

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Clarity**: Continuously improve subtitle quality to ensure they remain clear, fast, and stable enough for live conversation.
- **Memory Trust**: Enhance memory support to accurately remember names, faces, and context without overconfident or incorrect recall.
- **Low-Friction Assistance**: Refine one-line prompts to provide helpful assistance in a non-disruptive manner.

## Weakening Signals
- **Privacy vs. Usefulness**: Reduce the risk of privacy invasion while maintaining useful assistive behavior. Focus on clear, transparent interactions that respect user data.
- **Latency vs. Richness**: Prioritize timely responses over richer but slower processing. Ensure real-time subtitle clarity and memory recall are prioritized.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Discreet UX vs. Visual Clarity**: Strive for a balance where the glasses remain socially acceptable while ensuring subtitles and prompts are readable in noisy environments.
- **Always-On Sensing vs. Battery and Thermals**: Limit always-on features to reduce battery drain and thermal issues, focusing on essential real-time functions.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy**: Revisit the subtitle placement and visual hierarchy to ensure they remain unobtrusive yet clear for the wearer.
- **Memory/Cache Policy**: Evaluate local caching strategies to balance fast lookup with privacy concerns.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Mission Components
1. **Subtitle Quality, Memory Reinforcement, and Fast Lookup**:
   - Ensure subtitles are high-quality, memory is reinforced through interactions, and fast lookups provide timely support.
2. **Privacy, Battery, Latency, and Usability**:
   - Surface real-world constraints early to avoid wasting time on impossible optimizations.

## Open Questions
- **Subtitle Placement**: Determine the most usable subtitle placement without becoming distracting.
- **Confidence Display**: Decide on the best confidence format (label only, label + short reason, or score + label) that builds trust.
