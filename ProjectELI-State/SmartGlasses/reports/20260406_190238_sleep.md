- **Subtitle Clarity** (Core Attractor): Ensure subtitles remain clear, fast, and stable enough to maintain trust during live conversations.
- **Memory Trust** (Core Attractor): Enhance the reliability of memory support by remembering names and faces without overpromising or creating misleading recall.

## Weakening Signals
- **Low-Friction Assistance** (Core Attractor): Consider reducing the number of one-line prompts to avoid overwhelming the user with too much assistive information, which can detract from natural interaction.

- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: The system must balance providing useful assistance without compromising privacy.
- **Latency vs Richness**: Optimize for timely responses while avoiding unnecessary complexity and processing delays.
