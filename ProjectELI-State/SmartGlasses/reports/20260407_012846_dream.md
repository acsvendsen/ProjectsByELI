### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a fixed subtitle placement rule that is stable and unambiguous, we can reduce visual distraction while ensuring the wearer always has a clear, readable display of the subtitles. This will help maintain trust in the system by providing consistent, reliable information without overwhelming the user.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple, default fixed subtitle placement rule that positions subtitles at the bottom center of the glasses frame. This avoids dynamic adjustments based on confidence or gaze direction, which can be distracting and less reliable in noisy environments.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A consistent subtitle placement will reduce visual clutter and distraction during conversations, making it easier for the wearer to focus on both the spoken words and their surroundings. This stability also allows users to build trust in the system by having a predictable and reliable display experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one fixed subtitle placement rule at the bottom center of the glasses frame for V1.
