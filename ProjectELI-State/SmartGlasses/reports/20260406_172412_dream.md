### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain trust by providing consistent, predictable reading experiences for the wearer. Stable placement ensures that subtitles do not move erratically or distract from the conversation, thereby enhancing the user's ability to focus on the context and flow of live interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence situations (like known speakers) where subtitles are placed prominently but stably, and another for low-confidence situations (like new or unfamiliar voices) where subtitles are slightly lower but still visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing distinct visual zones for different confidence levels, the system can better manage distractions while maintaining clarity in critical moments. This enhances user trust by ensuring that important information is easily readable without being overly intrusive.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
