## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 project is focused on creating a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. Key constraints include minimal on-glasses compute to ensure lightweight design and social acceptability, as well as near-real-time subtitle quality and clear confidence displays.
