# Consolidated Signals for SmartGlasses Project

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Clarity**: The system should prioritize real-time subtitle clarity, ensuring subtitles remain readable, stable, and trustworthy during live conversations (high confidence).
- **Memory Support Trustworthiness**: Enhance the reliability of name and face memory support to ensure accurate recall without creating false certainty or misleading the wearer.
- **Low-Friction Assistance**: Optimize for one-line prompts that provide just enough context without overwhelming the user, maintaining a low-friction interaction experience.

## Weakening Signals
- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: The project should continue to balance privacy concerns with the utility of assistive features. Avoid creating invasive or misleading behavior.
- **Latency vs Richness**: Prioritize timely responses over richer but slower processing solutions that might sacrifice real-time trustworthiness.
- **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**: Strive for a display design that remains socially acceptable while ensuring subtitles and prompts are readable in motion and noise.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Frame-Touch Only V1 Interaction**: Ensure all interaction designs stay within the frame-touch constraint, avoiding voice commands or complex command surfaces.
- **Phone-First Runtime**: Focus on leveraging the phone for heavy processing while keeping early hardware simple. Limit cloud reliance to clearly justified cases.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy**: Re-examine subtitle placement strategies to find a balance between readability and minimal distraction, especially in noisy environments.
- **Confidence Display Format**: Consider the most effective way to represent confidence (e.g., label only, label + short reason) to enhance trust without overloading the user.

## Concrete Tensions Around
1. **Subtitle Quality**:
   - Subtitle placement should ensure they are easily readable and do not distract from conversation.
2. **Confidence Trust**:
   - Confidence display should be clear and trustworthy to build wearer confidence in the system’s reliability.
3. **Automatic Memory Behavior vs Explicit Remember-Touch Behavior**:
   - Balance automatic memory reinforcement with explicit remember-touch interactions, ensuring both methods enhance trust without overwhelming the user.
4. **Fast Lookup Requirements**:
   - Local caching of face/name data should be optimized for fast lookup while respecting privacy and battery constraints.

## Open Questions
- **Subtitle Placement**: Determine the best placement strategy to ensure subtitles are readable yet unobtrusive.
- **Confidence Display**: Evaluate different confidence formats (label only, label + short reason) to find the most effective and least intrusive method.
- **Memory/Cache Policy**: Decide on an appropriate level of local caching for face/name data that balances trust with privacy and battery constraints.

## Summary
The project should focus on enhancing subtitle clarity, memory support reliability, and low-friction assistance while maintaining core constraints like frame-touch interaction, phone-first processing, and balanced privacy vs. usefulness tradeoffs. Open questions around subtitle placement and confidence display need further exploration to ensure the system remains both useful and user-friendly.
