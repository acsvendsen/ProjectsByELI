# Consolidation of Recent Project Signals

## Strengthening Signals

### Core Attractors
- **Subtitle Clarity**: Continuously improve the real-time subtitle quality to ensure they remain readable, stable, and trustworthy in live conversations.
- **Memory Trust**: Enhance memory support for names and faces, ensuring that the system neither overconfidently recalls information nor incorrectly remembers data.

### Tensions
- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Strive to balance privacy concerns with the need for useful assistance. Ensure that the system does not silently collect or store more data than necessary.
- **Latency vs Richness**: Optimize for timely, reliable responses while maintaining a level of detail and richness that adds real value without causing delays.

### Core Deepening
- Focus on improving subtitle quality, memory trust, and visual UX. Avoid broad feature expansion unless it directly supports these core areas.

## Weakening Signals

### Non-negotiables
- **Early Hardware Simplicity**: Reduce reliance on heavy on-glasses computation to preserve battery life and maintain social acceptability.
- **User Interaction**: Limit interactions to frame touches for now, avoiding voice commands in V1.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive

### Tensions
- Ensure that the system remains low-friction but still provides meaningful assistance. Avoid creating an interface that feels intrusive or burdensome.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating

### Subtitle Placement and Visual Clarity
- Revisit subtitle placement strategies to ensure they remain unobtrusive yet effective in noisy environments. Explore different display formats for one-line prompts without overwhelming the wearer.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Areas

### Subtitle Quality, Confidence Trust, Automatic New-Person Memory, Memory Reinforcement, Fast Lookup, Privacy, Latency, Battery, and Visual UX
- **Subtitle Quality**: Ensure subtitles are clear and stable. Avoid breaking trust by having them lag or appear grainy.
- **Confidence Display**: Use a label + short reason format to display confidence levels clearly and accurately.
- **Automatic New-Person Memory**: Reinforce memory for new faces and names over time, ensuring they become reliable without feeling invasive.
- **Memory Reinforcement**: Prioritize fast lookup by reinforcing frequently accessed memories. Avoid storing unnecessary information.
- **Battery Management**: Optimize power consumption to prevent overheating and extend battery life. This is critical in V1 where on-glasses compute is minimal.

## Reality Check

### Early Limits
- Surface real-world constraints early, including hardware, battery, thermal, latency, privacy, and usability limits. Ensure that the project does not waste time optimizing impossible or low-value directions.

## Open Questions

### Subtitle Placement, Confidence Display, Memory/Cache Policy, Phone/Cloud Boundary, Visual Hierarchy
- **Subtitle Placement**: Determine the best placement for subtitles to balance readability with non-distracting design.
- **Confidence Display**: Decide on the format (label + short reason) and how it impacts the overall UX.
- **Memory/Cache Policy**: Define local caching strategies to ensure fast lookup while maintaining privacy.
- **Phone/Cloud Boundary**: Clarify when cloud processing is necessary and when it can be avoided in V1.
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Design a visual hierarchy that supports clear, unobtrusive information display.
