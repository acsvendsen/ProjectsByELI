## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on creating a discreet, context-aware personal assistant that provides real-time subtitles, memory support, and one-line prompts through frame-touch interactions. The project remains constrained by hardware limitations such as battery and thermal pressure, aiming to keep V1 lightweight and socially acceptable while maintaining core mission goals.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The project is adhering to the hardware stack constraints by focusing on minimal on-glasses computation and keeping the glasses simple. Frame-touch interaction ensures low hardware strain and minimal battery drain.
- next focus: Evaluate the impact of different subtitle placements and ensure they do not exceed battery limits while maintaining readability.
