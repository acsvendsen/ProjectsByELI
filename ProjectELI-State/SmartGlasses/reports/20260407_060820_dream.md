### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining fixed subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are always readable and do not distract from conversation flow. This stability improves trust as users learn to rely on consistent visual cues.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence (0.8+), one for medium-confidence (0.4-0.79), and one for low-confidence (below 0.35). Each rule specifies a fixed position or priority order in the visual hierarchy.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will enhance subtitle readability by ensuring they do not move around unpredictably, thereby reducing cognitive load and increasing trust. Users will be able to focus more on the conversation rather than constantly adjusting their gaze between faces and subtitles.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving confidence display formats helps ensure that the wearer has a clear understanding of the system's certainty, which is crucial for building trust. A well-designed confidence format can help reduce cognitive load by providing immediate feedback on the reliability of subtitles and memory support.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define one specific confidence format candidate that combines a numeric score with a label and short reason. For example: "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'; score 0.42 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'; score 0.63 + 'Medium' + 'Name heard once in introduction'".

3. **Likely payoff**

This format will provide the wearer with a clear and concise indication of the system's certainty, which can help them understand when to trust the subtitles or memory support more confidently. It also helps in maintaining transparency and reducing the risk of over-reliance on the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

This balance improves daily-use value by providing reliable support while maintaining battery efficiency.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific threshold in seconds or frames where, if a face or name is seen multiple times within this period, they are automatically linked to an existing memory object with high confidence.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will enhance the reliability of new-person recall by ensuring that frequent sightings lead to strong, accurate memories without unnecessary battery drain from continuous updates and lookups.

4. **Immediate next probe**
