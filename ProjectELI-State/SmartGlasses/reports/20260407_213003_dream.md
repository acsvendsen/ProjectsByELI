### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement helps maintain trust in real-time subtitles. By providing consistent and reliable subtitle positions, the system can ensure that the wearer always knows where to look for important information, reducing cognitive load and improving overall understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule based on visual hierarchy principles, such as placing subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view with minimal overlap between lines.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A stable subtitle placement rule will help reduce eye strain and cognitive load for the wearer, making real-time subtitles more effective and less distracting. This can lead to increased trust in the system’s ability to provide timely and accurate information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format ensures that the wearer receives clear, trustworthy cues about the certainty of the information being presented. This helps in maintaining trust and reducing cognitive load by providing easily understandable feedback.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a new confidence object format using a numeric score with a label and short reason: e.g., "score 0.85 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'".

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly communicating the certainty of each piece of information, users will be more confident in relying on the system's assistance, leading to higher trust and better overall user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving memory cache thresholds ensures that frequently encountered faces and names remain top-of-mind for the wearer, enhancing trust in name recall during conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a local cache threshold of 5 to 7 recent face/name encounters before they are considered cold and less likely to be recalled without explicit reinforcement.

3. **Likely payoff**

A well-defined cache threshold will reduce the likelihood of missed name references due to cold memories, thereby improving overall memory trust and conversation flow.

4. **Immediate next probe**

Define a local cache threshold of 6 recent face/name encounters before they are considered cold and less likely to be recalled without explicit reinforcement.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that subtitle placement remains stable and readable while minimizing distraction is crucial for maintaining the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles. By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that high-confidence subtitles are more prominent while lower-confidence ones are less intrusive.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one where high-confidence subtitles are placed prominently at the bottom of the field of view and another where low-confidence subtitles are placed in a smaller, more discrete area or as tooltips only when touched.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping high-confidence subtitles prominent and reducing the visual impact of lower-confidence ones, we can maintain clarity without overwhelming the wearer's attention, thereby enhancing the overall effectiveness of real-time subtitles.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement stability and visual hierarchy in V1 will enhance the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles, making them more readable and less distracting. This will reinforce the core attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity" by ensuring that critical information is presented clearly and consistently.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a single static subtitle placement rule that prioritizes top-center for high-confidence text, with slight adjustments to avoid overlapping on-screen elements like one-line prompts or navigation controls. This rule will be applied universally across all frames without repositioning based on motion or confidence changes.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By establishing a clear and stable visual hierarchy, the glasses will reduce cognitive load for the wearer, allowing them to focus more on the conversation itself rather than constantly adjusting their gaze between subtitles and one-line prompts. This improved readability and consistency in subtitle placement will strengthen trust in the product as an assistive tool.

4. **Immediate next probe**
