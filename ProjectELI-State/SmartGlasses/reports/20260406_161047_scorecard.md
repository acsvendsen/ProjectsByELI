## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on creating a discreet, context-aware personal assistant with live subtitles, face/name recall, and conversation memory. The V1 version will use frame-touch interaction exclusively and rely heavily on the phone for processing to manage battery and thermal constraints.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The project is focusing on a frame-touch interaction pattern to reduce power consumption and maintain social acceptability. The glasses are designed to be lightweight, which aligns with the goal of minimizing on-glasses computation.
- next focus: Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
