### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules, we can ensure that subtitles remain readable and do not distract the wearer during live interactions. 2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches (e.g., names or repeated phrases) and another for lower-confidence matches (e.g., new faces or less certain spoken content). 3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing these rules, we can significantly improve subtitle clarity and reduce distraction during conversations. This will help users stay focused on their surroundings while still benefiting from real-time captioning, enhancing both their understanding and social interactions.
