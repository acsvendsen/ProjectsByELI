### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain clear and stable, enhancing the wearer's ability to understand the conversation in noisy environments. This directly supports the attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a fixed position for subtitles during low confidence states (e.g., when the system is unsure about who is speaking). This minimizes visual distraction and ensures that the wearer can focus on the conversation without being pulled away by constantly shifting or unstable text.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By setting a fixed subtitle placement, the wearer will experience more consistent and less distracting subtitles, which can significantly reduce cognitive load in noisy environments. This improves overall understanding and trust in the system's ability to assist during live conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format will enhance the trustworthiness of the subtitle and memory assistance provided by V1. A well-designed confidence indicator helps the wearer understand when to rely on the system's output, thereby increasing overall user satisfaction and reliability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence label with a short reason for why the certainty exists or is lacking. For example, use "High" + "Seen recently" for recent face encounters or "Low" + "Weak match" for less certain identifications.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear and concise confidence indicators, V1 can reduce user anxiety about relying on the system's output, especially in critical social interactions where accuracy is paramount. This will help build trust between the wearer and the assistant, making both subtitle and memory recall more effective and reliable.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By setting a clear local memory threshold for new-person recall, we ensure that the system focuses on providing relevant and trustworthy information without overwhelming the wearer with too much data.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a minimum confidence score threshold (e.g., 0.65) below which new persons are not added to the local memory cache until they are reinforced through repeated interactions or explicit user capture.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - This change helps maintain trust by preventing the system from suggesting names and faces that have only been seen once, thus avoiding potential errors in recall. It also reduces the cognitive load on the wearer by keeping the memory display focused on well-reinforced entities.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the subtitle placement rules to be more stable and less reactive will help maintain a consistent reading experience for the wearer, reducing cognitive load and enhancing trust in the system's reliability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence speakers (e.g., well-known faces or frequently encountered individuals) where subtitles can be slightly lower on screen to avoid overlap with important visual content, and another for low-confidence speakers (e.g., new people or unfamiliar voices) where subtitles are placed higher up but remain stable.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining these two subtitle placement modes, the system will provide a better reading experience without distracting the wearer from critical visual information. This will enhance trust and usability, aligning with the core mission of helping the wearer understand real-world interactions more effectively.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support helps maintain the wearer's focus on essential information without overwhelming them. This balance ensures that critical subtitles remain prominent, while contextual prompts are easily accessible but do not distract from the main conversation.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple precedence rule: prioritize subtitle display over one-line support by default, with an option to show one-line prompts when necessary. Implement this rule in `TranscriptLab` to test its impact on usability and distraction levels.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly separating the primary function of subtitles from contextual prompts, we can reduce cognitive overload during real-world interactions. This will enhance the wearer's trust in the product by ensuring they focus on what matters most—understanding the conversation—and not be distracted by less critical information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
