### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain clear, stable, and unobtrusive during live conversations. Stable and optimal placement enhances readability and reduces distraction, thereby increasing the wearer's trust in the system’s ability to provide accurate and timely information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two fixed subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence text (e.g., known speakers or repeated names) and another for low-confidence text (e.g., new faces or uncertain words). This approach ensures that critical information is always prominently displayed, while less certain text remains discreet.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing these fixed placement rules, the system can more effectively manage screen real estate, reducing visual clutter and improving overall readability. This will enhance the wearer's trust in the subtitles by ensuring they are easily readable without being overly intrusive, especially during fast-paced conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that memory support is handled phone-locally where possible, while falling back to cloud for intensive tasks like transcript cleanup and memory archive sync, helps maintain a balance between privacy and utility. This approach allows the system to provide immediate, reliable assistance without compromising on user data privacy.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one local-cache size limit for face/name recall that ensures frequent but not excessive memory refreshes, balancing between reinforcement over time and real-time relevance.

   By limiting the local cache to a reasonable size while still refreshing memories frequently enough, the system can maintain high confidence in person memories without overburdening the phone's storage or battery. This leads to more reliable face/name recall and faster lookup times during conversations.
