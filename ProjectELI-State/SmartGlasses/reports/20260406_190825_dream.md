### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement helps maintain a consistent, non-distracting experience for the wearer. Stable subtitles reduce cognitive load and enhance trust in the system's real-time capabilities.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two fixed subtitle placement modes: one at eye level (default) and another slightly below eye level to account for glasses frames. This allows the system to adapt based on whether the user is wearing glasses or not without repositioning dynamically.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing a stable, non-distracting subtitle placement, users can focus more on the conversation rather than adjusting their gaze between subtitles and other participants. This improves overall interaction quality and trust in the system’s real-time capabilities.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define two fixed subtitle placement modes: one at eye level (default) and another slightly below eye level for glasses wearers, to ensure stable, non-distracting subtitle delivery.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   To ensure the wearer can trust subtitles in live conversation, clear, fast, and stable placement is crucial. By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence levels, we can maintain readability and avoid distraction while still providing useful information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 subtitle placement rules that are context-aware and confidence-adaptive but remain stable over time. For example:
   - **High Confidence**: Display subtitles at the top center.
   - **Medium Confidence**: Display subtitles slightly lower, maintaining visual clarity without overlapping with other information.
   - **Low Confidence**: Display subtitles in a less prominent position to avoid distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By establishing stable yet adaptive subtitle placement rules, we can enhance the wearer's trust in the system’s accuracy and maintain a low-friction user experience. This will help reduce cognitive load and ensure that the wearer can focus on the conversation without being distracted by constantly shifting or intrusive subtitles.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving memory cache policies ensures that frequently accessed information remains readily available, enhancing the wearer's trust in the system by providing quick and reliable recall of names, faces, and prior context. This consistency in performance is crucial for maintaining a seamless user experience without introducing latency or complexity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one local cache eviction rule that prioritizes frequently accessed entities while ensuring less frequent but important information does not get evicted too quickly. For example, set a policy where memory objects are retained based on recent access frequency and recency.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting up an appropriate cache eviction strategy, the system can maintain a balance between keeping essential memory items readily accessible and managing overall storage efficiently. This improves lookup speed and reduces the need for cloud interactions, enhancing battery life and user trust in the system's reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a local cache eviction rule based on recency of access to ensure frequently used entities remain available.
