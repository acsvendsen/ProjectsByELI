### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles remain readable and stable in noisy environments while reducing distraction. This improves the wearer's ability to trust real-time subtitles during live conversations, aligning with the attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity."

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two specific subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence subtitles (clearly visible) and another for low-confidence subtitles (more subtle and less distracting).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By creating distinct placement rules, we can enhance the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles, reducing cognitive load during noisy conversations and improving overall understanding.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format will enhance the trustworthiness and clarity of the system's assistance, aligning better with the wearer's understanding and confidence in the system's abilities. This directly supports the attractor of "Trustworthy memory support" by ensuring that the memory recall is clearly presented without overconfident or misleading certainty.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a new confidence format that combines a numeric score, a label (High, Medium, Low), and a short reason for why the system is certain about its memory of a person or name. This simple change will provide a clear indication to the wearer of the reliability of the memory support.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this confidence format, users will better understand when they can trust the system's memory recall, leading to improved daily-use value and reduced cognitive load in social interactions. The clarity and honesty of the system’s assistance will contribute to a more reliable user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

This improves overall trust in the system’s ability to remember important details without unnecessary complexity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
