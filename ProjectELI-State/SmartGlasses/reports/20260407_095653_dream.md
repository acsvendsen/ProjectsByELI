### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a stable subtitle placement rule based on confidence state, we can ensure that subtitles remain readable and do not distract the wearer during high-confidence situations while still providing clear assistance when needed. This will improve overall user trust and understanding without overwhelming the wearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a single rule: For high-confidence interactions (e.g., well-known speakers or repeated names), place subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view; for lower confidence (new faces, unfamiliar context), place subtitles slightly above the baseline to avoid overlapping with text and reduce visual clutter.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will enhance subtitle clarity and readability without causing distraction during high-confidence interactions. It will also maintain a clear visual hierarchy that supports low-friction assistance, improving user trust and overall experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that subtitles coexist with one-line prompts in a way that does not overwhelm the wearer while still providing necessary support is crucial for maintaining trust and usability. A well-defined visual hierarchy ensures that the wearer can easily distinguish between ongoing conversation (via subtitles) and supplementary information (one-line prompts), reducing cognitive load and social awkwardness.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a priority rule that gives subtitles precedence over one-line prompts when both are present, but allows for brief, critical one-line prompts to be shown alongside the subtitle during key moments. This can be achieved by setting a visual hierarchy where subtitles always occupy primary display space, with one-line prompts appearing as secondary information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By establishing this priority rule, the system will maintain clarity and focus on the core task of understanding live conversation through clear subtitles, while still providing necessary contextual support. This reduces the risk of overwhelming the wearer with too much information at once, thereby enhancing trust and usability in noisy environments where real-time understanding is crucial.

4. **Immediate next probe**
