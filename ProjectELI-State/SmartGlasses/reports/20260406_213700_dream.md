### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance readability and stability, reducing distractions and increasing trust. Stable subtitle placement ensures that subtitles remain within a fixed area of the frame, making them easier to follow without shifting around.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches (e.g., names, faces) placed at the bottom center of the screen with minimal movement, and another for lower-confidence content that can be slightly more dynamic but still within a defined area to maintain readability.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By keeping high-confidence subtitles in a fixed position, we reduce cognitive load and increase trust. Lower-confidence content can be allowed some flexibility, but always staying within a known region of the screen ensures that the wearer can easily follow along without constant repositioning.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
