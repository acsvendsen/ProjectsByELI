### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules that prioritize stability and readability, we can ensure that subtitles remain unobtrusive yet clear enough for the wearer to follow conversations comfortably. This enhances trust by providing consistent, reliable support without overwhelming the wearer with frequent repositions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 2 to 3 subtitle placement rules based on confidence states: one rule for high-confidence situations where subtitles can be placed closer to the face and remain stable; another for medium-confidence situations that allow slightly more movement but still maintain readability; and a third rule for low-confidence scenarios where subtitles are moved away from the face to reduce distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will likely improve the overall user experience by making subtitles more predictable and less distracting, thereby enhancing trust in the system’s reliability during critical moments of conversation.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
