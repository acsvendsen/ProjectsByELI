### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining more prominent subtitle placement rules, we ensure that subtitles remain clear and easily readable even in noisy environments, which is crucial for real-time clarity and trust.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed position at the bottom center of the field of view for subtitles with a fallback rule to move slightly upward if the wearer tilts their head down.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will enhance subtitle readability, reduce distraction, and maintain consistent placement across different head positions, improving overall trust in the system's real-time support.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that confidence labels are clear, concise, and appropriately displayed enhances the trustworthiness of memory support in live interactions. This improves the wearer's ability to rely on remembered information without feeling overconfident or underconfident.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a standard confidence format for all memory objects: score 0.8 + "High" + "Seen recently". Use this format consistently across all memory-related UI elements and ensure that the label is displayed next to any name or face recall, providing clear context for the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a consistent confidence format, users will have a better understanding of how reliable their memory support is in real-time interactions. This clarity can reduce social awkwardness and increase trust in the system's ability to assist accurately without over- or under-exaggerating its capabilities.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving memory cache policies ensures that frequently accessed information is quickly available, reducing latency and enhancing trust in the system's reliability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a recency-first eviction policy for the local cache to prioritize keeping recently seen names and faces close at hand while removing less recent entries more aggressively.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By prioritizing frequently accessed memory, the system can reduce the need for cloud queries, improving response speed and ensuring that the wearer's interactions feel seamless and frictionless.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary between what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud for live subtitle processing, we ensure that subtitles remain fast and reliable. This direct interaction with real-time audio input helps maintain the trustworthiness of the system in noisy environments, which is crucial for its primary mission.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific rule for when live subtitle processing should occur on the phone versus falling back to cloud. For example, if the audio quality or network conditions are below a certain threshold, automatically fallback to the cloud for processing.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By ensuring that critical real-time subtitle processing stays local, we can maintain low latency and high reliability, which are essential for the system's core mission of providing clear, fast subtitles in noisy environments. This also helps avoid potential privacy concerns related to offloading sensitive audio data to the cloud.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one phone-cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing based on audio quality or network conditions.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line prompts helps maintain the wearer's focus on the most critical information, reducing cognitive load and enhancing overall understanding during conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a precedence rule where subtitles are displayed prominently, with one-line prompts shown in a less intrusive manner that does not compete for the wearer’s attention unless necessary.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing subtitle visibility over one-line prompts, the system can ensure that critical information is always legible and quickly accessible to the wearer, thereby improving overall interaction quality and reducing the chance of missed or misunderstood messages.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
