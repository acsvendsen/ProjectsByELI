### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By defining clear subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are always in a position that maximizes readability while minimizing distraction. This helps maintain the wearer's focus on the conversation and reduces cognitive load.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define two subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence text (e.g., names of people already remembered) and another for low-confidence text (e.g., new faces or spoken words that are not yet confirmed).

3. **Likely payoff**
   - This will enhance the user's trust in the subtitles by ensuring they are always easily readable, which can lead to better overall understanding during conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

This clarity will help the wearer better understand when they can rely on the information provided, enhancing overall user trust and satisfaction.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a specific task boundary for cloud fallback in live subtitle processing, we ensure that critical real-time subtitle quality is maintained while leveraging phone-first processing to reduce thermal and battery strain.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Modify the cloud fallback boundary so that live subtitle processing only falls back to the cloud when local processing cannot handle high-confidence, low-latency requirements, and always keep high-confidence subtitles locally processed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change ensures that critical real-time subtitles are always accurate and stable, which directly benefits the core attractor of "real-time subtitle clarity" by maintaining trust in live conversation scenarios. It also optimizes battery usage and thermal performance without compromising on the quality of assistance provided.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.
