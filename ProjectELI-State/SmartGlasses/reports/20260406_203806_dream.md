### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a stable subtitle placement rule based on confidence levels, we ensure that subtitles remain unobtrusive and clear, enhancing the wearer's understanding without causing distraction. This helps maintain trust in the system by providing reliable, easy-to-read information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches (e.g., names seen multiple times), another for medium-confidence matches (e.g., names heard once in introduction), and a third for low-confidence matches (e.g., new people not yet reinforced).

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these rules will reduce the risk of subtitles becoming distracting, especially during dynamic conversations where trust is critical. It also supports the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity by ensuring that only high-priority information is prominently displayed.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
