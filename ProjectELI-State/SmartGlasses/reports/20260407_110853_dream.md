### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement helps maintain clear, readable subtitles that are crucial for real-time communication and understanding. Stable placement reduces distraction and ensures that subtitles remain visible and legible throughout conversations, thereby supporting the wearer's ability to follow along without interruption.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that prioritizes central, bottom-aligned display regardless of head or gaze movements. This simple change can prevent frequent repositioning and ensure consistent visibility, making subtitles more reliable for the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By maintaining stable subtitle placement, we enhance the trustworthiness of the subtitles in noisy environments. This stability reduces cognitive load and ensures that the wearer remains focused on the conversation rather than adjusting their view to catch missed words or phrases.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format ensures that the wearer can trust the system's memory and recall, which is crucial for building reliable support during interactions. By using a clear and consistent label with a short reason, the wearer understands when to trust the information provided.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one specific confidence object format: `score 0.8 + "High" + "Seen recently in conversation"`. This format provides a numeric score for quick understanding, a clear label for context, and a short reason that offers additional clarity when needed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this specific confidence format, the system will be able to convey its certainty levels more effectively, reducing confusion and increasing trust during interactions. The wearer will have a better sense of when to rely on the system's memory support, leading to improved overall user satisfaction and real-world usefulness.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a specific local memory threshold for face/name recall, we ensure that critical information is readily available to the wearer without overwhelming their visual field. This improves trust and usability by providing just-in-time support while minimizing distraction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit of 5-10 most recently seen faces/names for quick lookup on the phone, with periodic reinforcement from cloud data.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will enable fast, reliable face/name recall that supports daily use without burdening the wearer or draining battery life. It enhances trust by providing timely and relevant support when needed.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing stays local on the phone helps maintain real-time performance and minimizes battery drain, which is crucial for trust in noisy environments where timely information is essential.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear rule that live subtitles are processed locally on the phone without falling back to cloud support under any circumstances unless explicitly needed for memory archive sync or conversation snapshot upload.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitle processing local, we ensure minimal latency and high reliability, which strengthens trust in real-world interactions where timely information is critical. This decision also aligns with V1's focus on low-friction assistance and battery-conscious architecture.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear, stable, and trustworthy subtitle display is crucial for the wearer's understanding in real-time conversations. By optimizing subtitle placement rules, we can maintain high subtitle clarity without overwhelming the wearer with visual noise, thereby enhancing their ability to follow the conversation naturally.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 2 to 3 subtitle placement rules for different confidence states: one rule where subtitles are always placed at the bottom of the field of view (FOV), another where they float above the speaker's face but stay within a certain FOV threshold, and a third that only shows subtitles when the speech is highly uncertain or non-verifiable.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will help in maintaining visual clarity without being overly intrusive, ensuring that the wearer can trust the subtitles while keeping distractions to a minimum during high-concentration activities like meetings or social gatherings.

4. **Immediate next probe**
