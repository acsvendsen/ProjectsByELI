### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles remain stable and readable while minimizing distractions. This helps maintain trust in the system by providing clear and reliable information during live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence situations where subtitles are shown at a fixed position near the wearer's line of sight, and another for medium to low-confidence situations where subtitles are placed slightly lower or to the side to avoid distraction but remain visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing clear and stable subtitle placement rules will reduce cognitive load and distractions during conversations. This will help maintain the wearer's focus on the conversation while providing necessary support, thereby enhancing overall user satisfaction and trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

By implementing a clear confidence display format, users will have greater trust in the subtitles and memory support provided by the glasses. This increased trust can lead to more frequent use of the device, better understanding during conversations, and overall higher satisfaction with the product.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving the memory cache policy ensures that the system can quickly recall relevant information about names and faces, which is crucial for providing trustworthy support to the wearer. By optimizing how new evidence is linked to existing person-memory objects, we can enhance the accuracy and reliability of our real-time subtitle assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a local cache eviction rule that prioritizes recent interactions over older ones when memory space is limited. This will ensure that frequently seen or recently encountered people are always quickly accessible for fast recall and reinforcement.

3. **Likely payoff**

By implementing a more efficient cache eviction policy, we can significantly reduce the latency in recalling names and faces during conversations, leading to more seamless and trustworthy real-time subtitle support. This improvement will help maintain high confidence levels and ensure that critical context is readily available when needed most.

4. **Immediate next probe**

Set one local-cache size limit for face/name memory that balances cache capacity with frequent recall needs, ensuring optimal performance on the phone without overwhelming it.
