### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By establishing clear, stable subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always legible and unobtrusive, thereby enhancing the wearer's ability to follow conversations without distraction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence content (always visible), one for medium-confidence content (visible with a subtle indicator of uncertainty), and one for low-confidence content (temporarily hidden or replaced by a placeholder).

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will reduce visual clutter, maintain the wearer's focus on conversation flow, and ensure that only high-priority information is always visible. It also allows the system to convey less certainty without overwhelming the user with too many changes.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
