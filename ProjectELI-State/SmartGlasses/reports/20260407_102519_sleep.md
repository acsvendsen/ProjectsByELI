## Weakening Signals
- **Always-On Sensing vs Battery/Thermals**: Reduce dependency on always-on sensing to minimize battery drain and thermal risks.
- **Rich Context vs Low-Latency Response**: Optimize for faster, less complex context processing to reduce latency while maintaining rich memory support.
