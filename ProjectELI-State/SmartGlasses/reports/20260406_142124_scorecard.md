## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, and conversation memory. The current iteration is frame-touch interaction-driven, with heavy reliance on the phone for processing, and minimal local computation to meet battery and thermal constraints.

- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
