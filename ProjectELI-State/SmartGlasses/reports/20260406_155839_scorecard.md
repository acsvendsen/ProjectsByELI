## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on creating discreet, context-aware smart glasses for live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 will use frame-touch interaction exclusively, relying primarily on the phone for processing tasks, with minimal local computation to preserve battery life and social acceptability.
