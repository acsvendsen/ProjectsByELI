- **Memory Trust**: Enhancing the reliability of name, face, and context recall without overconfidence or misleading the wearer.
- **Low-Friction Assistance**: Improving one-line prompts to provide timely help without creating command overhead.

## Weakening Signals
- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Reducing the tendency to prioritize ease of use at the expense of privacy.
- **Latency vs Richness**: Balancing real-time responses with the need for richer context, but not at the cost of high latency.
- **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**: Simplifying display behavior while maintaining readability in noisy environments.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Always-On Sensing vs Battery and Thermals**: Prioritizing always-on sensing only when it does not significantly impact battery life or thermals.
- **Rich Context vs Low-Latency Response**: Ensuring that the system remains responsive while delivering rich contextual support.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
