### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, the system can ensure that subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive while still providing essential information when needed. This improves the wearer's ability to follow conversations in noisy environments without being overwhelmed by too much text.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two placement modes: one for high-confidence content (like names or familiar faces) near the center of the field of view, and another for lower-confidence content (like less certain words or phrases) at the bottom of the screen.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping high-confidence subtitles centrally visible, the system can enhance the wearer's confidence in understanding key interactions. Lower-confidence text can be relegated to a less intrusive position without causing visual clutter. This balanced approach improves overall usability and trust in the subtitle quality.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
