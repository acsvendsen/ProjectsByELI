## Subtitle Placement: Feasible Now
- **Subtitle Placement**: The current implementation in `TranscriptLab` allows subtitles to be displayed on the phone screen, mimicking what a future glasses display might look like. Given that this is a phone-first approach and the goal is to ensure real-time clarity, it's feasible now to explore various subtitle placements (e.g., top-center, bottom-center) through simple UI tests in `TranscriptLab`. This can be iterated on quickly without hardware constraints.
