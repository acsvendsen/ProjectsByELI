### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring consistent subtitle placement helps maintain a clear, unobtrusive visual experience that supports real-time understanding without overwhelming the wearer. Stable and predictable subtitle positioning enhances trust in the system's reliability, which is crucial for daily use.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule where subtitles are always positioned at the bottom of the frame, ensuring they do not move or animate during the conversation. This rule will prevent distractions and maintain focus on the conversation.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting a consistent, unchanging subtitle position, we reduce cognitive load for the wearer, who can quickly and easily read subtitles without having to adjust their gaze. This improves overall user experience and trust in the system’s reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps build trust in the system's ability to provide accurate and reliable information. By refining how certainty is conveyed, users can better judge when to rely on the subtitles or seek further clarification.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a concise confidence format that includes both a numeric score, a label, and a short reason for the score. For example: "Score 0.85 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'".

3. **Likely payoff**

   A well-designed confidence display can significantly enhance user trust by making the system's certainty levels transparent. Users will be more likely to rely on the subtitles and engage in conversations with greater confidence, knowing when they should seek further clarification.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the local memory threshold for face/name recall allows V1 to maintain a balance between rich context and low latency, ensuring that frequently encountered individuals are remembered with high confidence without overloading the wearer's experience.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache eviction policy that retains memory objects for frequently seen faces and names but evicts less frequently encountered ones after a certain period to avoid excessive storage and processing overhead.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting a reasonable local cache threshold, V1 can provide timely and relevant assistance while reducing the risk of battery drain and thermal stress associated with continuous background memory updates.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set a local-cache size limit for face/name memories to 50 entries.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what runs phone-local versus cloud, we ensure that V1 interactions remain lightweight and battery-conscious while still providing essential support. This clarity helps in maintaining trust by avoiding overloading the glasses with complex processing tasks.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one task-specific cloud fallback boundary for exactly one task such as live subtitle processing or face/name recall lookup. For example, determine what parts of name memory recall should run locally on the phone and which parts can fall back to the cloud if needed.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly defining local vs cloud tasks, we reduce battery consumption and thermal load while ensuring that critical functions like live subtitle processing remain fast and reliable. This also helps in maintaining a discreet UX by keeping complex operations off the glasses themselves.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line prompts helps ensure that critical information is prioritized, reducing cognitive load and maintaining the wearer's focus on the conversation. By defining a clear precedence rule for display, we can enhance the clarity of real-time subtitles while also providing useful, unobtrusive one-line support.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a visual priority rule that ensures subtitles are always displayed prominently over one-line prompts when both are active. This can be achieved by setting a higher z-index for subtitles or by using a simple display precedence logic in the UI layer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing subtitle clarity, we ensure that the wearer remains engaged with the conversation and can rely on accurate real-time information. This improves overall understanding and reduces cognitive friction, making the product more useful and trusted. Additionally, this rule supports the core mission of low-friction assistance by ensuring that critical information is not overshadowed.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
