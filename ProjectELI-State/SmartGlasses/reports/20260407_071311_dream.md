### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain a consistent, distraction-free experience for the wearer. Stable rules prevent subtitles from constantly moving or flashing, which can break the wearer's focus and reduce trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence states: high-confidence (always visible), medium-confidence (only show when needed), and low-confidence (hide unless explicitly requested).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing stable subtitle placement rules, the system will become more reliable and less distracting for the wearer. This will increase trust in the system's ability to provide accurate and useful subtitles without disrupting the user’s natural flow of conversation.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
