### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules helps ensure that subtitles remain stable, readable, and not distracting during live conversations. This enhances the wearer's ability to follow along without getting lost in visual clutter or disorientation.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define three fixed subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence speaker-specific content (e.g., names), another for low-confidence general information, and a third fallback mode that ensures subtitles do not overlap or move frequently.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By establishing stable placement rules, the system can reduce visual distraction and maintain focus during conversations. This improves overall clarity and trust in the subtitle experience, aligning with the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps build trust in both subtitle clarity and memory recall. A clear, concise, and accurate confidence indication ensures that the wearer understands the reliability of the information provided, which is crucial for maintaining their trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific confidence format that includes a numeric score, a label, and a short reason. For example: "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'". This format provides both quantitative and qualitative indicators of certainty, allowing users to quickly assess the reliability of the information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly indicating confidence levels through a consistent and easily understood format, the system can significantly reduce user skepticism and increase trust in its functionality. This will enhance the overall user experience by ensuring that users feel confident when relying on the system's assistance during critical moments such as conversations or meetings.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the local cache size threshold for name recall helps ensure that frequently encountered names and faces are always available quickly, reducing latency in memory lookups and maintaining a high level of trust with the wearer. This improves both subtitle clarity (as context is more readily accessible) and memory support (by ensuring that important entities are consistently remembered).

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size threshold for name recall on the phone, setting it to retain at least 10 frequently encountered names and faces.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By caching more commonly encountered names and faces locally, we reduce the need for frequent cloud requests, which can help in maintaining low latency and ensuring that memory lookups are quick and reliable. This improvement will contribute to a smoother user experience by providing fast and accurate name recall without overloading the network.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one local-cache size limit for frequently encountered names and faces on the phone, setting it to retain at least 10 entities.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud, we ensure that the system remains lightweight and efficient. This helps in maintaining high real-time subtitle clarity and trustworthiness while respecting battery and thermal constraints.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific task boundary for live subtitle processing that keeps all critical subtitle-related tasks phone-local.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing local, we can ensure faster response times, reduced latency, and more stable performance, which enhances the wearer's trust in the system. This also helps in managing battery consumption more effectively without sacrificing real-time quality.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support, the system ensures that critical information is prioritized without overwhelming the wearer. This helps maintain focus during live conversations and supports the core mission of providing essential context in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple visual priority rule where subtitles are always displayed prominently above one-line prompts. For example, use a fixed-size subtitle area at the bottom center of the screen, while one-line prompts appear below or to the side with reduced size and duration.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change ensures that subtitles remain the primary focus, enhancing readability and trust during conversations. It also prevents distractions from less critical information, maintaining a clear and uncluttered interface. Users will be more likely to rely on this assistive feature, improving daily-use value without adding complexity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
