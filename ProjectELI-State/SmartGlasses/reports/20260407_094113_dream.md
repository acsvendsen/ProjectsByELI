### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving subtitle placement rules will enhance the readability and stability of subtitles, ensuring that the wearer can trust them during live conversations. This will directly support the primary attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a fixed subtitle placement rule for different confidence states: high-confidence subtitles should be placed in a fixed position at the bottom center of the field of view, while low-confidence subtitles can be slightly adjusted to avoid distraction but remain clearly visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

By setting clear and consistent placement rules based on confidence levels, the system will reduce visual clutter and ensure that the wearer focuses on high-certainty information without being overwhelmed by lower-confidence subtitles. This improvement will enhance user trust in the subtitle accuracy during noisy or complex environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

This improvement ensures that the wearer can understand when a piece of information is highly certain versus less reliable, which is crucial for maintaining real-time subtitle clarity and memory support.

   Define a numeric score + label + short reason format for confidence objects. For example, "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'; score 0.42 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'; score 0.63 + 'Medium' + 'Name heard once in introduction'."

3. **Likely payoff**

   This format will provide clear and concise feedback to the wearer, helping them distinguish between highly certain and less reliable information. It will improve trust by making confidence levels transparent and understandable.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
