## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. V1 will use frame-touch interaction exclusively and rely heavily on the phone for processing while minimizing on-glasses complexity to ensure battery efficiency and social acceptability.
