### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a consistent subtitle placement rule based on confidence levels, V1 can ensure that subtitles are always readable and unobtrusive while still providing clear assistance to the wearer. This helps in maintaining trust by ensuring that the wearer can rely on the subtitles without feeling overwhelmed or distracted.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple subtitle placement rule where high-confidence subtitles are shown fixed at the bottom of the frame, low-confidence subtitles are shown slightly above but still visible, and medium-confidence subtitles are shown in a semi-transparent overlay that does not cover important visual elements like faces or objects.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that the wearer can always see the most likely information while maintaining a clean and unobtrusive interface. It reduces cognitive load by prioritizing the most reliable information, thereby enhancing the overall user experience and trust in the system's assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving confidence display by using a numeric score with a label and short reason helps users understand the reliability of memory recall without overwhelming them. This approach strikes a balance between providing clear feedback and maintaining a discreet, low-friction UX.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a consistent format for confidence objects that includes a numeric score, a label, and a brief reason. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this format, users will have a clear understanding of the reliability of name or face memory. This clarity can enhance trust in the system's assistance and reduce cognitive load for the wearer, making the product more effective and user-friendly.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that subtitles are prioritized correctly in visual hierarchy helps maintain the wearer's trust and comprehension during live conversations. By defining a clear precedence rule between subtitles and one-line support, we can reduce visual clutter without sacrificing important information. This enhances the overall user experience by keeping the most critical assistance visible and readable.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a priority rule that gives subtitles higher visual prominence over one-line support when confidence in face/name recognition is low. This means that if the system has lower confidence in identifying who is speaking, the subtitle should be displayed more prominently to ensure the wearer can follow along without confusion.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing subtitles over one-line prompts during moments of lower confidence, we reduce the risk of the wearer missing critical information or becoming distracted by less important prompts. This improves overall user trust and comprehension in noisy or challenging environments, thereby strengthening the core mission of enhancing real-world understanding through reliable subtitle support.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
