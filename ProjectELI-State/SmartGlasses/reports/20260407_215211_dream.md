### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By defining a consistent subtitle placement rule that minimizes distraction while ensuring clear readability, we can enhance the wearer's trust in the system during live conversations. This ensures subtitles remain visible and readable without overwhelming the user, thus supporting real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a fixed position for subtitles at the bottom of the screen with a margin to avoid overlapping with the main view area, ensuring they are always visible but not intrusive.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - A consistent and unobtrusive subtitle placement will reduce cognitive load and ensure that the wearer can rely on the subtitles without distraction. This will improve overall user trust and satisfaction in noisy or crowded environments where real-time understanding is crucial.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the subtitles and memory support provided by the system. A clear and consistent confidence indicator will reduce ambiguity and build stronger trust in the assistive features, thereby enhancing the overall quality of real-time subtitle clarity and trustworthy memory support.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing a clear confidence format, the wearer can better understand the reliability of the subtitles and memory support. This clarity will help them focus more on the conversation or task at hand, reducing cognitive load and increasing trust in the system's accuracy.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

This will contribute to the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the phone/cloud boundary for live subtitle processing ensures that real-time subtitles remain clear, stable, and trustworthy during live conversations. By keeping critical subtitle generation local to the phone, we can minimize latency and ensure that the wearer receives immediate feedback without delays caused by cloud processing.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a rule that all high-confidence speech-to-text transcription is processed locally on the phone before being transmitted to the glasses for display. This ensures that even if there are minor network issues, subtitles remain reliable and timely.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By maintaining real-time subtitle clarity through local processing, we will significantly reduce the risk of dropped or delayed subtitles during critical conversations. This enhances trust in the product and improves the overall user experience by ensuring that the wearer can rely on the subtitles for accurate understanding.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle-vs-one-line priority helps maintain a clear visual hierarchy that supports the wearer's understanding without overwhelming them with too much information at once. This ensures that subtitles remain central and trusted while one-line prompts are used for additional support when necessary, enhancing overall usability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a visual priority rule where subtitles always take precedence over one-line prompts in the display hierarchy, but allow one-line prompts to appear alongside subtitles if they add critical context without distracting from the primary subtitle text.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By clearly prioritizing subtitles over one-line prompts, the system ensures that the most crucial information (subtitles) remains at the forefront of the wearer's attention. This reduces cognitive load and enhances the wearer’s ability to follow along during conversations or presentations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
