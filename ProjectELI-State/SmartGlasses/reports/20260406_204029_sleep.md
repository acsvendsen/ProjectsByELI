# Consolidated Insights for SmartGlasses V1

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Clarity**: Enhance real-time subtitle clarity to ensure they remain readable, stable, and trust-preserving in live conversation.
- **Memory Trust**: Improve the accuracy of memory support (names, faces) without over-relying on certainty or creating false confidence.

## Weakening Signals
- **Low-Friction Assistance**: Simplify one-line prompts and interactions to reduce command overhead and maintain social acceptability. Avoid overly complex interfaces that could detract from the core functionality.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Ensure assistive behavior is helpful without becoming invasive, particularly in memory recall.
- **Latency vs Richness**: Balance between providing timely help (low latency) and offering rich contextual support.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Subtitle Placement**: Revisit subtitle placement to find a balance between readability and non-distracting visual clarity.
- **Confidence Display**: Consider different confidence formats that build trust, such as labels with short reasons or numeric scores.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Areas

### Subtitle Quality, Confidence Trust, Automatic New-Person Memory
- **Subtitle Quality**:
  - **Strengths**: Ensure subtitles are clear, fast, and stable.
  - **Dangers**: Avoid overloading the wearer with too much text in one go.

### Memory Policy, Fast Lookup, Privacy, Battery, Visual UX
- **Memory Policy**:
  - **Strengths**: Focus on remembering names and prior context without pretending certainty.
  - **Weaknesses**: Be cautious about storing unnecessary information that could compromise privacy or create false confidence.
  
- **Fast Lookup**:
  - **Strengths**: Support quick access to stored memories to enhance conversation flow.
  - **Dangers**: Ensure fast lookup does not drain battery or distract the wearer.

### Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity
- **Discreet UX**:
  - **Strengths**: Maintain social acceptability and minimal disruption in real-world use.
  - **Weaknesses**: Risk of being too subtle to be useful, especially in noisy environments.

- **Visual Clarity**:
  - **Strengths**: Ensure subtitles are clear enough for the wearer to read comfortably.
  - **Dangers**: Avoid making subtitles so prominent that they become distracting.

### Phone/Cloud Boundary
- **Phone-First Runtime**:
  - **Strengths**: Keep heavy processing on the phone to reduce battery and thermal strain.
  - **Weaknesses**: Ensure cloud support is only used where clearly justified, such as for model upgrades or non-critical background tasks.
