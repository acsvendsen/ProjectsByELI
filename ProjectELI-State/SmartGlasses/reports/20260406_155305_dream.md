### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles are always visible and readable without causing distraction. This helps maintain a balance between providing necessary information and keeping the wearer focused on their environment, which is crucial for real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: 
     - High-confidence subtitles placed more prominently in the center of the screen.
     - Medium-confidence subtitles slightly less prominent but still visible.
     - Low-confidence subtitles appear only as needed and are less obstructive.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - This approach enhances the wearer's ability to follow conversations without becoming distracted by constant subtitle movement. It also reduces the likelihood of missing important information, thus improving overall understanding and trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence format used for subtitles, we can ensure that the wearer understands the reliability of each subtitle in real-time, which is crucial for trust and effective use. This clarity helps reduce social friction by making it clear when the system is uncertain, thus avoiding potential misunderstandings.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a simple numeric score alongside a confidence label and short reason to describe the certainty of each subtitle. For example: "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'".

3. **Likely payoff**

   This format provides a clear, concise way for the wearer to understand the reliability of subtitles in real-time. It helps build trust by showing that the system is aware of its limitations and can convey this information transparently.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

### 1. **Why it benefits the core**
Improving the local cache size for face/name memory enhances the reliability of quick lookups during conversations, ensuring that new people are recognized without latency issues. This strengthens the core mission by providing more consistent assistance in real-time interactions.

### 2. **What small change unlocks it**
Define a local cache size limit for recently seen faces and names stored on the phone, with an eviction policy based on recency and confidence score.

### 3. **Likely payoff**
By setting a local cache of, say, 10-20 people, the system can quickly recall new individuals during conversations without needing to query the cloud or wait for updates. This reduces latency and enhances user trust in the system's ability to handle real-time interactions effectively.

### 4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.
