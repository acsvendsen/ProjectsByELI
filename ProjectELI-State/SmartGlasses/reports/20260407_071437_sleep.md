# Consolidated Signals for SmartGlasses Project

## Strengthening Signals
1. **Subtitle Clarity**: Enhance real-time subtitle clarity to ensure subtitles remain readable, stable, and trustworthy in live conversation.
2. **Memory Trust**: Improve memory support by remembering names, faces, and prior context accurately without creating misleading certainty.

## Weakening Signals
1. **Low-Friction Assistance**: Reduce the complexity of one-line prompts to minimize command overhead and social awkwardness while still providing useful assistance.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
1. **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Ensure that helpful assistive behavior does not quietly slide into invasive capture or retention.
2. **Latency vs Richness**: Prioritize timely responses over richer but slower processing to maintain trust and usability.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
1. **Subtitle Placement**: Revisit the placement of subtitles to find a balance between readability and non-distracting visual clarity.
2. **Confidence Display**: Explore different formats for confidence display, such as labels with short reasons or numeric scores, to build user trust.

## Concrete Tensions Around UX
1. **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**:
   - Ensure the system remains socially acceptable while still being readable in motion and noise.
2. **Battery vs Always-On Sensing**: Balance always-on sensing needs with battery constraints to minimize thermal and energy usage.
3. **Local Processing vs Cloud Intelligence**: Determine the best split between local processing and cloud intelligence based on practical limitations.

## Open Questions
1. **Subtitle Placement**: Identify the optimal placement for subtitles that are both readable and non-distracting.
2. **Confidence Display**: Decide on the most effective way to represent confidence, such as labels with short reasons or numeric scores.
3. **Memory/Cache Policy**: Determine how much face/name memory should be cached locally versus relying on cloud storage.

## Current Core Focus
1. **Core Deepening**: Continue to focus on improving subtitle quality, memory trust, and visual UX rather than broad feature expansion.
2. **Constraint Pressure**: Surface battery, latency, privacy, and usability limits early to guide decision-making.
