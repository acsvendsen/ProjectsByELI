### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

   Define a two-tiered cache policy: one for frequently seen people (e.g., score 0.8+ with recent interactions) that evicts less aggressively, and another for infrequently seen entities (e.g., score 0.5-0.79) that are more aggressively pruned to save resources.

3. **Likely payoff**
