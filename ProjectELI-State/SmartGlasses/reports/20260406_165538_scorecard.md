## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on creating a discreet personal assistant with real-time subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line prompts. The V1 iteration is constrained to frame-touch interactions, phone-first architecture, and minimal on-glasses processing to manage battery and thermal limitations.
