### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive while still providing necessary assistance in noisy environments. This will help the wearer stay engaged and informed during conversations, thereby deepening the core mission of real-time subtitle clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a fixed position for subtitles that is slightly above the center of the field of view, but not so high as to cause eye strain or distraction. For example, place subtitles 10-15 degrees below and centered within the wearer's gaze.

3. **Likely payoff**
   A well-defined subtitle placement rule will result in consistent and unobtrusive subtitle visibility, reducing distractions and enhancing real-time clarity. This improvement will directly contribute to the core attractor of "real-time subtitle clarity."

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the system's assistance without overloading them with unnecessary information. A clear, concise, and consistent confidence label and short reason will enhance the wearer's understanding of when to rely on the subtitles and memory support.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple yet informative confidence format: `score + " " + label + " " + short reason`. For example, use `0.85 High Recently Seen` for high-confidence memory or `0.3 Low Weak Match` for low-confidence names.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear and consistent confidence information, the system can reduce cognitive load on the wearer while maintaining trust. This will help in situations where the wearer needs to focus on the conversation without constantly questioning the accuracy of the subtitles or memory support.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

   By setting a local memory threshold for face/name recall, we ensure that the system retains only the most relevant and recent memories without overwhelming the wearer's privacy or causing unnecessary cognitive load. This helps in maintaining trust by ensuring that the memory support is both efficient and contextually appropriate.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit for face/name recall, such as retaining only 5-10 most recently interacted names/faces with a retention period of 3 days.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will reduce the cognitive load on the wearer by providing them with relevant and recent memories without overwhelming their attention. It also helps in maintaining privacy by limiting the amount of data stored locally, thereby reducing the risk of sensitive information being retained unnecessarily.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud, we can ensure that subtitle processing remains fast and stable, crucial for real-time subtitle clarity. This helps maintain trust in the system by ensuring that critical functionality is not delayed or compromised due to cloud latency.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific task boundary where live subtitle processing should remain phone-local. For example, if the system needs to handle face recognition and name recall for live subtitles, ensure these operations are completed on the phone rather than relying on cloud services.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping real-time subtitle processing local, we can achieve faster response times, lower latency, and a more stable user experience. This will help maintain trust in the system by ensuring that subtitles remain clear and readable even under network conditions where the cloud might be slower or unavailable.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one task-specific phone/cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing.
