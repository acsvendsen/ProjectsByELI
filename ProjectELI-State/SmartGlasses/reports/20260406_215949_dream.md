### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the wearer's understanding by ensuring that subtitles are always clear, stable, and not distracting. This will help maintain trust in the system during live conversations, which is crucial for the core mission of providing real-time assistance without overloading the user.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that keeps subtitles at the bottom of the frame, centered horizontally, but with a slight margin from the edges to avoid visual overlap and distraction during motion.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting a consistent and unobtrusive placement, the system can ensure that subtitles remain readable and stable across various head movements and orientations. This will help in maintaining user trust and focus on the conversation rather than the display.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
