## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering real-time subtitles, reliable face/name recall, and low-friction one-line support. The V1 will remain frame-touch interaction based, phone-first in processing, and ensure minimal on-glasses compute to maintain battery efficiency and social acceptability.
