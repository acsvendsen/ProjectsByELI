**Likely Waste of Time**
- The current guidance favors recency first memory caching (Recency First). While this is a practical starting point, extensive local caching may lead to privacy concerns and thermal stress on the glasses. Given the emphasis on phone-first processing and real-world limits, a more balanced approach with periodic cloud syncing might be better for long-term utility.

#### Phone/Cloud Boundary
**Feasible Now**
- The preference for phone-first architecture is well-grounded in current limitations. Using the phone as the primary runtime reduces battery drain and ensures that heavy lifting can still occur without overheating the glasses. This approach is pragmatic and aligns with the core mission of discreet, context-aware personal assistance.

**Feasible Now**
