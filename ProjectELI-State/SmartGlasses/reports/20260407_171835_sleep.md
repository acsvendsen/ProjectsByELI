1. **Subtitle Quality, Confidence Trust**:
   - Ensure subtitles are clear and stable enough for the wearer to trust them in live conversations.
   - Maintain confidence objects that clearly indicate uncertainty where necessary.

2. **Automatic Memory Behavior vs Explicit Remember-Touch Behavior**:
   - Continue to reinforce automatic memory behavior with confidence objects.
   - Ensure explicit remember-touch behavior is intuitive and effective for new faces and names.

3. **Fast Lookup Requirements**:
   - Balance the need for quick access to stored information against the risk of storing low-value data.

4. **Privacy, Battery, Latency, Visual UX**:
