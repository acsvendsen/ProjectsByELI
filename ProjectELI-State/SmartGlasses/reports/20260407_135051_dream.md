### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive while still providing necessary support in real-time conversations. This will help maintain trust by delivering accurate and timely information without overwhelming the wearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a simple rule that places subtitles at the bottom of the frame for high-confidence matches, and slightly above or to the side for lower-confidence matches. This rule ensures that high-confidence subtitles are clearly visible while lower-confidence ones do not distract from the conversation.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this subtitle placement strategy, we can enhance the wearer's trust in the system by ensuring that critical information is always easily readable. This will reduce cognitive load and improve the overall user experience without compromising on visual clarity or social acceptability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - By refining the confidence display format to be more precise, the system can better convey trust and certainty without overloading the wearer with unnecessary complexity. This improves the overall user experience by ensuring that the wearer understands when they are being provided with reliable information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a confidence object format that uses both a numeric score and a label with a short reason, such as "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'; score 0.42 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'; score 0.63 + 'Medium' + 'Name heard once in introduction'."

3. **Likely payoff**
   - This small change will help the wearer quickly understand the reliability of the information provided by the system, thereby increasing trust and reducing cognitive load. The concise format also aligns well with the low-friction assistance attractor.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Enhancing the local memory cache size for face/name recall ensures that common entities are quickly accessible, reducing latency and improving trust by providing fast and reliable information to the wearer without cloud dependency.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a minimum threshold of 50 recent face/name interactions to be cached locally on the phone with a retention period of 7 days.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This cache policy will significantly reduce the need for frequent cloud requests, leading to faster and more reliable name recall during conversations. It will also reinforce user trust by ensuring that common names are always available quickly, even when the glasses are not connected to the internet.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing remains local to the phone enhances real-time performance, reducing latency and maintaining the trust of the wearer in the system's accuracy. By keeping this task on the phone, we avoid the potential delays and instability that could arise from cloud communication, which is critical for a reliable assistive device.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one subtitle placement rule that keeps subtitles at the bottom of the screen with minimal movement, ensuring they remain in view without distracting the wearer. This static placement reduces complexity and ensures consistent behavior across different interactions.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing local, we can achieve near-real-time performance, which is crucial for maintaining trust and usability. This focus on real-time clarity will also simplify the UI design, making it more discreet and less distracting during conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

   Ensuring a clear, stable, and unobtrusive visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support improves the overall user experience by reducing cognitive load and maintaining social acceptability. This enhances the wearer's ability to focus on the conversation while still receiving necessary contextual cues.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining this visual hierarchy, the system will provide a clearer signal to the wearer about what information is most critical at any given moment. This reduces confusion and ensures that subtitles remain the primary focus, while one-line prompts can be quickly accessed when needed without overwhelming the user.
