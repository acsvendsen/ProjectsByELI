### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a fixed subtitle placement rule for different confidence states, we ensure that subtitles remain readable and stable in live conversation. This helps maintain trust by preventing flickering or erratic subtitle movement, which could distract the wearer during important interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple subtitle placement rule where low-confidence subtitles are always placed at the bottom of the screen, while high-confidence subtitles have a higher priority to be displayed above other content. This can be achieved by setting a fixed position for each confidence level in the system.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A consistent and predictable subtitle placement will reduce cognitive load and maintain trust. The wearer can focus on the conversation without worrying about subtitles moving unexpectedly, which improves overall user satisfaction and acceptance of the product.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the local cache size for face/name memory ensures that commonly encountered individuals are remembered more reliably without overloading the phone's processing or battery resources. This reduces the need for continuous cloud sync, maintaining a balance between privacy and utility.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache threshold of 10-20 entries for frequently seen faces and names, with automatic removal based on recency and interaction frequency.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By limiting the number of memory entries stored locally, the system can handle more frequent interactions without excessive battery drain or cloud sync activity. This improves trust through stable behavior while maintaining useful local recall capabilities.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define a local cache size limit of 20 entries for frequently seen faces and names, with automatic removal based on recency and interaction frequency.
