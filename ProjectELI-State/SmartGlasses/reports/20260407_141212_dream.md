### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement reduces visual distraction and enhances trust in the system's real-time assistance. Stable placement rules prevent subtitles from flickering or moving erratically, which can break concentration and lower confidence in the wearer’s understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two specific subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence states (when speaker identity is clear) and another for low-confidence states (when there's uncertainty about who is speaking). This allows the system to adjust its visual priority based on certainty, reducing distraction during critical moments.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By maintaining stable and context-aware subtitle placement, the wearer can focus more on the conversation itself without being distracted by erratic text movement. This improves overall understanding and trust in the system's real-time assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what runs on the phone versus the cloud, we can ensure that real-time subtitle processing stays local to reduce latency and maintain trust. This helps in providing timely, reliable subtitles without overloading the glasses hardware.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one task-specific cloud fallback boundary for exactly one task such as live subtitle processing. For example, decide which parts of subtitle generation and display can be done locally on the phone versus when a cloud fallback is necessary due to complexity or latency constraints.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This decision will ensure that subtitles remain clear and stable during critical real-time interactions, thereby enhancing the core mission of creating a reliable personal assistant. It will also set a precedent for handling other tasks with similar sensitivity to latency and reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one task-specific cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing, ensuring it runs phone-local when possible but falls back to cloud only for complex or high-latency operations.
