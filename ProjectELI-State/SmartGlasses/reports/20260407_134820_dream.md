### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain the wearer's trust in the system by providing consistent, predictable visual cues during live conversations. This consistency is crucial for real-time understanding and effective memory support.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed position for subtitles that remains visible but unobtrusive across different confidence states. For example, place subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view, with a margin from the edges to avoid distraction during movement.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitles in a consistent location, the system can reduce cognitive load and ensure that the wearer always has access to critical information without having to reorient their gaze or focus frequently. This stability will enhance the overall trust and utility of the product.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format, we can ensure that the wearer gets clear, concise, and context-aware cues about the reliability of subtitles and memory support. This enhances trust by making the system's certainty levels explicit and understandable.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a new confidence format: `score + label + short reason`. For example, "0.84 (High) - Seen recently" for a high-confidence subtitle that has been seen frequently.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This format provides clear and concise feedback to the wearer, enhancing trust by making the system's certainty levels explicit and understandable. It also supports fast lookup and reinforcement over time by attaching specific reasons to confidence scores.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a robust memory cache policy for fast lookup of names and faces enhances the wearer's trust in the system, allowing them to rely on remembered interactions without constant uncertainty. This direct support is crucial for improving low-friction assistance and maintaining high clarity in real-time subtitles by ensuring that relevant context is always at hand.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache eviction rule that retains recently seen faces and names based on their confidence levels, but removes less frequently encountered entities after a set time to optimize memory usage while keeping the most useful information readily accessible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this local cache policy, the system can provide faster and more reliable face/name recall, reducing latency and enhancing the overall trust in the system's ability to assist without overloading the phone or cloud with unnecessary data. This leads to a smoother user experience where the wearer can confidently rely on remembered interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This clear boundary will enhance subtitle clarity by keeping sensitive and frequently used memory operations local, reducing latency and improving user trust. It will also help in managing battery usage more effectively by offloading non-critical processing to the cloud when possible.

1. **Why it benefits the core**
