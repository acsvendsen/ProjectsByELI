### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we ensure that subtitles remain clear and stable, enhancing trust in live conversations. This stability helps maintain the wearer's focus on the conversation without being distracted by rapidly changing or poorly placed text.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 subtitle placement rules based on varying levels of confidence: high confidence (always visible), medium confidence (only appear when necessary), and low confidence (disappear quickly if not confirmed).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing these rules, we can significantly improve the readability and stability of subtitles, reducing the chance of distraction or misinterpretation during conversations. This will enhance the overall user experience by making the system more reliable and less intrusive.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that confidence labels are clearly displayed, along with a concise reason for the label, helps build trust by providing transparency about the system's certainty in its memory and recognition capabilities. This enhances the wearer's understanding of when to rely on the system's assistance and when additional confirmation might be necessary.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a confidence object format that includes both a numeric score (e.g., 0.84) along with a label ("High") and a short reason ("Seen recently"). This format will provide clear, actionable information to the wearer about the reliability of the memory and recognition.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this confidence format, users can better trust the system's recommendations. This increased trust will reduce cognitive load by allowing wearers to focus on the task at hand rather than questioning the system's accuracy. It also ensures that when new faces or names are encountered, the wearer can take appropriate action with confidence.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
