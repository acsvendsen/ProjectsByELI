### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain clear and stable enough to be trusted during live conversations. By defining consistent rules for where subtitles appear on the display, we can minimize distractions while maximizing readability. This stability is crucial for maintaining trust in the system's assistive capabilities.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence speech (e.g., clear and familiar voices) that places subtitles slightly above or below the speaker’s face, and another for lower-confidence speech (e.g., less familiar voices or background noise) that places subtitles closer to the center of the display with a slight offset.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing these two rules, we can ensure that high-confidence subtitles are more discreet and do not distract from the conversation, while low-confidence subtitles are still visible enough to be useful without overwhelming the wearer. This balanced approach will enhance trust in the system’s ability to provide accurate, timely support.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing stays local on the phone minimizes latency, preserves battery life, and enhances trust by providing near-real-time assistance without cloud delays. This focus helps in maintaining a high level of clarity and reliability in subtitles, which is crucial for the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
