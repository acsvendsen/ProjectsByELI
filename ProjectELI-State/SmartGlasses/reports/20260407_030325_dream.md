### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always visible but not so prominent as to become distracting. This improves the wearer's ability to follow conversations without creating visual clutter.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement rules: 
   - For high-confidence matches (likely correct names or faces), place subtitles at the bottom of the field of view.
   - For medium-confidence matches, place subtitles slightly above eye level but not directly in the center.
   - For low-confidence matches, provide minimal visual cues such as a brief flash or subtle text overlay.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that high-confidence information is quickly visible and readable while lower confidence information is still accessible without overwhelming the wearer's field of view. It balances clarity with non-intrusiveness, enhancing the overall user experience and trust in the system’s accuracy.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
