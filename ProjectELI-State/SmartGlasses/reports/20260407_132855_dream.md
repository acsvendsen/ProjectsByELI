### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules under different confidence states will ensure that subtitles are always in a high-visibility position when needed most, enhancing the wearer's understanding and trust without becoming distracting.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 subtitle placement rules based on confidence scores: one for low-confidence text, another for medium-confidence text, and one for high-confidence text. These rules will ensure that subtitles are prioritized in a way that maintains clarity while minimizing distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing specific subtitle placement rules, the system can provide more reliable and unobtrusive support, reducing cognitive load on the wearer during conversations. This will enhance overall trust and usability, aligning with the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

This helps in maintaining trust by providing accurate and timely assistance while keeping the interface uncluttered.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache size limit of 50 entries for people, names, and topics, ensuring that the most recent and relevant interactions are prioritized and retained. This simple threshold helps in managing memory without adding unnecessary complexity to the system.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting a clear cache size limit, we can achieve faster lookup times for known entities, reducing latency and improving overall user trust. The system will be more responsive and reliable during interactions, which is crucial for maintaining the core mission of enhancing understanding in real-world scenarios.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one local-cache size limit of 50 entries for people, names, and topics to balance between recall speed and memory management.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line support enhances the wearer's ability to focus on critical information without becoming overwhelmed by too much text. This clarity supports the core mission of reducing social and cognitive friction, making real-time assistance more effective.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a priority rule that ensures subtitles always have precedence over one-line prompts in the visual display order. This can be achieved by setting a simple subtitle-vs-one-line display rule where subtitles are shown first unless explicitly hidden or suppressed by the user, and one-line prompts are displayed only when subtitles are not visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing subtitles, the wearer will remain focused on the primary information source during conversations. This reduces cognitive load and ensures that the most critical real-time text is always easily readable. The wearer can quickly refer to one-line prompts without breaking their flow of understanding.
