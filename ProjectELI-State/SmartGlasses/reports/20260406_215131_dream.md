### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always readable and do not distract from the wearer's natural interaction. This improves real-time subtitle clarity, which is a primary attractor for the project.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three fixed subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence scenarios where subtitles should be positioned at the bottom of the frame to ensure they are always visible but do not block the wearer's view; another for medium-confidence scenarios where subtitles can be placed slightly higher, reducing visual obstruction; and a third rule for low-confidence scenarios where subtitles might benefit from being displayed on the periphery or temporarily hidden.

3. **Likely payoff**

   These fixed subtitle placement rules will help maintain high real-time subtitle clarity, ensuring that important information is always accessible to the wearer without causing distraction. This will enhance the core mission of making the wearer understand better in noisy environments and reduce social friction by maintaining a discreet UX.
