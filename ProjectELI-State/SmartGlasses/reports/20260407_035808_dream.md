### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the wearer's trust in the system by ensuring subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive during conversations. Stable subtitle placement helps maintain readability, which is crucial for real-time assistance. By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we ensure that high-confidence text appears prominently while lower-confidence text is less intrusive.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple subtitle placement rule where subtitles are displayed at the bottom of the frame when they have high confidence (e.g., over 0.85), and use a slightly offset position (e.g., to the side) for lower confidence (e.g., below 0.85).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this rule, subtitles will be more readable during critical moments when they are highly confident, reducing the chance of the wearer missing important information. This improves overall trust and understanding in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving confidence display ensures that the wearer can trust the subtitled information provided by the glasses. A clear and consistent confidence format helps in reducing social awkwardness and ensuring that the user is not left guessing about the reliability of the subtitles.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**
   By providing clear and consistent confidence labels, the wearer can better understand the reliability of the subtitles in real-time conversations, thereby enhancing trust and reducing cognitive load.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving the phone-cloud boundary for live subtitle processing can ensure that subtitles remain clear, stable, and fast enough to be trusted in real-time conversations. This is critical for maintaining the wearer's trust and ensuring the subtitles are reliable.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a specific task boundary where live subtitle processing will always stay local: live subtitle processing should remain on-device to avoid any latency introduced by cloud connectivity.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By keeping live subtitle processing local, we can ensure that subtitles are near-real-time and stable, which is essential for maintaining the wearer's trust and providing a high-quality assistive experience without introducing potential delays or instability from cloud processing.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the rule for real-time subtitle processing to always occur locally on the device with no fallback to the cloud.
