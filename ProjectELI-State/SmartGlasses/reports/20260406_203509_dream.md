### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain consistent, reliable subtitle visibility for the wearer, reducing cognitive load and increasing trust in the system's ability to assist during live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two fixed subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence scenarios where subtitles should appear at the bottom of the frame with minimal overlap, and another for low-confidence scenarios where subtitles are shown slightly above the center to avoid distracting the wearer but remain visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining clear placement rules based on confidence states, the system can ensure that subtitles are always legible and do not distract from the conversation, thereby enhancing the overall user experience and trust in the system's reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
