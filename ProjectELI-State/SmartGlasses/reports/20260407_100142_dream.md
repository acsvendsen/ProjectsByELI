### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement enhances the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles, which is critical for live conversations and understanding. Stable placement reduces distractions and ensures that subtitles remain visible, contributing to the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence states (when the system is highly certain about who is speaking) and another for lower-confidence states (where the speaker is less clear or new). This ensures that subtitles are always placed in a way that maximizes visibility and readability, regardless of the confidence level.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing stable subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, the system will become more predictable and reliable. This stability will enhance trust in the subtitles, making them more useful during live interactions and contributing to the overall core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing remains on-device allows for a more consistent and predictable experience, critical for maintaining trust in real-time communication. By keeping this task local, we can ensure subtitles are always available without latency issues, which is essential for high-quality assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one subtitle placement rule that prioritizes stable, non-reactive positions over reactive adjustments based on confidence levels or gaze direction. This ensures that the subtitles remain consistent and do not distract the wearer during live conversations.

3. **Likely payoff**
