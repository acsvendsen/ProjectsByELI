# Consolidation of Core Signals

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Clarity**:
  - The subtitle text must remain clear, fast, and stable to ensure the wearer can trust them in live conversation.
  
- **Memory Trust**:
  - Memory support should be reliable without creating false certainty or corrupting recall. This includes remembering names and faces accurately.

- **Low-Friction Assistance**:
  - One-line prompts and visible support should enhance understanding without introducing command overhead or social awkwardness.

## Weakening Signals
- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: The system must balance providing useful assistive behavior with respecting privacy, ensuring it does not quietly slide into invasive capture or retention.
  
- **Latency vs Richness**: The system should provide timely help but avoid overloading the runtime with richer processing that could introduce latency.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**:
  - Display behavior must remain socially acceptable while still being readable in motion and noise. This tension needs to be managed carefully as the design evolves.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Subtitle Placement**: Revisit the optimal placement of subtitles for fast reading with minimal distraction.
- **Confidence Display**: Explore different formats for confidence display, such as labels, short reasons, or scores, that build trust without overwhelming the user.
