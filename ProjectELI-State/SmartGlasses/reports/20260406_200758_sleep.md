## Core Field Consolidation

### Mission
Create smart glasses that act as a discreet, context-aware personal assistant focusing on live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support to help the wearer understand and respond better in real-world interactions.

### Non-Negotiables
- The glasses must remain simple in early hardware.
- V1 interaction is frame-touch only.
- Use phone-first architecture with cloud optional where clearly justified.
- New faces and names should be remembered with confidence objects and reinforcement over time.
- Real-world constraints must be surfaced early, including latency, battery, privacy, social acceptability, on-device limits.

### Core Attractors
1. **Subtitle Clarity**: Keep subtitles clear, fast, and stable enough that the wearer can trust them in live conversation.
2. **Memory Trust**: Remember names, faces, and prior context accurately without creating false certainty or recall issues.
3. **Low-Friction Assistance**: Provide one-line help and visible support without creating command overhead or social awkwardness.

### Core Tensions
1. **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Helpful assistive behavior should not quietly slide into invasive capture or retention.
2. **Latency vs Richness**: The system should provide timely help without overloading with richer but slower processing options.
3. **Discreet UX vs Visual Clarity**: Display behavior must remain socially acceptable while still being readable in motion and noise.
