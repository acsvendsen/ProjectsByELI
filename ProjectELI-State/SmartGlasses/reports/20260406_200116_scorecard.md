## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focusing on delivering real-time subtitles that are clear and stable enough for live conversations while maintaining low-friction assistance without overloading the wearer with commands or social awkwardness. The core mission of discreet, context-aware personal assistance remains central, but ongoing challenges include balancing privacy against usefulness and ensuring low latency to maintain trust.
