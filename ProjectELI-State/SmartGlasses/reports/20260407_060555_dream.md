### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules for different confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always readable and stable enough for real-time use without becoming distracting. This will help maintain trust and enhance the wearer's understanding during live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 2 to 3 subtitle placement rules based on confidence level: one for high-confidence scenarios where precise placement is less critical, another for medium-confidence scenarios that require more visibility but still need to be subtle, and a third for low-confidence scenarios where subtitles should appear prominently.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these placement rules will improve the visual clarity of subtitles in various contexts, reducing distraction while maintaining readability. This will contribute to higher trust and better real-time understanding during conversations, aligning with the core mission of helping the wearer stay present and informed.

4. **Immediate next probe**
