## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant with live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 will use frame-touch interactions, rely heavily on phone processing, and avoid voice commands to maintain simplicity and social acceptability while ensuring real-time responsiveness and trust.
