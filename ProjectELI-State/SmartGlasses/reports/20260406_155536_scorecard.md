## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 is focused on providing discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The project must remain simple, reliable, and socially acceptable in its initial iteration, with an emphasis on real-time performance, low-friction interaction, and clear user trust.
