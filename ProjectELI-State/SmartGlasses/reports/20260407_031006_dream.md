### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain consistent and reliable visual clarity, which is crucial for real-time understanding in noisy environments. Stable placement reduces distraction and enhances trust by providing predictable and unobtrusive support.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that prioritizes screen center or slightly above the gaze point but does not animate based on confidence levels. This ensures subtitles remain visible without being overly intrusive, regardless of the wearer's level of understanding or the context.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By maintaining consistent and stable subtitle placement, the system will reduce cognitive load for the wearer and improve overall trust in the assistant’s ability to provide accurate information. This stability also helps in reinforcing memory recall by ensuring that important information is always easily accessible without constant repositioning.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that confidence objects are presented clearly and accurately helps build trust in the system's reliability. By using a simple numeric score, label, and short reason format for confidence display, users can better understand the certainty of the information provided, which is crucial for maintaining their faith in the assistive nature of the glasses.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a standard confidence format that uses a numeric score (e.g., 0-1), a label (e.g., High, Medium, Low), and a short reason (e.g., "Seen recently", "Weak match"). This format will help users quickly interpret the reliability of the information presented.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear and concise confidence indicators, the system can reduce user anxiety about the accuracy of the subtitles and memory recall. This clarity will enhance trust and ensure that users rely on the glasses more confidently in various social and hearing-challenged environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a specific cloud fallback boundary for live subtitle processing, we ensure that critical real-time subtitles remain stable and trustworthy even when occasional network issues arise. This focus on core functionality helps maintain the wearer's trust in the product, especially during high-stakes conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one local cache eviction or refresh policy for live subtitles such that subtitles older than 30 seconds are cached locally to ensure stability and speed during network interruptions.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will improve the reliability of real-time subtitles, ensuring they remain stable even in low-latency contexts like live conversations. By keeping a recent cache of subtitles, we reduce dependency on cloud fallbacks, which can be less reliable due to varying network conditions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
