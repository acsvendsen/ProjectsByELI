### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always legible and not distracting while maintaining trust in their accuracy.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement modes: fixed at bottom of screen, slightly above but within view when looking down, and dynamically adjust to avoid obstructing gaze direction with high confidence matches.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will enhance the wearer's trust in the subtitles by ensuring they are always visible and not disruptive during conversations. It also reduces the risk of misplacement leading to confusion or missed information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   By implementing this confidence format, the system will provide clear feedback on its certainty, helping the wearer understand when to rely on the subtitles or memory support. This improves overall trust and usability, aligning with the core attractors of subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

It also optimizes resource use by offloading less-critical tasks to the cloud where they can run more efficiently.
