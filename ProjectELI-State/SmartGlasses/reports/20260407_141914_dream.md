### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement is crucial for maintaining real-time subtitle clarity, which directly supports the core attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity." Stable placement prevents subtitles from flickering or shifting unpredictably, ensuring that the wearer can trust and easily read the subtitles in live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle position for different confidence states. For example, set a default top-right corner placement with adjustable margins based on current confidence levels. This ensures that subtitles remain consistently visible and unobtrusive while still providing clear information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining stable subtitle positions, the system will provide more reliable and trustworthy real-time subtitles. This stability enhances the wearer's ability to follow conversations in noisy environments, thereby reinforcing the core mission of making the wearer feel understood and informed without overloading them with unnecessary distractions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear and concise confidence format, we ensure that the wearer can quickly understand the reliability of the subtitles and memory support provided by the glasses. This improves trust and helps the wearer make informed decisions based on the system's certainty.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define the confidence format to include a numeric score, label, and short reason, such as "score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'; score 0.42 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'; score 0.63 + 'Medium' + 'Name heard once in introduction'."

3. **Likely payoff**

   A well-defined confidence format will help the wearer understand the reliability of the subtitles and memory support, leading to better trust and more effective use of the glasses. This reduces the risk of misunderstanding or misinterpretation, ensuring that the wearer can rely on the system's assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
