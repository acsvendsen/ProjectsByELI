# V1 Reality Check

## Decision Areas

### Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy

**Subtitle Placement**
- **Current Direction:** The glasses should prioritize subtitle placement to be usable without becoming distracting.
- **Reality Check:**
  - **Feasible Now:** Given the current hardware limitations, subtitles can be placed at the bottom of the frame or slightly off-center. This approach balances readability with social acceptability and minimizes distraction.
  - **Feasibility:** Subtitle placement should avoid covering critical visual areas (e.g., eyes) to maintain social acceptance.

**Visual Hierarchy**
- **Current Direction:** One-line subtitles should visually differ from prompts, maintaining clarity.
- **Reality Check:**
  - **Feasible Now:** Use a different color or font style for one-line prompts. For example, use bold text for prompts and regular text for subtitles.
  - **Feasibility:** Ensure the visual hierarchy is clear but not overly complex to maintain readability and social acceptability.

### Confidence Display

**Current Direction:**
- The confidence display should provide a label with a short reason or a score plus a label.

**Reality Check:**
- **Label + Short Reason:** This approach balances simplicity and trust. A simple label (e.g., "High" or "Low") paired with a brief reason (e.g., "Likely" or "Unsure") can provide clear feedback.
  - **Feasibility Now:** Implement this using a small, readable font size that does not overwhelm the wearer but still conveys meaningful information.

### Memory/Cache Policy

**Current Direction:**
- New faces and names should be automatically remembered with confidence objects, while explicit remember-touch behavior is for reinforcement or user-prioritized capture.
- Face/name memory must expose uncertainty clearly to maintain trust.

**Reality Check:**
- **Automatic vs Explicit Remember-Touch:** Automatic remembering can provide a smooth user experience but may need clear mechanisms for users to correct or refine memories.
  - **Feasible Now:** Implement an automatic remember mechanism with a low-friction option (e.g., a double-tap) for explicit confirmation. This ensures the system can learn while giving users control over their data.
- **Uncertainty Display:** Clearly indicate when memory is uncertain (e.g., using a "Question Mark" symbol or a softer color).
  - **Feasibility Now:** Implement this with minimal UI changes to avoid distraction.

### Phone/Cloud Boundary

**Current Direction:**
- The system should primarily rely on the phone for processing, with cloud support as an optional extension.
- Heavy local compute is not part of V1.

**Reality Check:**
- **Phone First:** Given the current constraints, focusing on phone-first processing makes sense. This approach reduces battery drain and thermal pressure.
  - **Feasibility Now:** Ensure that all critical processing (speech recognition, memory lookup, context handling) occurs on the phone to keep V1 lightweight.

### Interaction Constraints

**Current Direction:**
- V1 interaction should be controlled through frame touches only.

**Reality Check:**
- **Feasible Now:** Given the hardware and battery constraints, frame-touch interactions are practical. Avoiding voice commands simplifies the system and reduces complexity.
  - **Implementation:** Ensure that all interactions (e.g., confirming a memory) can be done with simple touch inputs without adding unnecessary complexity.

### Battery and Thermals

**Current Direction:**
- Early versions should not depend on heavy on-glasses compute.

**Reality Check:**
- **Feasible Now:** Keep the glasses lightweight, using minimal local processing. Heavy compute tasks should be offloaded to the phone.
  - **Implementation:** Design the firmware to minimize power draw and ensure that any local processing is optimized for low energy use.

### Privacy and Trust

**Current Direction:**
- The system should help the wearer understand better without faking understanding, while keeping privacy and consent pressure visible.

**Reality Check:**
- **Feasible Now:** Implement clear mechanisms for users to review and manage their data. Provide transparency about how memory is used and stored.
  - **Implementation:** Include a simple settings interface where users can view and manage their memories or opt-out of certain features if desired.

### Summary

Based on the current constraints and goals, the following directions are feasible:

- **Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy:**
  - Subtitle placement at the bottom or off-center.
  - One-line prompts visually distinct from subtitles.

- **Confidence Display:**
  - Label + short reason for confidence display.

- **Memory/Cache Policy:**
  - Automatic remembering with low-friction explicit confirmation.
  - Clear indication of uncertain memories.

- **Phone/Cloud Boundary:**
  - Heavy processing on the phone, minimal local compute.

- **Interaction Constraints:**
  - Frame-touch interactions only.

- **Battery and Thermals:**
  - Minimal local processing to reduce power draw.

- **Privacy and Trust:**
  - Clear mechanisms for data management and transparency about usage.

The following areas are also feasible but may require further evidence or refinement:

- **Subtitle System:** Ensure near-real-time, readable subtitles.
- **Memory System:** Support fast, trustworthy face/name recall with minimal low-value information storage.

Areas that could be a waste of time based on current constraints:

- **Voice Activation and Spoken Commands:** Not part of V1 interaction design until further evidence suggests otherwise.
