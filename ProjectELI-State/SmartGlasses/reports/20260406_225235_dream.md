### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the stability and readability of subtitles, reducing cognitive load for the wearer and ensuring they remain focused on the conversation.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define two new subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence speech (e.g., clearly spoken words) that appears at the top of the screen, and another for low-confidence speech (e.g., background noise or less clear words) that appears slightly lower to avoid distraction but remain visible.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By separating high- and low-confidence subtitles visually, wearers can more easily distinguish between spoken content they should pay attention to and background noise, reducing cognitive strain and improving overall understanding in noisy environments.
