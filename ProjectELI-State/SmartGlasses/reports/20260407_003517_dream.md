### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules under different confidence states ensures that subtitles remain clear and readable while not becoming distracting. This helps in maintaining the wearer's trust by ensuring that critical information is always easily accessible, which is crucial for real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 to 5 subtitle placement rules based on varying levels of confidence:
   - High confidence: Fixed position at a standard readable height.
   - Medium confidence: Slightly lower or higher than the fixed position to avoid distractions but remain visible.
   - Low confidence: Dynamic adjustment with minimal vertical movement to reduce visual noise.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By refining subtitle placement based on confidence, we can ensure that subtitles are always in a prime viewing area without causing unnecessary distraction. This will enhance the wearer's trust in the system’s ability to provide accurate and useful information during conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving the confidence display format ensures that the wearer can trust the system's memory and recall without overloading them with unnecessary details. A clear, concise confidence label and short reason will help the wearer understand the reliability of the information provided, thereby increasing their overall trust in the product.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a consistent confidence display format that includes a numeric score, a confidence label (e.g., "High," "Medium," "Low"), and a short reason for the confidence level. This simple change will enhance the clarity of the system's memory recall while keeping the information digestible.

3. **Likely payoff**

By providing a clear and concise confidence display, the wearer can quickly assess the reliability of the information provided by the glasses. This will reduce uncertainty and increase trust in the system’s memory support, leading to more frequent use and higher satisfaction.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring robust memory cache and lookup policies strengthens the core mission by enhancing the reliability and trustworthiness of name and face recall, which is crucial for low-friction assistance and real-time subtitle clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining clear boundaries for what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud processing, we can ensure that the system remains lightweight and socially acceptable while still delivering high-value assistive behavior. This helps maintain trust by avoiding unnecessary privacy invasions and ensures efficient use of limited on-device resources.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one specific task boundary for live subtitle processing: all face recognition and name recall should be handled phone-locally to avoid cloud latency, while memory archive syncs and conversation snapshots can fall back to the cloud when necessary.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitle processing local, we can ensure real-time responsiveness and maintain clear, stable subtitles that the wearer can trust in live conversations. This will enhance the core attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity" without compromising on privacy or battery life.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

   By defining a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line prompts, we can ensure that critical information is always visible while reducing cognitive overload. This helps maintain focus during conversations and enhances the wearer's understanding without overwhelming them with too much text.

3. **Likely payoff**
