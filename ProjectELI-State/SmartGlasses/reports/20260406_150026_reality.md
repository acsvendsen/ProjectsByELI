## Reality Check for SmartGlasses V1 Direction

### Core Mission and Constraints
- **Core Mission**: Discreet assistive glasses focusing on real-time subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support.
- **V1 Interaction**: Frame touches only. No voice activation or spoken commands.
- **Phone/Cloud Architecture**: Phone-first architecture with optional cloud processing.

### Subtitle Placement
**Feasible Now**: 
- **Subtitle Display**: Place subtitles directly below the primary text to minimize distraction while still providing clarity. This approach respects the user's context and minimizes intrusion, making it suitable for V1.
  
  - **Reasoning**: Direct placement ensures that subtitles are easily readable without overwhelming the wearer’s field of view.

**Likely Waste of Time**: 
- **Subtle Text Overlay**: Adding subtle text overlays or semi-transparent subtext layers. This approach could be too distracting and might not provide a clear enough visual separation between the primary content and the subtitles.
  
  - **Reasoning**: Subtle overlays might blend with the main text, leading to confusion and reducing usability.

### Confidence Display
**Feasible Now**: 
- **Label + Reason**: Use labels such as "high confidence" or "low confidence" along with a short reason why. For example: "High (Face match), low (Contextual clue)."

  - **Reasoning**: This method provides clear feedback on the reliability of memory recall, enhancing user trust without overwhelming them.

**Feasible Later**: 
- **Confidence Score + Label**: Introducing a numeric score along with the label. This could be more complex but might provide a finer gradation of confidence.
  
  - **Reasoning**: While this adds complexity, it can offer users a deeper understanding of the system’s reliability over time.

### Memory/Cache Policy
**Feasible Now**: 
- **Contextual Caching**: Cache context and memory objects on the phone to reduce cloud dependency. This approach ensures that critical information is available offline.
  
  - **Reasoning**: Contextual caching balances battery usage with the need for persistent memory, making it a practical solution.

**Assumptions Needing Evidence**: 
- **Cloud Storage for Rare Events**: Investigate scenarios where heavy processing or large data sets might benefit from cloud storage, such as during training or deep analysis.
  
  - **Reasoning**: Determine specific use cases where the benefits of cloud storage outweigh the potential drawbacks in terms of battery and latency.

### Phone/Cloud Boundary
**Feasible Now**: 
- **Phone First Architecture**: Use the phone for primary processing with optional cloud support. This ensures that V1 remains focused on core functionality without overloading the glasses.
  
  - **Reasoning**: This approach minimizes thermal and power usage while ensuring robust performance.

**Assumptions Needing Evidence**: 
- **Local Inference**: Explore scenarios where local inference can be beneficial, such as in noisy environments or when network connectivity is poor. 

  - **Reasoning**: Determine the feasibility of integrating limited on-glasses inference without compromising battery life and thermal management.

### Visual Hierarchy
**Feasible Now**: 
- **Subtitles Dominate**: Ensure subtitles dominate the visual hierarchy to maintain focus while still providing clear information.
  
  - **Reasoning**: Dominating the hierarchy ensures that subtitles remain the primary form of assistance, minimizing distractions from other UI elements.

**Likely Waste of Time**: 
- **Complex UI Layers**: Adding multiple layers or complex UI interactions. This could be overly complicated and detract from usability.
  
  - **Reasoning**: Simplistic design is more effective in maintaining user trust and reducing cognitive load.

### Interaction Constraints
- **Frame Touches Only**: Stick to frame touches for all V1 interactions, avoiding voice commands entirely until future iterations.

  - **Reasoning**: Frame touch interaction ensures a low-friction, discreet experience that aligns with the core mission of the glasses.

**Feasible Later**: 
- **Limited Voice Commands**: Consider integrating limited voice commands in future versions to enhance user convenience. 

  - **Reasoning**: Evaluate specific use cases where voice input might provide significant benefits without introducing unnecessary complexity.

### Summary
- **Subtitle Placement and Visual Hierarchy**: Focus on clear, non-distracting placement.
- **Confidence Display**: Label + reason is feasible now; consider score + label for later.
- **Memory/Cache Policy**: Use contextual caching with phone-first architecture. Explore cloud storage for rare events.
- **Phone/Cloud Boundary**: Maintain a phone-first approach while exploring local inference for specific use cases.

By adhering to these constraints and focusing on core functionality, the project can achieve its mission without overcomplicating the design or introducing unnecessary complexity.
