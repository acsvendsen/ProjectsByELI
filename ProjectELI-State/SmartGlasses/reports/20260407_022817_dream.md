### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain the wearer's trust in the system by providing consistent, reliable information during live conversations. Stable and predictable subtitle placement minimizes distractions and ensures that subtitles remain readable and understandable, which is crucial for the core mission of helping the wearer understand real-world interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement mode where subtitles are always displayed at the bottom center of the field of view, with slight adjustments based on head movement but no reactive changes to text position or size. This simple rule ensures that subtitles remain in a predictable location, reducing visual distractions and improving readability.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this stable subtitle placement rule, users will experience more consistent and readable subtitles during live conversations. This will help maintain their trust in the system and ensure they can rely on the real-time information provided by the glasses without visual interruptions or changes that might be distracting.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format in V1 will enhance trust by clearly indicating the reliability of the provided subtitle content to the wearer. This ensures that subtitles are only displayed when sufficiently confident, reducing uncertainty and increasing the user's reliance on the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a consistent confidence format that includes a numeric score, a label for high-level certainty (e.g., "High", "Medium", "Low"), and a short reason (e.g., "Seen recently", "Weak match"). For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By clearly displaying confidence, the system will reduce user doubt about subtitle accuracy, increase trust, and ensure that subtitles are only displayed when sufficiently reliable. This improves overall user satisfaction and the perceived reliability of the product.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By optimizing the phone-cloud boundary for live subtitle processing, we can ensure that subtitles remain stable, clear, and real-time even when the device is under thermal or battery constraints. This will maintain trust in the system's ability to provide accurate information during critical interactions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific task-boundary rule: for live subtitle processing, always keep this task on-device locally unless the cloud can provide faster or more robust results without significant latency impact.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will ensure that subtitles remain stable and real-time, enhancing user trust and satisfaction during critical interactions. It will also reduce thermal and battery strain, allowing for longer use in noisy environments where clear, accurate subtitles are essential.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear visual hierarchy rule for subtitles versus one-line support, we ensure that the wearer can easily distinguish between ongoing conversations and contextual prompts. This clarity supports real-time subtitle reliability and low-friction assistance, both critical attractors.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a priority rule in V1 where subtitles always take precedence over one-line prompts, but allow one-line prompts to be visible as needed without obscuring the main conversation text.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This visual hierarchy will reduce cognitive overload by ensuring that the wearer can focus on the most important information—subtitles—and only glance at contextual support when necessary. It enhances trust in real-time subtitles and reduces social awkwardness, aligning with the core attractors of subtitle clarity and low-friction assistance.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
