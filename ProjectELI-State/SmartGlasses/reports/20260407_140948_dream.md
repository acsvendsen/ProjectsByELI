### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules, we can ensure that subtitles remain stable and unobtrusive while providing clear, useful information to the wearer. This improves real-time subtitle clarity and reduces distraction, aligning with our primary attractor of "Real-time subtitle clarity."

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence speech (where subtitles are stable and less intrusive) and another for lower-confidence speech (where subtitles may be more frequent but remain unobtrusive). This fine-tuning will help in managing visual clutter while maintaining readability.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these placement rules will reduce the likelihood of subtitle distractions during conversations, thereby enhancing the wearer's trust in the system. Clear and stable subtitles improve overall understanding without overwhelming the wearer, which is crucial for real-time clarity and memory support.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the confidence display format in V1 will enhance the wearer's trust by providing clear, concise, and accurate indicators of the system's certainty about the information being presented. This will help maintain a balance between useful assistance and avoiding overconfidence that could lead to social awkwardness or incorrect assumptions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a confidence format that uses a numeric score combined with a label and short reason for each piece of displayed information. For example, "Score 0.84 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'; Score 0.42 + 'Low' + 'Weak match'; Score 0.63 + 'Medium' + 'Name heard once in introduction'."

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear and concise confidence indicators, the system can help the wearer better understand the reliability of the displayed information. This will enhance trust and reduce social awkwardness, making the system more useful and acceptable for daily use.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Strengthening memory trust by optimizing local cache size ensures that likely new-person memories are retained reliably while not overloading the phone's storage and processing capacity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a local cache size limit for person-memory objects, ensuring that each new memory is stored but not exceeding a threshold of 10-20 recent memories.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By limiting the number of stored memories to a manageable size, the system can maintain fast lookup performance and reduce the risk of privacy concerns from storing too much information locally.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud for live subtitle processing, we can ensure that subtitles remain responsive and reliable in real-time scenarios. This helps maintain trust by providing fast, accurate, and stable subtitle assistance, which is critical for the core mission of reducing social and cognitive friction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a single task boundary: live subtitle processing. For this task, keep all subtitle-related data and logic phone-local until explicit fallback to cloud is needed. This simplifies the initial implementation while ensuring real-time responsiveness.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping live subtitle processing local, we can reduce latency, ensure stability, and maintain high-quality subtitles that are crucial for the wearer's understanding in noisy environments. This will enhance the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity and help strengthen trust by providing reliable support when needed most.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one phone-local vs cloud fallback boundary for exactly live subtitle processing.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line prompts enhances the wearer's ability to focus on critical information without being overwhelmed by too much text. This clarity supports real-time subtitle readability, which is crucial for maintaining trust in live conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a display precedence rule where subtitles are shown at the top of the field of view with one-line prompts below them, ensuring that subtitles take priority when both types of information are available simultaneously.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing subtitles over one-line prompts, the wearer can focus more on what is being said without visual distractions, which improves overall comprehension and trust in the assistive system. This clarity also reduces cognitive load during live interactions, making the glasses a more reliable tool for real-world use.

4. **Immediate next probe**
