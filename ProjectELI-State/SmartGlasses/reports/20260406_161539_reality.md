- The project focuses on live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support.

**Feasible Now**: 
- Placing subtitles more prominently on the glasses can enhance readability without significantly increasing distraction. Current hardware constraints allow for this, as it does not require advanced AR capabilities.

**Feasible Now**: 
- Using both a label and a short reason to display confidence can provide users with clear information on the reliability of subtitles or memory. This is simple enough to implement without heavy on-glasses processing.

**Feasible Now**: 
- Implementing a recency-first policy means that recently encountered entities or conversations are prioritized. This approach is straightforward to implement on the phone and does not require complex local processing.

**Feasible Now**:
