### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain clear, readable, and stable enough to be trusted in live conversation. This directly supports the primary attractor of real-time subtitle clarity, enhancing the wearer's ability to follow conversations without distraction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that prioritizes top-center alignment for all confidence states except very low-confidence instances where subtitles might temporarily drop to the bottom center to avoid overlap with text or visuals. This ensures that subtitles remain visible and unobtrusive while maintaining readability.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By maintaining consistent, high-quality subtitle visibility, users will trust the subtitles more in noisy environments, improving overall comprehension and interaction quality without overwhelming the wearer with distractions. This enhances the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity and supports a low-friction assistive experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

   Improving the confidence display format helps ensure that the wearer can trust the subtitles and memory support by clearly indicating the reliability of the information provided. A well-defined confidence object will reduce uncertainty and enhance the wearer's confidence in the system, making the assistive behavior more reliable and useful.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a consistent and clear format for confidence objects that combines a numeric score with a label and short reason. For example: "score 0.85 + 'High' + 'Seen recently'". This simple change will provide the wearer with immediate context about the certainty of the information, making the system more trustworthy.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this confidence format, the system can build stronger trust over time as the wearer sees reliable and accurate subtitles and memory support. This increased trust will likely lead to higher daily-use value and better overall user experience, aligning with the core mission of enhancing understanding in real-world interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
