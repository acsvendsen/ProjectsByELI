# Consolidation of Core Signals for SmartGlasses V1

## Strengthening Signals
1. **Subtitle Clarity**: Enhance subtitle quality to ensure they remain clear, stable, and trust-preserving in live conversations.
2. **Memory Support**: Improve the ability to remember names, faces, and prior context without overconfidence or corrupted recall.
3. **Low-Friction Assistance**: Optimize one-line prompts for fast, low-distraction support that does not interfere with natural conversation flow.

## Weakening Signals
1. **Always-On Sensing vs Battery/Thermals**: Prioritize minimal always-on sensing to reduce battery and thermal strain, focusing on critical functionalities.
2. **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Balance assistive behavior against privacy concerns, ensuring the system remains helpful without sliding into invasive data capture.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
1. **Drift Risk of Gimmickry**: Ensure the project does not devolve into a gimmicky product that lacks real-world usefulness.
2. **Interaction Sprawl**: Avoid adding new command surfaces or hardware complexity before V1 is fully optimized for core functionality.
3. **Feature Sprawl**: Focus on improving subtitle quality, memory trust, and low-friction UX rather than broad feature expansion.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
1. **Automatic New-Person Memory**: Reinforce the automatic capture of likely new people during introductions when supported by context.
2. **Speaker Focus Logic**: Implement speaker focus based on audio cues and visual observation to prioritize subtitle display, even if multi-person interactions are not fully supported.

## Concrete Tensions Around Subtitle Quality, Confidence Trust, Automatic New-Person Memory, Memory Reinforcement, Fast Lookup, Privacy, Latency, Battery, Visual UX
1. **Subtitle Quality**: Ensure subtitles remain readable, stable, and trust-preserving.
2. **Confidence Display**: Maintain clear and honest confidence indicators to build user trust without overconfidence or false certainty.
3. **Automatic New-Person Memory**: Balance the automatic capture of new people with manual correction paths to avoid fragmented memory.
4. **Memory Reinforcement**: Strengthen existing memories through repeated encounters while preserving stability.
5. **Fast Lookup**: Implement efficient fast lookup across key entities like people, names, and topics without overwhelming the user.
6. **Privacy Management**: Ensure privacy controls are clear and easily accessible, allowing users to review and correct stored information.
7. **Latency Considerations**: Address real-time response requirements while minimizing latency that could break trust.
8. **Battery Optimization**: Minimize on-glasses processing to reduce battery drain without compromising core functionality.
9. **Visual UX**: Design the subtitle display and memory prompts for minimal distraction, ensuring they do not overwhelm the wearer.

## No Fluff
- Focus solely on improving the core functionality of live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support.
- Avoid speculative features or hardware improvements that are not directly aligned with these core goals.

By consolidating these signals, we can ensure that SmartGlasses V1 remains focused on delivering a robust, reliable, and discreet personal assistant for real-world conversations.
