### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining subtitle placement rules, we ensure that subtitles remain stable and unobtrusive during live conversations. This stability builds trust and ensures that the wearer can rely on the subtitles without distraction, aligning with our primary attractor of real-time subtitle clarity.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three fixed subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence speaker (always visible), one for medium-confidence speakers (visible only when needed), and one for low-confidence or background speakers (minimal visibility). This allows the system to prioritize clarity and focus while reducing visual clutter.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these fixed subtitle placement rules will reduce cognitive overload during live conversations, making it easier for the wearer to follow the conversation without distractions. This improvement directly supports our core mission of helping the wearer understand better in noisy environments and dense multi-person contexts.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the local memory cache threshold for face/name recall ensures that relevant person memories are consistently available to the wearer, enhancing the trustworthiness of name and face recall during interactions. This improvement helps maintain a balance between privacy and utility, ensuring that the system does not rely too heavily on cloud services or leak personal data unnecessarily.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a local cache threshold for face/name memory objects that balances frequent access with limited storage, ensuring that the most frequently recalled names and faces are cached locally while less relevant memories are evicted to save space.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting a clear threshold for local caching, we can reduce latency in name recall during live interactions, improve the consistency of memory support, and enhance user trust by providing faster, more reliable memory assistance without overloading the device's storage or compromising privacy.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one cache eviction rule for face/name memory objects that evicts less frequently accessed memories after a specified period to maintain a balance between utility and storage constraints.
