## Project Summary
The project is focused on creating discreet smart glasses that provide live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The current V1 will use frame-touch interaction exclusively and prioritize the phone for processing with minimal local compute.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery, thermal pressure, weight, social acceptability
- progress: The glasses remain simple with a focus on minimal local processing. The design prioritizes frame-touch interaction to keep hardware requirements low.
- next focus: Refine subtitle placement and ensure it is visually prominent without overwhelming the wearer.
- confidence: 0.9
