### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Stable subtitle placement rules enhance the wearer's ability to read subtitles without distraction. By defining consistent and unambiguous placement, the system reduces cognitive load and ensures that important information is always visible and understandable.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 fixed subtitle placement modes: one at the bottom center, one slightly above the center, and one aligned with the speaker's face but not covering their mouth or eyes.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Users will experience more consistent and readable subtitles, reducing the cognitive effort required to follow along in conversations. This leads to a more seamless and less distracting user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

   By refining the confidence display format, we can ensure that the system's reliability is communicated clearly to the wearer, thereby increasing trust in the assistant's capabilities. This will help the wearer better understand when they should focus on the subtitles and when additional context might be needed.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple numeric score format for confidence levels, where scores are rounded to one decimal place with a descriptive label and a short reason.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A clear and concise confidence display will reduce ambiguity and increase the wearer's trust in the system. This can lead to more reliable usage of the glasses in critical situations, such as during important meetings or when understanding complex conversations is essential.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
