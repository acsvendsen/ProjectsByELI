### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring subtitles are placed consistently and unobtrusively helps maintain a low-friction experience for the wearer, reducing distractions while enhancing their ability to understand and participate in conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one fixed at the bottom of the frame (default) and another slightly above center but only if confidence is high enough to warrant more prominent visibility.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing a balanced approach, this rule will optimize readability while minimizing visual intrusiveness, thus maintaining the wearer's focus on their surroundings and interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a fixed position rule for subtitles at the bottom of the frame as the default, and a placement fallback rule that centers them slightly above when the confidence state justifies more prominence.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By setting a local cache threshold for name recall, we ensure that frequently encountered names are quickly available even when the glasses cannot communicate with the phone. This reduces latency and maintains high trust by providing immediate responses.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a recency-based eviction policy where recently seen names are kept in a local cache on the glasses for quick recall, while older or less frequently encountered names are evicted to make space for new entries.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that common interactions with friends and acquaintances are handled swiftly without relying on cloud communication, which can be slow or unreliable. It also maintains a clean and uncluttered local memory system.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one recency threshold for face/name memory caching on the glasses, prioritizing names seen within the last 24 hours.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By carefully defining which memory lookups and face/name recognition processes stay on the phone versus fall back to the cloud, we can ensure that the system remains lightweight and battery-efficient while still providing reliable and timely assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define specific thresholds for local cache sizes and fallback boundaries for memory lookups based on user interaction patterns and confidence levels.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This decision will help maintain a balance between real-time performance and privacy, ensuring that the system remains useful without overloading the glasses or draining the battery significantly.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a threshold for local caching of recently interacted faces and names to minimize cloud requests while ensuring timely responses.
