### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the overall user experience by ensuring that subtitles remain stable and readable during dynamic interactions. This stability is crucial for maintaining trust, especially in noisy environments where real-time understanding is critical.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two fixed subtitle placement modes: one for low-confidence states (e.g., background placement) and another for high-confidence states (e.g., foreground placement). This simple distinction can help keep subtitles readable without overwhelming the wearer's field of view.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing a clear visual hierarchy that respects the wearer’s focus, this change can significantly reduce distraction during conversations. It also helps maintain a balance between readability and non-intrusiveness, which is essential for real-time understanding in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display to clearly convey uncertainty for new or likely new people, we enhance the trustworthiness of the system in noisy environments and memory-challenged situations. This ensures that the wearer does not over-rely on potentially incorrect information, thereby maintaining their understanding and engagement during conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Implement a confidence format that includes a label (e.g., "Low") along with a short reason (e.g., "Name heard once in introduction"). This simple change will allow the system to explicitly indicate when names or faces are recognized but may be uncertain, helping the wearer to better manage their expectations.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This improvement will reduce instances of the wearer misinterpreting uncertain information as certainty, thereby decreasing misunderstandings and enhancing overall confidence in the smart glasses' ability to assist without overstepping its bounds. It will also help maintain a low-friction UX by ensuring that the system remains transparent about its level of certainty.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving the local cache size for face/name memory ensures that frequently encountered people are always quickly accessible, enhancing real-time subtitle quality and confidence in recognition. This reduces latency and improves the wearer's trust in the system.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define a small threshold (e.g., 5-10 recent interactions) for local caching of face/name memory objects on the phone. This allows the system to quickly retrieve recently seen names, reducing the need for cloud queries and improving response time.

3. **Likely payoff**
   By caching frequently encountered people locally, we can significantly reduce lookup latency, ensuring that the wearer receives subtitle and confidence information more reliably in real-time. This enhances the overall user experience by making memory recall faster and more consistent.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a clear boundary for what stays phone-local versus what falls back to cloud during live subtitle processing, the system can ensure real-time responsiveness and reliability while maintaining low latency and battery efficiency.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific task boundary: live subtitle processing should be handled entirely on the phone due to its real-time requirements. Only critical data or heavy computation steps that cannot be locally processed should fall back to the cloud.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This decision will ensure that subtitles remain stable and fast, enhancing user trust and understanding in noisy environments. By keeping subtitle processing local, we can avoid latency issues and ensure the system remains responsive even under high thermal conditions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

1. **Why it benefits the core**
