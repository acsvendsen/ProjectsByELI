### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the wearer's ability to read subtitles without distraction, thereby increasing the trust in the system during live conversations. Stable and strategic placement ensures that the subtitles are always visible and unobtrusive, which is crucial for real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two fixed subtitle placement modes: one for high-confidence situations (e.g., known speakers or familiar contexts) where subtitles can be placed slightly lower in the field of view to save space, and another for low-confidence situations (new faces, ambiguous contexts) where subtitles should appear more prominently at the bottom of the screen.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining these fixed placement modes based on confidence levels, the system will become more reliable and less distracting, leading to higher trust in the wearer's ability to follow conversations accurately. This can significantly reduce cognitive load during interactions, making the product more useful and socially acceptable.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
