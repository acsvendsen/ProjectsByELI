### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules in V1 will enhance the wearer's trust in the system, making it more reliable and less distracting. This stability is crucial for the core mission of providing real-time, readable subtitles that aid in understanding conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement rule that keeps subtitles at the bottom center of the field of view, regardless of head or eye movement. This rule ensures consistency and prevents subtitles from shifting unexpectedly during speech or motion.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By maintaining consistent subtitle placement, the system will be more predictable, reducing cognitive load on the wearer. This predictability can lead to higher trust in the system's reliability, making it a valuable tool for understanding conversations in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear, consistent confidence display helps build trust in the system's reliability and accuracy, which is crucial for the wearer to feel comfortable relying on subtitles and memory support.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence format that uses a numeric score (0-1), a label ("Low", "Medium", "High"), and a short reason description.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A clear, consistent confidence display will help the wearer understand when to trust the subtitles more or less, improving overall user experience and trust in the system's capabilities.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that new faces and names are automatically remembered with confidence objects enhances the trustworthiness of the memory support system. This helps in maintaining a consistent and reliable user experience, reinforcing the core mission of providing accurate and useful personal assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple entity-linking rule where any face or name encountered is automatically linked to an existing person-memory object if there is a strong match, with no explicit remember-touch required for automatic new-person memory.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will reduce the cognitive load on the user by eliminating the need for them to manually capture every new face or name. It will also improve the system's ability to provide relevant and timely assistance, thereby enhancing overall trust in the product.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one entity-linking rule for attaching new evidence to an existing person-memory object based on a strong match threshold.

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By carefully defining where processing happens on the phone versus in the cloud, we can optimize the system's real-time performance and battery usage while maintaining privacy and ensuring low social friction. This decision will directly impact the subtitle clarity and memory trust attractors by balancing the need for timely responses with the constraints of battery and thermal management.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear phone/cloud fallback boundary specifically for live subtitle processing, where minor errors or latency are acceptable but real-time responsiveness is crucial.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By ensuring that critical real-time subtitle processing stays on the phone, we can maintain high subtitle clarity and stability without overloading the cloud. This will help in reducing battery drain and thermal stress while maintaining a low-friction UX for the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring clear visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line prompts enhances the wearer's ability to focus on essential information without feeling overwhelmed or distracted. This improves overall understanding and trust in the system.
