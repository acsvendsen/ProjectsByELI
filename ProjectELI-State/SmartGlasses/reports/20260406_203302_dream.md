### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain a consistent, predictable experience for the wearer, reducing cognitive load and ensuring that important information is always visible and reliable in noisy or complex environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: low-confidence subtitles should be placed at the bottom of the screen, medium-confidence subtitles can overlap with each other but remain readable, and high-confidence subtitles can have a slightly higher priority placement.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining clear and consistent placement rules, the system will provide a more reliable and predictable experience, reducing distractions and ensuring that the wearer can trust the subtitles in dynamic situations like meetings or noisy environments. This improves the overall assistive value of the product without overwhelming the user with too much information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining how confidence is displayed, we can ensure that the wearer understands the reliability of the information provided by the glasses in real-time interactions. This clarity builds trust and ensures that the subtitles and memory support are used effectively without overloading the wearer with unnecessary or uncertain information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a simple numeric score next to each confidence label, such as "High," "Medium," or "Low." For example, display "High (0.85)" for very certain identifications and "Low (0.3)" for weaker matches. This provides immediate context on the reliability of the information.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Improved confidence display will help the wearer quickly assess the credibility of subtitles and memory recall. It reduces ambiguity, builds trust in the system, and ensures that the glasses are used as intended without causing confusion or distrust.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
