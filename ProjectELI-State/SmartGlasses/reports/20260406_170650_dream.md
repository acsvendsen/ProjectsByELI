### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement helps maintain trust in real-time communication, which is crucial for the wearer to rely on the glasses in noisy environments or when hearing is impaired.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence content and another for lower-confidence content.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By having distinct placement rules based on confidence, the system can prioritize more critical information in a way that minimizes distraction but ensures clarity, thereby improving overall trust and utility.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

3. **Likely payoff**

4. **Immediate next probe**

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing remains on-device can reduce latency, enhance reliability, and maintain a discreet user experience by avoiding cloud delays or potential privacy concerns.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific subtitle placement rule for V1 that prioritizes on-device processing over cloud fallback, ensuring subtitles are displayed in real-time without any delay from network requests.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitle processing local, the system can provide faster and more reliable subtitle rendering, reducing the risk of missed speech segments or degraded audio-to-text performance due to network latency. This enhances the core value proposition of real-time clarity and trustworthiness in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.
