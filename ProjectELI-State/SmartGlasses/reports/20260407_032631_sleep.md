5. **Fast Lookup**: Balance the need for fast lookup with the richness of information provided by the system.
6. **Privacy**: Respect user privacy while providing useful assistive behavior.
7. **Latency**: Provide timely responses without overwhelming the wearer with too much information at once.
8. **Battery**: Manage battery usage carefully, especially given the importance of real-time performance.
9. **Visual UX**: Ensure that the visual hierarchy and placement of subtitles do not become distracting.
