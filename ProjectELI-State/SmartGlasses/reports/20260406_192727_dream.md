### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles are always clear, readable, and unobtrusive, enhancing the wearer's ability to follow conversations without distraction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define three fixed subtitle placement rules for different confidence states: high-confidence static placement, medium-confidence dynamic adjustment, and low-confidence temporary hiding.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - These rules will ensure that subtitles remain stable and clear even during challenging speech recognition scenarios, thereby maintaining the wearer's trust in the system's ability to provide accurate real-time support.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By refining the confidence display format, we can ensure that the system provides clear and trustworthy feedback to the wearer, which is crucial for maintaining their trust in the assistant's reliability. A well-defined confidence format will help users understand when they should pay more attention or seek clarification, thereby enhancing the overall user experience.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Introduce a simple numeric score with corresponding labels and short reasons. For example, use scores like 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"; 0.42 + "Low" + "Weak match"; 0.63 + "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction".

3. **Likely payoff**

   A clearly defined confidence format will help users better understand the system's certainty, reducing misunderstandings and increasing trust. This clarity can also guide user behavior by highlighting when additional confirmation might be necessary.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Improving memory cache policy will enhance the trustworthiness of name recall and context support, ensuring that the wearer can rely on accurate and timely information without overburdening their cognitive load.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a local cache eviction rule based on recency and confidence score: Evict items from the cache if they have low confidence scores (e.g., below 0.5) or if they are older than 7 days, but keep high-confidence memories for longer periods.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - This change will reduce memory overhead while maintaining essential information that has been seen recently and is highly confident, thus enhancing the overall trust in name recall and conversation memory without compromising on daily-use utility.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Set one local cache eviction rule for face/name memory in V1.

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By carefully defining where processing happens on the phone versus in the cloud, we ensure that the system remains lightweight and socially acceptable while still delivering essential real-time assistance. This balance is crucial for maintaining trust and ensuring that the assistive behavior does not become a burden or an invasion of privacy.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define one specific task boundary where processing should be phone-local versus cloud-fallback, focusing on live subtitle processing as the primary V1 task.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By optimizing for real-time subtitle clarity and stability in live conversation, we can significantly enhance the core mission of providing clear and reliable subtitles without compromising battery life or privacy concerns. This focus will ensure that the system is both useful and discreet, aligning with user expectations and project constraints.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one phone-local vs cloud fallback boundary for exactly one task such as live subtitle processing.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Enhancing the visual hierarchy between subtitles and one-line prompts ensures that the wearer can focus on the most critical information without feeling overwhelmed. Clear prioritization helps in maintaining high trust by avoiding unnecessary distractions, which is crucial for real-time understanding and interaction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear visual precedence rule where subtitles are always displayed prominently but not overlapping with one-line prompts, ensuring that one-line support does not overshadow the critical subtitle text during conversations.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitles as the primary focus while allowing for essential context in one-line prompts, the system will provide a clearer and more reliable visual experience, reducing cognitive load and increasing overall usability. This can significantly enhance the wearer's ability to follow along in noisy or complex environments without feeling bombarded with too much information.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define a visual precedence rule where subtitles are always displayed prominently and do not overlap with one-line prompts.
