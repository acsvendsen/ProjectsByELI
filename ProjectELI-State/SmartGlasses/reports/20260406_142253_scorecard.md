## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware smart glasses system that provides live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 will prioritize frame-touch interaction over voice commands, rely heavily on the phone for processing, and limit heavy on-glasses compute to minimize battery drain and thermal issues.
