## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant for smart glasses with an initial emphasis on subtitle quality, memory trust, and low-friction UX. The current V1 guardrails are strictly adhered to, including frame-touch interactions only and phone-first architecture.

- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The glasses are kept simple with a focus on text display and basic sensors. The frame-touch interaction constraint is well adhered to in V1.
- next focus: Continue optimizing subtitle placement and ensure minimal heat generation from the hardware.
