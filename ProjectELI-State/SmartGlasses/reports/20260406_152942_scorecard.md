## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 will use frame-touch interactions exclusively, rely heavily on the phone for processing, and minimize on-glasses computation to ensure social acceptability and minimal battery drain.
