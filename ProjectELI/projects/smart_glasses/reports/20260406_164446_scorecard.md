## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project remains focused on delivering discreet, context-aware personal assistance with real-time subtitles, face/name recall, and conversation memory. The V1 is centered around frame-touch interaction, minimal on-glasses compute, and a phone-first architecture to ensure lightweight, socially acceptable glasses while maintaining utility and trust.
