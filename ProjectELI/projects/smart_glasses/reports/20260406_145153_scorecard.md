## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focusing on V1 release with key features like real-time subtitles and face/name recall. The interaction remains touch-first, and the glasses primarily act as a display with minimal local processing. Real-world constraints are being managed by leveraging phone-based heavy processing while maintaining simplicity in hardware and interactions.
