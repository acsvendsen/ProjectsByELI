## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on V1 implementation with frame-touch interaction, phone-first architecture, and minimal on-glasses compute. The current progress shows some concrete next steps but needs more detailed testing to address core tensions such as subtitle quality vs battery life and memory trust vs privacy.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: weight and social acceptability
- progress: The hardware stack remains focused on minimizing complexity and weight to ensure social acceptability. The current plan to use frame-touch interaction and rely heavily on the phone for processing is aligned with the goal.
- next focus: Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display, as suggested in the Dream Report.
