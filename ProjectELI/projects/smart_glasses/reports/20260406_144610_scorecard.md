## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focusing on delivering discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 release will remain lightweight and socially acceptable, with frame-touch interactions as the primary method of control. Key challenges include ensuring real-time subtitle quality, managing battery life, and maintaining user trust while respecting privacy.
