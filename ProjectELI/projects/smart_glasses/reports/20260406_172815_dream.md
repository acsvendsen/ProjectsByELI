1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring a clear, consistent confidence display helps build trust in the system's capabilities. By defining specific formats for confidence labels and short reasons, the wearer can quickly understand the reliability of the information provided by the glasses. This enhances the overall user experience and ensures that the wearer feels supported without overloading them with unnecessary details.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple, two-part confidence format: `score + label`. The score represents the certainty level (e.g., 0.84), while the label provides a qualitative assessment (e.g., "High"). This format allows for clear and concise feedback without overwhelming the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing this simple confidence format, the system can provide more reliable and understandable information to the wearer. This will enhance trust in the glasses' capabilities and improve overall user satisfaction. The ability to quickly assess the reliability of subtitles or memory support will make interactions smoother and more natural for the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one confidence format candidate for V1: `score + label`.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

   Define a local cache size limit for frequently encountered names, setting a threshold based on recency and frequency. For example, names seen more than 5 times in recent conversations or within the last week could be stored locally with high confidence objects.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By caching names locally, the system can reduce latency for frequent interactions, improving real-time subtitle quality and memory recall speed. This enhances trust as the wearer consistently receives timely and accurate information, reducing instances of missed connections due to delays.
