## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support in V1. The glasses will use frame-touch interaction and rely primarily on the phone for processing to maintain simplicity and manage battery life.
