## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 is focused on delivering discreet assistive features such as live subtitles, face/name recall, and conversation memory using a phone-first architecture. The project is making steady progress but faces key constraints around battery, thermal pressure, and social acceptability.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and social acceptability
- progress: The glasses remain simple with minimal hardware dependencies. The focus is on maintaining a low profile and ensuring the design aligns with real-world constraints.
- next focus: Optimize subtitle placement to balance readability and distraction.
- confidence: 0.8
