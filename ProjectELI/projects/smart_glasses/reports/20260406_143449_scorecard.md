## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 is focused on delivering discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The project aims to keep V1 lightweight, with minimal local compute and heavy reliance on the phone for processing, while maintaining a clear user experience and avoiding unnecessary complexity.
