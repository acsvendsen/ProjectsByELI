# Consolidation of SmartGlasses Core Field and Recent Signals

## Core Mission
Create smart glasses that act as a discreet, context-aware personal assistant focused on live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support to help the wearer understand and respond better in real-world interactions.

### Strengthening Signals
- **Enhance Context Persistence:** Ensure context persists seamlessly across sessions by refining storage and retrieval mechanisms.
- **User-Centric Design:** Focus on low-friction human experience. Prioritize clear, concise prompts over complex outputs.
- **Privacy Measures:** Implement robust privacy protocols to ensure user data is handled securely.

### Weakening Signals
- **Avoid Gimmickry:** Stay focused on core functionality rather than adding unnecessary features that might distract from the primary mission.
- **Optimize for Real-Time Performance:** Minimize latency in responses and processing to maintain user trust.

### Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Discreet vs. Informative Design:** Balance the need for discreet design with the necessity of providing clear, useful information.
- **Cloud vs. Local Processing:** Evaluate the trade-offs between local processing for faster response times and cloud-based solutions for more advanced capabilities.

### Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Contextual Awareness Improvements:** Explore additional ways to improve context awareness (e.g., environmental sounds, motion detection) without overwhelming the user.
- **Social Acceptability Testing:** Conduct tests in real-world social scenarios to ensure the glasses are socially acceptable and do not cause discomfort or embarrassment.

### No Fluff
- **Focus on Core Features:** Prioritize real-time subtitles, name recall, and one-line prompts. Avoid unnecessary features that may complicate the design.
- **Continuous Reality-Check:** Regularly surface hardware, battery, thermal, latency, privacy, and usability constraints to ensure the project remains grounded in practical realities.
