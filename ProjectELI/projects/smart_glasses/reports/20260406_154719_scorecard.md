## Project Summary
The project is focused on creating discreet smart glasses with V1 features limited to live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The goal is to maintain a lightweight design that enhances understanding in noisy environments while respecting real-world constraints like battery life and social acceptability.
