1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving confidence display clarity helps build trust in both subtitles and memory recall, ensuring that the wearer can rely on the system's assistance without feeling uncertain or misled.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple, concise format for confidence objects that combines a numeric score with a label and short reason. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"` for subtitles, and similarly for memory recall.
