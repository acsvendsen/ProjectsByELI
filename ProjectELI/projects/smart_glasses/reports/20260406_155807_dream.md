### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules can enhance the wearer's ability to focus on conversations while minimizing distraction. By optimizing where subtitles appear, the glasses can help reduce visual clutter, ensuring that subtitles are always in a prime position for easy reading and understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence situations (e.g., well-known speakers) and another for lower confidence scenarios (e.g., new faces or uncertain voices). This simple change will allow the glasses to adapt the subtitle location based on the context, reducing distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By adapting subtitle placement based on speaker confidence, the system can maintain a more focused visual experience for the wearer. This adaptive approach can lead to fewer distractions and improved comprehension during conversations, making the subtitles more useful in various social settings.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
