## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is on track to deliver V1 with a focus on real-time subtitles, prominent face/name recall, and one-line support through frame-touch interaction. The architecture favors phone-first processing while respecting battery and thermal constraints.

## Hardware Stack

- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.

- next focus: Define fixed subtitle placement rules for V1 to ensure readability without distraction.
