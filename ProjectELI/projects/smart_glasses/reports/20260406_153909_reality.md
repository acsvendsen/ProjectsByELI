## Decision Areas and Real-world Limits

### Subtitle Placement
- **Feasible Now**: Given the constraint of V1 interaction being frame-touch only, subtitle placement should focus on non-distracting yet easily readable areas. Placing subtitles above or below the center of the frame can ensure they are visible but do not obstruct primary visual attention.

- **Feasible Now**: Use a label + short reason format to build trust. For example, displaying "Uncertain" or "Confident" along with a brief description of why the system is uncertain can provide necessary feedback without overwhelming the user.
  - *Reason*: This helps manage expectations and reduces uncertainty in real-time interactions.

### Memory/Cache Policy
- **Feasible Now**: Use best effort within `memory/cache policy` to store face/name memory on the phone. Implement a caching mechanism that balances storage needs with quick lookup times, ensuring that critical information is always accessible.
  - *Reason*: Early versions should rely heavily on phone processing and avoid heavy cloud dependency unless strictly necessary.

### Phone/Cloud Boundary
- **Feasible Now**: Rely primarily on the phone for memory lookup and orchestration. Use cloud services only where it adds clear value, such as during model training or offline support.
  - *Reason*: This reduces thermal and battery strain while ensuring that V1 remains focused on real utility.

### Visual Hierarchy
- **Feasible Now**: Subtitles should dominate the display to ensure they are the primary focus. One-line prompts can be displayed in a less prominent area, such as at the bottom of the frame or in a semi-transparent overlay.
  - *Reason*: This maintains clarity and reduces visual clutter.

### Interaction Constraints
- **Feasible Now**: Frame touches should be the only interaction method for V1. Avoid assuming voice activation or spoken commands until explicitly justified by future project inputs.
  - *Reason*: This respects the current V1 design assumptions and minimizes complexity.
