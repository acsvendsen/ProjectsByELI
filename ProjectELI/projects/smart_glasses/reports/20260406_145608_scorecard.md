## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 is focusing on discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. Key areas of focus include subtitle placement, confidence display, and memory policy. The project maintains a lightweight hardware design to ensure social acceptability and minimal thermal/thermal pressure while optimizing for real-time responsiveness and user trust.
