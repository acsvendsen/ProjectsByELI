### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By establishing fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles remain unobtrusive yet clear enough for the wearer to follow conversations effectively. This helps maintain a balance between usability and social acceptability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches (e.g., known people) placed in a fixed position on the lower part of the screen, and another for low-confidence matches (e.g., new faces or names) positioned slightly higher to alert but not obstruct the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that subtitles are always visible when needed without becoming too distracting. It helps maintain the wearer's focus on the conversation while providing necessary assistance, enhancing their understanding and participation in real-world interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By carefully defining which tasks run on the phone versus which fall back to the cloud, we can optimize real-time performance while maintaining privacy and usability. This ensures that critical live subtitle processing happens locally to keep responses fast and reliable, reducing battery drain and latency issues.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear boundary for live subtitle processing: all live subtitle data should be processed on the phone in near-real time, with only final rendered text sent to the glasses. For transcript cleanup and memory lookup, establish a fallback to the cloud as needed but prioritize local computation.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will enhance the reliability of real-time subtitles by reducing latency and ensuring that the glasses always receive up-to-date, clear text. It will also improve privacy by keeping sensitive audio data off the cloud, thus avoiding potential security risks and maintaining user trust.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.
