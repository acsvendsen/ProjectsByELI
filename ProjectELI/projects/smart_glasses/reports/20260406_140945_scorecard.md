## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering near-real-time subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support through frame-touch interactions. The hardware remains simple to avoid thermal issues and excessive battery drain, with heavy processing handled by the phone or cloud where justified. Core tensions revolve around privacy, always-on sensing vs. battery management, and ensuring a low-friction user experience.
