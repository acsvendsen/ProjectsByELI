## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on V1 implementation with a core mission of providing discreet, context-aware personal assistance. The current configuration ensures minimal hardware complexity and phone-first processing while addressing key constraints such as battery life, thermal management, and social acceptability.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The project is on track to maintain a light hardware stack, focusing on frame-touch interaction and minimizing on-glasses computation. The current focus on corner or edge subtitle placement respects battery and thermal constraints.
