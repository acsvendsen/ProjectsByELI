## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 project aims to provide discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, and one-line support. The current focus is on ensuring the system remains lightweight, socially acceptable, and avoids broad feature sprawl or unnecessary complexity.

- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
