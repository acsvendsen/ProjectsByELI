## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware smart glasses system with live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support, adhering to V1 limitations such as frame-touch interaction and minimal on-glasses compute. Current core fields are well-defined, but progress remains constrained by real-world hardware, battery, privacy, and social acceptability limits.
