## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering real-time subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support through a discreet hardware stack centered on frame-touch interactions and phone-first architecture. The key dimensions include ensuring clear subtitles, trustworthy memory support, and low-friction assistance while managing privacy concerns and balancing rich functionality with real-time performance.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and social acceptability
- progress: The project is focused on keeping the hardware stack simple with frame-touch interactions and minimizing on-glasses computation to ensure battery efficiency and social acceptability. The current approach of using a phone-first architecture aligns well with this goal.
- next focus: Optimize subtitle placement for better readability without becoming distracting, especially in noisy environments.
- confidence: 0.85
