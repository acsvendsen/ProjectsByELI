### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement improves the wearer's trust in the system, making them more likely to rely on subtitles for real-time understanding. A consistent and predictable subtitle position reduces visual distraction and enhances readability, particularly in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle position that remains centered but slightly above the bottom of the screen, with minor adjustments based on confidence levels (high confidence: no movement; low confidence: slight upward shift).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting a consistent subtitle placement rule, the system can maintain a high level of trust among wearers. Stable subtitles will be easier to follow and less likely to cause visual distractions, thereby enhancing the overall user experience.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
