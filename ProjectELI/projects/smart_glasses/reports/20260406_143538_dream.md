### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always readable and unobtrusive, reducing distractions while maintaining clarity. This will help the wearer stay focused on conversations without visual clutter.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple rule where high-confidence subtitles appear at the bottom of the screen, medium-confidence ones in a semi-transparent overlay above the speaker's face, and low-confidence ones in a smaller text box that can be dismissed easily by a frame touch.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This placement strategy will enhance user trust by ensuring that critical information is always visible while minimizing distractions from less certain data. It will also reduce battery usage since fewer subtitles are displayed at any one time, aligning with the project's V1 constraints.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
