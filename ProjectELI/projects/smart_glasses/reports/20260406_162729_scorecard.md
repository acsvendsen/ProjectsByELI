## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is making progress in V1, focusing on a touch-first interaction model with minimal on-glasses computation. The primary goals are to deliver near-real-time subtitles, maintain face/name memory trust, and ensure social acceptability. Real-world constraints such as battery and thermal pressure continue to guide the design decisions.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The project is maintaining a focus on frame-touch interaction and phone-first processing to minimize hardware demands. The chosen subtitle placement (more prominent) and confidence display format (label + reason) align with the goals of simplicity and clarity.
