### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, the system can ensure that subtitles are always visible and readable without overwhelming the wearer. This improves understanding and ensures that critical information is not missed.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a set of fixed subtitle placement rules for different confidence states: high-confidence subtitles should be placed more prominently but still maintain readability; low-confidence subtitles should be placed slightly lower to avoid distraction, but with a clear visual indicator that the information might be less certain.

3. **Likely payoff**

   Clear and consistent subtitle placement can significantly enhance the wearer's ability to follow conversations by reducing the likelihood of missing important information. This will improve overall understanding and trust in the system without adding unnecessary complexity or distractions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By clearly representing confidence levels through a concise label and short reason, the system can enhance trust by showing when information is reliable or uncertain. This helps the wearer understand the reliability of the subtitles and memory without overwhelming them with complex details.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence format that uses a numeric score (0-1), a label (High, Medium, Low), and a short reason (e.g., "Seen recently", "Weak match").

3. **Likely payoff**

   This format will provide a clear and concise way to communicate the reliability of subtitles and memory to the wearer, which can significantly enhance trust in the system’s accuracy. It will also help the wearer quickly understand when they should pay more attention or consider the context.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
