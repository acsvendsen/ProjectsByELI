# Dream cycle

### Adjacent-Value Improvements

#### 1. Enhanced Face Memory with Contextual Clues
**Why it benefits the core:**
Improving face memory by incorporating contextual clues can help reinforce recognition without overwhelming the system or wearer.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Implement a simple context-aware algorithm that recognizes patterns in how names are introduced (e.g., "This is my colleague, Jane") and associates these patterns with the name for faster recall.

**Likely payoff:**
The glasses will be more effective at recognizing new faces by understanding common ways people introduce themselves. This reduces the need for extensive memory updates while still ensuring accuracy.

**Immediate next probe:**
Test the algorithm using a diverse set of introductions to gauge its effectiveness and refine the patterns recognized.

---

#### 2. Adaptive Subtitle Font and Display
**Why it benefits the core:**
Tailoring subtitles to the environment and wearer preferences can enhance usability without making the glasses overly complex or noisy.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Develop a dynamic subtitle system that adjusts font size, color, and style based on ambient light levels and personal settings (e.g., preference for larger fonts in darker environments).

**Likely payoff:**
Improves readability and reduces eye strain, thereby increasing user satisfaction and engagement with the glasses.

**Immediate next probe:**
Conduct a small-scale usability test to gather feedback on preferred font styles and sizes under different lighting conditions.

---

#### 3. Personalized Prompt Suggestion System
**Why it benefits the core:**
Personalizing prompts can make them more relevant and useful, aligning better with the wearer's context and needs.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Integrate a machine learning model that suggests one-line prompts based on recent interactions and user feedback.

**Likely payoff:**
Enhances the relevance of prompts, making them more likely to be helpful and reducing cognitive load for the wearer. This can lead to higher daily use value.

**Immediate next probe:**
Prototype and test different types of machine learning models (e.g., decision trees, neural networks) with a small subset of user data to determine effectiveness.

---

#### 4. Improved Battery Management through Smart Sensing
**Why it benefits the core:**
Efficient battery usage can ensure the glasses remain usable throughout the day without frequent recharging.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Implement adaptive sensing where the glasses switch between high-sensitivity mode and low-sensitivity mode based on recent activity (e.g., turn off voice recording when no conversation is detected).

**Likely payoff:**
Significantly extends battery life while maintaining essential functionality, improving the overall user experience.

**Immediate next probe:**
Deploy a prototype to monitor energy consumption during various usage scenarios and adjust thresholds for switching between modes.

---

#### 5. Enhanced Privacy Through Anonymized Data Collection
**Why it benefits the core:**
Maintaining privacy is crucial but can also enhance trust in the system if handled properly.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Collect anonymized data on face recognition patterns and usage habits without storing personal identifiers, ensuring compliance with privacy regulations while improving system performance.

**Likely payoff:**
Increases user trust by demonstrating a commitment to privacy while still allowing the system to learn and improve over time. This can lead to better feature refinement and adoption rates.

**Immediate next probe:**
Develop an anonymization protocol and test its effectiveness on real-world data, ensuring no personal information is retained or compromised.

---

#### 6. Contextual Awareness of Environmental Noise
**Why it benefits the core:**
Understanding environmental noise levels can optimize subtitle delivery timing and clarity.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Add a simple noise level sensor to automatically adjust subtitle visibility based on ambient sound intensity.

**Likely payoff:**
Improves the user experience by ensuring subtitles are displayed at optimal times, reducing visual fatigue and enhancing comprehension in noisy environments.

**Immediate next probe:**
Deploy a prototype with various noise levels and gather user feedback on subtitle clarity and timing adjustments.
