## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on creating a discreet personal assistant that provides real-time subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 iteration will use frame-touch interactions exclusively and rely heavily on the phone for processing with minimal local logic to keep hardware lightweight and socially acceptable.

- limitation pressure: weight, social acceptability, battery, thermal pressure
