## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 is focused on delivering real-time subtitles, robust face/name recall, and one-line prompts through a frame-touch-first interaction model. The project prioritizes lightweight hardware, minimal on-glasses compute, and practical software stack design to maintain battery efficiency and social acceptability while ensuring trust and user understanding.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery, thermal pressure, weight, social acceptability
- progress: The project is making progress by focusing on frame touches as the primary interaction method and keeping hardware simple. However, there are still concerns about battery draw and thermal management which need attention.
- next focus: Optimizing power consumption for subtitle display and ensuring low-thermal impact of hardware components.
- confidence: 0.8
