### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By clearly representing confidence levels through a numeric score, label, and short reason, the system can build trust with the wearer by showing both certainty and the basis for that certainty. This transparency helps the wearer understand when they should pay extra attention to the content.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a confidence object format that includes a numeric score (e.g., 0-1), a label (e.g., "High", "Medium", "Low"), and a short reason (e.g., "Seen recently", "Weak match").

3. **Likely payoff**

   This clear and concise confidence display will help the wearer understand when to trust the subtitles or memory, leading to more effective use of the system. It also sets a foundation for reinforcing correct information over time.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
