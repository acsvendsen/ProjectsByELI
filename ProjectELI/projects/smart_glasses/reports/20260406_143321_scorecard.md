## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The core constraints are maintaining a lightweight design, minimizing battery drain, ensuring social acceptability, and keeping interactions simple via frame-touch input only.
