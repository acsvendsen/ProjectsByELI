## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on creating a discreet, context-aware personal assistant that assists with live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The current V1 constraints prioritize frame-touch interaction, phone-first architecture, minimal on-glasses processing, and real-time responsiveness to maintain trust and battery efficiency.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery
- progress: The project is staying within the constraints of minimizing hardware complexity to ensure social acceptability and battery efficiency. The glasses will focus on simple display functionality with processing handled by the phone and cloud as needed.
- next focus: Optimize subtitle rendering algorithms to minimize latency while ensuring minimal battery draw.
- confidence: 0.9
