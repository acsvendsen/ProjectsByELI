1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining fixed subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we ensure that subtitles are always legible and unobtrusive, enhancing the wearer's understanding without causing distraction.

   Define three fixed subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches (e.g., names heard recently), one for medium-confidence matches (e.g., new faces or names encountered once in an introduction), and one for low-confidence matches (e.g., faint voice or distant speaker).

   These rules will make the subtitles more predictable, reducing cognitive load on the wearer. They will also help maintain a consistent visual hierarchy that prioritizes important information without overwhelming the user.

   Define a subtitle placement rule where subtitles are always displayed at the bottom of the screen, while one-line prompts are shown above or next to the subtitle text but with reduced prominence. The wearer will have a clearer understanding of what is essential and what provides supplementary information.
