## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 version will prioritize frame-touch interactions, minimal hardware complexity, and phone-first processing to ensure lightweight design, low battery drain, and social acceptability. Key decisions are being made based on real-world constraints and core mission goals.

## Hardware Stack

- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The project is aligned with the goal of keeping hardware simple to ensure social acceptability and low battery usage. The focus remains on text display and minimal sensors.
- next focus: Finalize subtitle placement design to ensure it is both prominent and non-distracting.
