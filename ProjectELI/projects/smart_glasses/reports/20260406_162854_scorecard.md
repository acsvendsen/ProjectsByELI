## Project Summary
The project is focused on creating discreet, context-aware smart glasses that provide live subtitles, face/name recall, and one-line support. The V1 will use frame touches for interaction, rely primarily on the phone for processing, and keep the hardware stack lightweight to ensure social acceptability and minimal battery drain.

- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The project is on track to keep the hardware stack simple with a focus on frame touches for interaction and light processing on the glasses. The design prioritizes social acceptability and minimal battery drain, aligning with the stated goal.
