## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant that provides live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The project adheres to strict hardware and interaction constraints, prioritizing battery efficiency, social acceptability, and clear confidence signals. The current configuration keeps the glasses lightweight, with minimal local computation, and relies on phone-first processing for heavy tasks.
