## Core Field Consolidation for SmartGlasses V1

### Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Quality**: Subtitle placement should be more prominent to ensure quick readability without becoming distracting. This will enhance the wearer's understanding of their environment, especially in noisy settings.

- **Memory Trust and Reinforcement**: Face/name memory must be reinforced over time through confidence objects and explicit remember-touch behavior. This ensures that new faces are remembered with a high degree of accuracy and trust.

### Weakening Signals
- **Interaction Complexity**: Interaction should remain simple, using frame touches only. Avoiding the introduction of voice commands or spoken interactions in V1 will maintain simplicity and focus on core functionality.

### Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Subtitle Placement vs. Visual Clarity**: The system must balance subtitle placement to be visible but not overly distracting, ensuring that subtitles enhance rather than detract from the wearer's experience.

### Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Memory Policy and Fast Lookup**: While the current memory policy focuses on strong assistance, there is a need to explore how fast lookup can be integrated without compromising privacy or battery life. This could involve caching more relevant data locally on the phone for faster access during high-cognitive load situations.

### Concrete Tensions Around Core Domains
1. **Subtitle Quality vs. Battery and Thermals**:
   - Ensuring subtitles are always near-real-time is crucial but must be balanced with battery constraints.
   
2. **Always-on Sensing vs. Privacy Perception**:
   - The system should continuously monitor environmental noise levels to trigger subtitles only when necessary, minimizing privacy concerns.

3. **Confidence Display vs. Social Acceptability**:
   - Confidence objects (labels and reasons) should provide clear but not overly intrusive feedback about the accuracy of face/name recognition. This ensures trust without overwhelming the wearer visually or socially.

4. **Local Processing vs. Cloud Intelligence**:
   - The balance between local processing for fast responses and cloud intelligence for more complex tasks must be carefully managed to optimize performance while respecting battery limits.

5. **Rich Memory vs. Fast Lookup**:
   - Implementing a hybrid approach where critical information is cached locally but less critical data is stored and retrieved from the phone/cloud can enhance both memory and lookup speed without overburdening resources.

### Open Questions
- **Subtitle Placement**: What subtitle placement maximizes readability while minimizing distraction?
- **Confidence Display**: How should confidence be represented to build trust effectively, using labels, short reasons, or scores?
- **Memory Policy and Fast Lookup**: How can the system cache relevant data for fast lookup without compromising privacy or battery life?

### Reality Rules
- **Subtitle Placement**: Subtitle placement should be more prominent but carefully balanced.
- **Confidence Display**: Use a label + short reason format to provide clear but not overwhelming feedback.
- **Memory Policy and Fast Lookup**: Explore caching strategies that enhance memory while optimizing local processing.

### Core Field Summary

#### Current Core Mission
Create smart glasses that act as a discreet, context-aware personal assistant focused on live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support to help the wearer understand and respond better in real-world interactions.

#### Key Tensions
- **Subtitle Quality vs. Battery and Thermals**: Ensure near-real-time subtitles without excessive battery drain.
- **Always-on Sensing vs. Privacy Perception**: Balance continuous monitoring with minimal privacy concerns.
- **Confidence Display vs. Social Acceptability**: Provide clear feedback but avoid social distractions.
- **Local Processing vs. Cloud Intelligence**: Optimize performance by balancing local and cloud resources.

#### Core Attractors
- Real-time subtitle assistance in noisy environments.
- Personal memory support for names and prior conversations.
- One-line contextual prompts rather than overwhelming paragraphs.
- Low-friction human experience; assistive, not deceptive.

#### Evolution Rule
Expand the system only if it strengthens the core mission, improves real-world usefulness, or increases trust and clarity.
