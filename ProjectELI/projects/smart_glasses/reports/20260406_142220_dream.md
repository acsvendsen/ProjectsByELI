### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always legible and unobtrusive, enhancing the wearer's understanding without becoming a distraction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement modes: static top-center, dynamic edge alignment (based on gaze), and reactive bottom corner for low-confidence matches.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will improve the readability of subtitles while maintaining a discreet look, reducing cognitive load and enhancing the wearer's experience in noisy environments.

4. **Immediate next probe**
