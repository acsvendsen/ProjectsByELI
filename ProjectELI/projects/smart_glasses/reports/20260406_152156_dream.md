### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always visible where they are most needed, reducing visual clutter while maintaining high utility.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define and implement two subtitle placement modes: a default fixed position at the bottom of the screen, and an adaptive mode that moves subtitles to a less distracting area when confidence is low.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will improve the visual clarity for the wearer by ensuring that important information is not obscured by subtitles in areas where they are less likely to be read or understood. It also allows for more natural conversation flow without constant interruption from the glasses.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define the fixed position rule for subtitles to be placed at the bottom of the screen.
