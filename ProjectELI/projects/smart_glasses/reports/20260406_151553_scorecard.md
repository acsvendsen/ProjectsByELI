## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant that enhances understanding through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support in V1. The current version relies heavily on phone processing for heavy tasks and minimizes hardware complexity to ensure social acceptability and minimal battery drain.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The glasses remain simple with text display and basic sensors. Frame-touch interactions ensure minimal hardware complexity while maintaining social acceptability.
- next focus: Optimize sensor usage to minimize thermal and battery impact without compromising core functionality.
- confidence: 0.8
