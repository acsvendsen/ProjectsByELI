### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving subtitle placement rules helps ensure that subtitles are always legible, unobtrusive, and provide clear support without becoming distracting or breaking the wearer's flow during conversation.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement modes: a default mode for general situations where subtitles should be prominent but not too visible, and a fallback mode for high-confidence scenarios where more detailed information is available.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing clear guidance on subtitle visibility based on confidence levels, the system can maintain a balance between aiding the wearer and avoiding distraction, enhancing both trust and usability in real-world interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
