## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 is focused on creating discreet, context-aware personal assistants through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The current approach prioritizes minimal hardware complexity, frame-touch interactions, phone-first architecture, and low-friction user experience to maintain social acceptability and usability.
