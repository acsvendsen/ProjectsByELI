   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles remain unobtrusive while still providing clear and timely assistance. This helps maintain social acceptability and reduces cognitive load for the wearer.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This rule will help balance the need for clear visibility with social acceptability by reducing distraction during high-confidence interactions and minimizing intrusion when the system is less certain. It will also allow the wearer to focus more on the conversation while still receiving necessary assistance.
