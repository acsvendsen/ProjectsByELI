## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering real-time subtitle assistance, face/name recall, and one-line support through frame-touch interaction. The goal is to keep V1 lightweight, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute while ensuring battery efficiency and trust-building features like confidence displays and clear memory policies.
