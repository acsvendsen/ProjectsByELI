# Consolidation of SmartGlasses Project Signals

## Strengthening Signals

### Core Mission Clarity
- Ensure that all development efforts align with the mission of creating smart glasses as a discreet, context-aware personal assistant for real-world interactions.

### Non-negotiables Reinforcement
- **Help the wearer:** Continue to emphasize that the system should never pretend knowledge the wearer does not have.
- **Simplicity in hardware:** Focus on simple display and recording functionalities while leveraging cloud processing for complex tasks.
- **Context persistence:** Ensure context information persists across sessions without compromising privacy.

### Core Attractors Enhancement
- **Real-time subtitles:** Prioritize real-time subtitle assistance, especially in noisy environments.
- **Memory support:** Develop robust memory support for names and prior conversations.
- **Low-friction UX:** Maintain a user-friendly experience with minimal disruption to the wearer's natural interactions.

## Weakening Signals

### Feature Sprawl
- Avoid adding features that do not significantly enhance the core subtitle/memory loop functionality. Focus on high-value, low-complexity additions.

### Gimmicky Design
- Ensure the system remains practical and useful rather than becoming a mere novelty item.

## Contradictions to Stay Alive

### Privacy vs Usefulness
- Strive for a balance where the system provides valuable assistance without compromising user privacy.
- Early surfacing of real-world limitations will guide design decisions that respect user privacy while delivering utility.

### Always-on Sensing vs Battery Constraints
- Prioritize low-power modes and efficient hardware to manage battery life, balancing continuous sensing with energy efficiency.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating

### Confidence Representation
- Explore innovative ways to represent confidence scores and reasons for recommendations in a concise manner.
- Consider user feedback mechanisms to refine and validate the effectiveness of different confidence indicators.

### Subtitle and Prompt Coexistence
- Develop strategies to integrate subtitles and one-line prompts effectively, ensuring neither overwhelms the wearer.

## No Fluff

### Reality-check Rule Implementation
- Continuously surface real-world limitations in planning sessions and prototype development.
- Surface hardware, battery, thermal, latency, privacy, and usability constraints early to avoid optimizing unfeasible directions.

### Open Questions Guidance
- Address core questions methodically: 
  - **V1 split:** Determine the optimal distribution between glasses, phone, and cloud functionalities.
  - **Memory storage:** Develop a strategy for efficient face/name memory storage and reinforcement.
  - **Confidence representation:** Experiment with different confidence indicators (numeric scores, labels, short reasons).
  - **Subtitle/prompt balance:** Design systems that coexist without overloading the wearer.

By focusing on these areas, the project can maintain its core mission while continuously adapting to real-world constraints and user needs.
