## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering a V1 of smart glasses that provide live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support through frame touches only, with an emphasis on lightweight hardware and practical software to maintain real-time performance and user trust.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure, social acceptability
- progress: The project remains focused on a simple hardware stack with frame-touch interactions only, aligning well with the goals of minimizing battery drain and maintaining social acceptability. The glasses do not include additional sensors or complex interaction methods that could increase thermal or battery demands.
- next focus: Continuously evaluate subtitle placement to balance readability and distraction while respecting thermal and battery constraints.
- confidence: 0.8

## Software Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep the cognition stack practical, maintainable, and centered on subtitle quality, memory trust, and low-friction assistive behavior.
- limitation pressure: complexity drift, latency, trust
- progress: The software remains focused on core functionalities like subtitles and memory recall with minimal complexity. The team has chosen concrete design decisions such as frame-touch input and label + reason confidence display to maintain simplicity and reliability.
- next focus: Implement the recency-first caching policy for face/name memory to ensure relevant information is quickly accessible without overwhelming local storage.
- confidence: 0.75
