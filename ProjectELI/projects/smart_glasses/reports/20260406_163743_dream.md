### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   
   Stabilizing subtitle placement rules helps ensure that subtitles are always easy to read without becoming distracting, which is crucial for maintaining the wearer's focus and understanding during conversations.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define 3 fixed subtitle placement modes: centered, top-aligned, and bottom-aligned, each with specific confidence thresholds (e.g., High Confidence -> Centered; Medium Confidence -> Top-Aligned; Low Confidence -> Bottom-Aligned).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By setting clear, consistent placement rules based on confidence, the system can minimize distraction while ensuring that critical information is always visible and easy to read. This improves user experience and trust in the assistant's reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
