### Improvement 1: Optimize Subtitle Placement Rules

**Why it benefits the core:** 
Proper subtitle placement ensures that subtitles are easily readable without distracting from the primary visual content, thus maintaining the wearer's focus and understanding.

**What small change unlocks it:**
Define 2 to 3 subtitle placement rules for different confidence levels. For instance:
- **High Confidence (90%+):** Place subtitles directly below the speaker.
- **Medium Confidence (60%-89%):** Position subtitles slightly off-center, possibly with a subtle fade-in effect.
- **Low Confidence (<60%):** Display subtitles above or near the face area but reduce opacity.

**Likely payoff:**
Enhanced readability and reduced distraction, leading to better user experience and trust in the system's accuracy.
