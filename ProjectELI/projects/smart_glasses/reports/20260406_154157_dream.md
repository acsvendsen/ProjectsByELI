### Idea 2: Confidence Display

   Providing a clear and concise confidence format helps build trust by making the wearer aware of the system's certainty level. This ensures that the wearer can better judge when to rely on the subtitles or prompts, enhancing overall user experience.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple confidence format that combines a numeric score with a label and a short reason. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"` for well-rehearsed names or `score 0.42 + "Low" + "Weak match"` for less certain identifications.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing a clear and transparent confidence format, the system can avoid overconfidence in uncertain situations while also reinforcing trust when the system is highly confident. This will help users understand the reliability of the information provided, leading to better engagement and usage.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
