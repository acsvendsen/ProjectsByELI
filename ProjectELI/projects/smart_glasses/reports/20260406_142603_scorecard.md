## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 version adheres to specific constraints such as frame-touch interaction for the glasses, heavy reliance on the phone for processing, and minimal use of cloud resources. The current state aligns closely with the defined goals but faces real-world limitations like battery and thermal pressure, social acceptability, and privacy concerns.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery, thermal pressure, weight, social acceptability
- progress: The glasses remain simple with a focus on text display and minimal local logic. Frame-touch interaction is the primary user input method, aligning with the hardware stack goals.
- next focus: Optimize subtitle placement to balance readability and non-distractiveness while ensuring low battery usage.
