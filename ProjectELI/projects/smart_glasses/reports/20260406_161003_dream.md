### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always visible when needed but not distracting when less certain. This helps maintain the wearer's focus on the conversation while providing critical information during key moments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple three-tier subtitle placement rule: 
   - Always place subtitles at the bottom center for high-confidence matches.
   - Place subtitles slightly above the gaze point for medium-confidence matches.
   - Do not display subtitles or use an ambient indicator (like a blinking dot) for low-confidence matches.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that the wearer is always informed about key speakers without being overwhelmed by unnecessary text, thus balancing clarity and distraction. It also helps maintain trust in the system’s reliability since critical information is consistently presented when most certain.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
