# Core Field Consolidation for SmartGlasses V1

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Quality**: The system should prioritize real-time, clear, and contextually relevant subtitles. Any delays or lag will significantly impact trust.
- **Memory Trust**: Face/name recall must be accurate and reliable, with confidence objects clearly displayed to the user.

## Weakening Signals
- **Feature Sprawl**: Avoid unnecessary features until core functionalities (subtitles, face/name recall) are well established and trusted.
- **Interaction Complexity**: Stick to frame-touch interactions in V1; avoid adding voice commands or other complex controls.

## Contradictions that Must Stay Alive
- **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Ensure the system is as useful as possible without compromising privacy. Users should feel their data is handled securely.
- **Battery and Thermals**: Always-on features must be carefully balanced to ensure battery life does not become a critical issue.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **One-Line Support**: Explore how one-line prompts can complement subtitles without overwhelming the wearer, focusing on high-value interactions.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Areas

### Subtitle Quality
- **Real-time vs. Delayed Processing**: Strive for real-time subtitle delivery but be mindful of battery constraints and latency.
- **Placement and Visual Hierarchy**: Optimize placement to ensure subtitles are visible without becoming distracting. Prioritize a clear visual hierarchy.

### Confidence Trust
- **Display Format**: Evaluate the best way to display confidence levels (label + reason, numeric score) to build user trust without overwhelming them.
- **Memory Reinforcement**: Implement mechanisms for reinforcing memory recall over time, ensuring new faces and names are remembered with high confidence.

### Automatic Memory Behavior vs. Explicit Remember-Touch Behavior
- **Automatic Memory**: Ensure automatic memory of new people is reliable but not intrusive.
- **Explicit Remember-Touch**: Provide a clear way to confirm or add to the memory database through user interaction.

### Fast Lookup Requirements
- **Local vs. Cloud Processing**: Determine where and when fast lookup should be handled on-device versus in the cloud, balancing speed and privacy concerns.

### Privacy, Latency, Battery, Visual UX
- **Privacy**: Ensure data handling respects user privacy while still providing useful information.
- **Latency**: Strive for low-latency responses to maintain trust but avoid overburdening the system.
- **Battery**: Balance always-on features with battery constraints to ensure the glasses remain usable throughout the day.
- **Visual UX**: Design subtitles and prompts in a way that minimizes distraction while maximizing utility.

## Open Questions
- **Subtitle Placement**: What is the best placement for subtitles to maximize readability without becoming distracting?
- **Confidence Display**: How should confidence be represented to users (label, score, reason) to build trust without overwhelming them?
- **Memory/Cache Policy**: How much face/name memory should be cached locally on the phone versus in the cloud?

## Reality Rules
- **Feasible Now vs. Feasible Later**: Prioritize features that are feasible with current technology and infrastructure.
- **User Acceptance**: Focus on designs that respect social norms and user acceptance, avoiding over-engineering for early-stage systems.

## Concrete Guidance
- **Subtitle First Mode**: Continue to use subtitle_first mode in operator guidance.
- **Frame Touch Interaction**: Ensure all interactions remain frame touch-based until V1 is complete.
