### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining more prominent subtitle placement rules for high-confidence states, we ensure that critical information is easily readable while minimizing distraction during less urgent interactions. This improves overall usability and trust by making important subtitles stand out without overwhelming the user.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a new subtitle placement rule: If the confidence score is above 0.95, place the subtitle at the top center of the screen for maximum visibility. For lower confidence states, use the default bottom-right corner placement to avoid distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This change will make high-confidence subtitles more noticeable without compromising on the subtlety of less certain information. Users can quickly identify key names and phrases, reducing cognitive load in critical moments. This enhances both the assistive value and user trust by making important information stand out clearly.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

   By clearly defining which tasks run on the phone versus which fall back to the cloud, we can ensure that V1 stays focused on real utility while managing hardware constraints effectively. 2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define specific rules for when live subtitle processing should use local phone resources versus falling back to cloud-based services. For example, if processing audio is too resource-intensive on the glasses, offloading this task could reduce battery drain and improve real-time performance.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By optimizing the subtitle processing boundary, we can enhance the reliability of subtitles in noisy environments, ensuring that the wearer receives timely and accurate information without unnecessary battery drain or thermal stress on the glasses. This will increase trust and satisfaction with the product.

4. **Immediate next probe**
