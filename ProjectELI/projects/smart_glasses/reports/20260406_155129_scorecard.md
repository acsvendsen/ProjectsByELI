## Project Summary
SmartGlasses V1 is focused on providing discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The core mission is to assist wearers without overwhelming them or breaking trust. Key constraints include lightweight design, battery management, social acceptability, and minimal on-glasses processing.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The glasses remain focused on display tasks while processing is offloaded to the phone and cloud. Frame-touch interactions are prioritized over more complex inputs, reducing hardware demands.
- next focus: Ensure that subtitle placement does not significantly impact battery or thermal constraints.
- confidence: 0.9
