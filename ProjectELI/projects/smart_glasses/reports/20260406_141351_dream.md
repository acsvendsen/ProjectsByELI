### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement rules ensures that subtitles remain clear and unobtrusive while still providing essential information. This helps maintain the wearer's focus on their surroundings, reducing cognitive load and enhancing real-time understanding.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches (e.g., known speakers or frequently encountered faces) that are placed slightly above center, and another for lower-confidence matches (e.g., new or less frequent encounters) that are placed closer to the edge of the field of view.

3. **Likely payoff**
   This change will provide a more consistent and unobtrusive user experience by balancing clarity with minimally disruptive placement. It reduces the chance of subtitles becoming distracting, thereby enhancing real-time understanding in noisy environments without overwhelming the wearer.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
