### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels, we can ensure that subtitles are always visible when needed and do not distract from the conversation. This improves the wearer's ability to understand and participate effectively in real-world interactions without feeling overwhelmed by text.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define three subtitle placement rules:
   - High confidence: Always show subtitles.
   - Medium confidence: Show subtitles but allow manual adjustment if needed.
   - Low confidence: Only show subtitles upon frame touch or explicit user request.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that the wearer receives necessary assistance when it matters most, while minimizing distractions during more straightforward conversations. It balances the need for real-time subtitle support with the desire to maintain a natural and unobtrusive interaction experience.
