1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining a consistent subtitle placement rule that places subtitles in a fixed position within the wearer's field of view, we ensure that subtitles remain easily readable without becoming distracting. This helps maintain focus on the conversation while providing essential information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a fixed subtitle placement zone at the bottom center of the wearer’s field of view for all confidence states, with minor adjustments only if necessary to avoid occlusion by objects like glasses frames or other items worn on the head.

3. **Likely payoff**

   A consistent and non-distracting subtitle placement improves user trust in the system, as they can always rely on subtitles appearing predictably. This reduces cognitive load and enhances the overall assistive value of the glasses, making them more useful and reliable during conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
