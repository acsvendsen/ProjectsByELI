### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence states, the system can ensure that subtitles are always readable and unobtrusive while maintaining a high level of assistance. This helps the wearer understand conversations better without causing distractions.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence matches (e.g., names or familiar faces) placed more prominently, and another for lower-confidence matches (e.g., new people or uncertain speaker identifications) placed less obtrusively but still visible.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach ensures that critical information is always clear to the wearer while minimizing distractions from less certain or less important details. It balances the need for high-level assistance with the requirement of a low-friction user experience, thus enhancing overall utility and trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   A well-defined confidence format helps build trust by clearly communicating the reliability of the system's responses. This ensures that the wearer understands when information is highly certain versus uncertain, which is crucial for maintaining the balance between assistance and not overstepping.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a simple but informative confidence label and short reason format. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"; score 0.42 + "Low" + "Weak match"; score 0.63 + "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction"`.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing a clear and concise way to display confidence, the system can reduce misunderstandings and build trust with the wearer. This clarity will help the user understand when they should rely more on their own judgment versus trusting the system's assistance, thereby enhancing overall user satisfaction and reliability.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
