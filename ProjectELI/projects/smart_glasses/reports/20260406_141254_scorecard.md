## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses V1 project is focused on creating discreet assistive glasses with real-time subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The current direction emphasizes simplicity, minimal hardware dependencies, and reliable user interactions through frame touches only. The key challenges include ensuring subtitle quality, memory trust, and low thermal and battery impact while maintaining social acceptability.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The project is adhering to the hardware stack goals by focusing on minimal local logic and using phone/cloud for heavy processing. The glasses remain simple with no unjustified hardware assumptions.
- next focus: Refine subtitle placement to ensure clear, unobtrusive visibility without overwhelming the user.
