# Dream cycle

### Idea 1: Enhanced Contextual Memory System

#### Why it benefits the core
Improving the contextual memory system will ensure that the glasses can better remember faces, names, and past conversations. This improvement is crucial for maintaining a reliable and useful assistant.

#### What small change unlocks it
Implement a more sophisticated machine learning model for face recognition and name recall. Use incremental training to continuously improve accuracy over time based on user interactions.

#### Likely payoff
The system will be better at recognizing and recalling people the wearer meets frequently, reducing the need for re-introductions and enhancing the overall conversational flow.

#### Immediate next probe
Conduct a small-scale pilot test with a diverse group of users in various social settings to gather qualitative feedback on name recall accuracy and user satisfaction.

### Idea 2: Adaptive Real-Time Subtitles

#### Why it benefits the core
Adaptive subtitles can improve the glasses' effectiveness by making them more responsive to different environments. This feature is vital for ensuring the wearer always has the information they need.

#### What small change unlocks it
Introduce adaptive subtitle settings that adjust based on ambient noise levels and speech clarity, dynamically switching between text and spoken prompts as needed.

#### Likely payoff
Users will have a better understanding of their surroundings in noisy or challenging environments, enhancing safety and social interactions.

#### Immediate next probe
Develop prototype adaptivity algorithms using real-world sound environment data to test different subtitle formats (e.g., large font for loud noises, small font for quiet settings) and gather user feedback.

### Idea 3: Contextual Prompt Personalization

#### Why it benefits the core
Personalizing one-line prompts based on context can make interactions feel more natural and less intrusive. This personalization is key to maintaining a low-friction user experience.

#### What small change unlocks it
Implement a content filtering system that generates personalized, contextually relevant prompts based on historical data and current context.

#### Likely payoff
Users will receive more relevant and timely prompts, leading to higher engagement with the glasses without overwhelming them with information.

#### Immediate next probe
Create a prototype with predefined contexts (e.g., meeting introductions, unfamiliar locations) and gather user feedback on the relevance and helpfulness of generated prompts.

### Idea 4: Improved Privacy Controls

#### Why it benefits the core
Enhancing privacy controls will address concerns about data use and build trust among users. This is essential for maintaining the glasses' utility without compromising personal boundaries.

#### What small change unlocks it
Implement a granular privacy setting that allows users to control which data (e.g., face recognition, voice recordings) are shared with the cloud and how long such data is retained.

#### Likely payoff
Users will feel more secure using the glasses, potentially increasing adoption rates in social or professional settings where privacy concerns may be higher.

#### Immediate next probe
Survey a sample group of users to understand their privacy preferences and design a user-friendly interface for setting these controls.

### Idea 5: Integration with Wearable Health Sensors

#### Why it benefits the core
Integrating health sensors can provide additional contextual information that could be beneficial for the wearer. This integration enhances the glasses' utility beyond just subtitles and memory support.

#### What small change unlocks it
Add compatibility with popular health wearables (e.g., heart rate, step count) to gather environmental data that can inform subtitle and prompt delivery.

#### Likely payoff
The glasses can provide more holistic assistance by integrating real-time health information into the decision-making process for prompts and subtitles.

#### Immediate next probe
Collaborate with a partner health wearable company to test data integration in various scenarios (e.g., outdoor activities, physical events) and gather feedback on usefulness and user experience.
