## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering discreet, context-aware personal assistance through real-time subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 will prioritize frame touches as the only interaction method and rely heavily on the phone for processing to maintain simplicity and low complexity.
