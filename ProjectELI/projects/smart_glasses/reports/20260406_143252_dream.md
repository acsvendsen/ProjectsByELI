1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules for different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are displayed in a way that enhances understanding without becoming distracting. ### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that confidence objects are clearly labeled and accompanied by a short reason helps build trust over time. Users can understand when they should pay more attention, making the system more reliable.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a confidence format that uses a numeric score, label, and a short reason. For example: `score 0.84 + "High" + "Seen recently"` for well-rehearsed names or familiar faces; `score 0.63 + "Medium" + "Name heard once in introduction"` for new but contextually relevant names.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By providing a clear and concise confidence display, the system can reinforce its reliability over time. This will help users trust the system more quickly, leading to better engagement and usage rates.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.
