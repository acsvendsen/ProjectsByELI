## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focusing on delivering discreet, context-aware personal assistance through live subtitles, face/name recall, and one-line support. The V1 design is frame-touch driven with minimal hardware complexity and heavy reliance on the phone for processing, ensuring real-time performance and low power consumption.
