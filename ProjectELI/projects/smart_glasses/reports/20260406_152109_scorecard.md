## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant with real-time subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 architecture remains phone-first with frame-touch interactions, avoiding voice commands and complex hardware assumptions. Current efforts prioritize subtitle quality, memory trust, and low-friction visual UX while adhering to battery and thermal constraints.
