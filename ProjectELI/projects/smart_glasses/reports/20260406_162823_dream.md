### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   - Consistent subtitle placement rules help ensure that subtitles are always easy to read without becoming distracting. This improves the wearer's understanding in real-time interactions, aligning with the core mission of helping them stay informed and present.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   - Define a set of fixed subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: high-confidence subtitles placed prominently but not overlapping critical visual elements; low-confidence subtitles slightly reduced in size and position to minimize distraction.

3. **Likely payoff**
   - By defining clear, non-reactive placement rules, the system can ensure that subtitles remain readable yet unobtrusive, enhancing user trust and overall experience without increasing complexity or battery drain.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.
