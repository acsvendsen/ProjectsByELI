### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring consistent subtitle placement rules can improve the wearer's understanding without overwhelming them. This helps maintain a balance between providing necessary assistance and not distracting from the conversation, thus deepening the core mission of helping the wearer stay informed.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence content (e.g., names and repeated phrases) and another for lower-confidence content (e.g., background sounds or secondary speakers).

3. **Likely payoff**

   By defining clear rules, the system can provide more targeted and less intrusive subtitles, reducing cognitive load and improving overall user experience. This clarity in placement will likely lead to higher satisfaction and more frequent use.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitle processing remains phone-local while only critical face/name recall requests fall back to the cloud can maintain real-time responsiveness, reduce battery drain, and respect user privacy.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a clear boundary where subtitles are processed locally on the phone, and only requests for specific face or name recall that cannot be handled by local cache fall back to the cloud.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This approach will ensure real-time subtitle delivery without battery strain, maintain user privacy through limited cloud interactions, and provide fast and accurate face/name recall when needed.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one phone-local vs cloud fallback boundary for exactly live subtitle processing.
