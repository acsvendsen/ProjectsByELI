## Project Summary
The project is focused on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 will use frame-touch interaction only, rely heavily on the phone for processing, and aim to minimize hardware complexity and battery drain.

## Hardware Stack

- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The project is adhering to the goal of keeping V1 lightweight and socially acceptable by focusing on frame-touch interaction and minimal local processing. The glasses remain simple with a text display and basic sensors, while heavy processing is offloaded to the phone.
