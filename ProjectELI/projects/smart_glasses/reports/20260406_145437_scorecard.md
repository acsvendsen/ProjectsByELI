## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is making good progress, focusing on delivering a lightweight, discreet assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. Key tensions include subtitle placement for readability, confidence display for trust, and balancing privacy with utility in V1. The system aims to remain simple, robust, and aligned with user needs while respecting hardware and thermal constraints.
