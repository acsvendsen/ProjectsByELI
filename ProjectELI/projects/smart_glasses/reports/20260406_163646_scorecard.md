## Project Summary
The project is focused on creating discreet, context-aware smart glasses with an initial frame-touch interaction model, emphasizing live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The current V1 direction prioritizes simplicity and minimal local processing to ensure lightweight, socially acceptable glasses that do not drain battery or cause overheating.

- limitation pressure: battery and thermal pressure
- progress: The glasses are designed to remain lightweight with minimal local processing, aligning well with the goal of keeping them socially acceptable without draining battery or causing overheating. The selected subtitle placement (more prominent) and confidence display format (label + reason) support this focus.
