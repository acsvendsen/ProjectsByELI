# Consolidated Insights for SmartGlasses V1

## Strengthening Signals
- **Subtitle Quality**: Subtitles must be clear, near-real-time, and minimally distracting. The system should prioritize accuracy over quantity of text displayed.

- **Memory Reinforcement**: New faces and names should be remembered with confidence objects and reinforced over time through explicit remember-touch behavior (e.g., touching a face in the frame to mark it as known).

- **Confidence Display**: A label plus short reason format for confidence is the most trusted approach, providing clear feedback on the reliability of memory and recognition.

## Weakening Signals
- **Complex AR Visuals**: Avoid introducing heavy or complex augmented reality visuals that could distract from the core functionality.
- **Heavy Autonomous Behavior**: Reduce reliance on autonomous behavior without strong confidence signals. Focus on guided interactions through frame touches for now.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Always-on Sensing vs Battery Concerns**: The system must balance always-on sensing with battery and thermal constraints, ensuring that energy-intensive features like always-on subtitle generation are carefully managed.
  
- **Privacy vs Utility**: Ensure that the system provides useful assistance while respecting privacy concerns. Avoid storing low-value or sensitive information.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Subtitle Placement**: Revisit the decision on subtitle placement to find a balance between visibility and distraction, ensuring subtitles do not block important visual cues.
  
- **One-Line Support vs Subtitles Coexistence**: Explore how one-line prompts can coexist with subtitled text without overwhelming the wearer. Ensure that these interactions are contextually relevant and non-intrusive.

## Concrete Tensions
### Subtitle Quality, Confidence Trust, Automatic Memory, Fast Lookup, Privacy, Battery, Visual UX
- **Subtitle Quality vs Latency**: The system must provide subtitles in real-time to maintain trust but also manage battery usage effectively.
  
- **Automatic New-Person Memory vs Explicit Remember-Touch Behavior**: Balance the automatic recognition of new faces with explicit user touch actions for reinforcement.

- **Memory Reinforcement vs Privacy Perception**: Ensure that memory caching and reinforcement do not compromise privacy, especially when dealing with sensitive names or contexts.

- **Battery Management**: Optimize subtitle generation to minimize battery drain while maintaining real-time quality. Consider adaptive power management strategies based on activity levels.
  
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Design a clear visual hierarchy where subtitles are prominent but not overly distracting, and one-line prompts provide additional contextual support without overwhelming the wearer.
