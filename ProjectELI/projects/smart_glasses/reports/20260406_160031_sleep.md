- **Memory Trust**: Enhance face/name recall with clear confidence indicators, such as labels or short reasons.
- **Low Friction UX**: Optimize subtitle placement to be more prominent while maintaining low visual distraction.

## Weakening Signals
- **Complex AR Features**: Avoid adding complex augmented reality (AR) features that could introduce unnecessary complexity and reduce usability.
- **Voice Activation**: Do not assume voice activation as a primary interaction method in V1; stick to frame-touch interactions only.

## Contradictions That Must Stay Alive
- **Phone/Cloud Balance**: Favor phone-first architecture but allow cloud processing where latency is acceptable, such as heavy model training or background tasks.
- **Battery Concerns**: Always consider battery and thermal constraints, ensuring that always-on features like subtitles do not excessively drain the battery.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
- **Subtitle Placement Exploration**: Consider different subtitle placements to find a balance between readability and visual intrusion. For example, testing top-center placement versus sidebars.
- **Memory Caching Strategy**: Explore caching memory locally on the phone for faster recall of names and faces without relying heavily on cloud processing.
