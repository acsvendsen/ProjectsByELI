## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project aims to create discreet assistive glasses that provide live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The current V1 focuses on frame-touch interaction and minimal local processing with heavy reliance on the phone for heavy lifting.

## Hardware Stack
- status: on_track
- goal: Keep V1 lightweight, discreet, socially acceptable, and dependent on minimal on-glasses compute.
- limitation pressure: battery
- progress: The project is maintaining a simple hardware stack that prioritizes battery efficiency by offloading processing to the phone. The glasses are kept as light and unobtrusive as possible.
- next focus: Ensure continued optimization of thermal management, even though current constraints allow minimal local processing.
- confidence: 0.9
