2. **Memory Support**: Improve the reliability of name and face memory recall to provide accurate and timely support without creating false certainty.
3. **Low-Friction Assistance**: Ensure one-line prompts are useful yet low-impact, avoiding unnecessary command overhead.

### Weakening Signals

2. **Latency Tradeoffs**: Prioritize timely responses over overly rich context to maintain real-time utility.
3. **Discreet UX**: Strive for a socially acceptable display without overwhelming the wearer with too much information.
