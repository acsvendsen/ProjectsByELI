### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**
   Improving subtitle placement ensures that subtitles are always readable and unobtrusive, enhancing the wearer's ability to follow conversations without distraction.

2. **What small change unlocks it**
   Define specific subtitle placement rules based on confidence levels: high-confidence text at the bottom of the screen, medium-confidence text slightly above center, low-confidence text with a subtle underline or color change.

It will also ensure that critical information is highlighted with high confidence, while less certain data is presented with appropriate caution.

4. **Immediate next probe**
