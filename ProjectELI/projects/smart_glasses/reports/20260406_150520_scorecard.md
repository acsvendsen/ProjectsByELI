## Project Summary
The project is focused on creating smart glasses that provide live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 version will remain simple with frame-touch interactions, prioritize phone-first architecture, and avoid unnecessary complexity to maintain social acceptability and usability.
