# Consolidation of Core Field for SmartGlasses V1

## Strengthening Signals
1. **Subtitle Quality**: Focus on real-time subtitles that feel near-perfect to reduce cognitive load and improve understanding.
2. **Face/Name Memory**: Ensure robust face/name recall with confidence objects that are clearly displayed, enhancing trust in the system's memory.

## Weakening Signals
1. **Complex Interactions**: Reduce reliance on complex interactions; frame touches should remain the primary mode of interaction for now.
2. **Heavy Cloud Dependency**: Limit heavy cloud dependency to ensure quick responses and low latency.

## Contradictions that Must Stay Alive
1. **Privacy vs Usefulness**: Strive to balance providing useful information with respecting privacy concerns, especially in face/name recall.
2. **Real-Time vs Battery Life**: Prioritize real-time response over battery life constraints, but do not compromise on energy efficiency.

## Dormant Ideas Worth Reactivating
1. **Contextual One-Line Prompts**: Consider integrating one-line prompts that provide relevant context without overwhelming the wearer with too much information.

## Concrete Tensions Around Core Areas

### Subtitle Quality and Confidence Display
- **Subtitle Placement**:
  - Current preference: More prominent placement.
  - Ensure subtitles are readable but do not distract from the conversation or environment.
  
- **Confidence Display**:
  - Preferred format: Label + short reason.
  - Avoid overly complex interfaces that might overwhelm the user.

### Memory and Recall
- **Memory Policy**:
  - Cache memory on the phone for fast lookup.
  - Ensure face/name recall is reliable with clear confidence objects, but avoid storing irrelevant or low-value information.
  
- **Phone/Cloud Boundary**:
  - Phone-first architecture: Use cloud only where it adds significant value.
  - Limit cloud dependency to maintain quick response times.

### Visual UX and Interaction
- **Visual Hierarchy**:
  - Subtitles should dominate the screen, with minimal intrusion from one-line prompts.
  
- **Interaction Constraints**:
  - Frame touches as the primary interaction method; avoid voice commands for V1.

## Summary

The core mission remains focused on creating a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The V1 should prioritize real-time responsiveness, robust memory, and clear confidence displays while respecting constraints on battery life and social acceptability. Key tensions include balancing privacy with utility, maintaining readability without distraction, and ensuring interactions are simple yet effective. Concrete decisions will help guide the development of a user-friendly, reliable system that enhances daily interactions for the wearer.
