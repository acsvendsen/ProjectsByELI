### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   By defining specific subtitle placement rules under different confidence states, we can ensure that subtitles are always visible but do not distract the wearer. This will enhance the wearer's understanding without overwhelming them with too much information.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two to three subtitle placement rules for different confidence levels: one rule for high-confidence matches (e.g., clear speaker identification), another for medium-confidence cases (e.g., potential new names or faces), and a third rule for low-confidence scenarios (e.g., uncertain speaker identity).

3. **Likely payoff**

   Implementing these specific subtitle placement rules will result in more consistent and useful subtitles, reducing the risk of visual overload while maintaining clarity during critical moments. This will enhance the user's experience and trust in the system.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

This balance helps maintain social acceptability and battery efficiency, which are critical for the core mission of providing discreet, context-aware assistance.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a specific task boundary for live subtitle processing that stays phone-local to ensure real-time performance. 3. **Likely payoff**
