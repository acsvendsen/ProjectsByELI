## Project Summary
The SmartGlasses project is focusing on delivering a discreet, context-aware personal assistant through live subtitles, face/name recall, conversation memory, and one-line support. The current V1 version will use frame-touch interactions exclusively and rely primarily on the phone for processing while minimizing heavy local computation to avoid thermal and battery strain.
