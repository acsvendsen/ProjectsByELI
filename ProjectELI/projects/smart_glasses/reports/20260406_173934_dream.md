### Idea 1: Subtitle Placement

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring stable subtitle placement rules helps maintain the wearer's trust in real-time subtitles, which is crucial for their ability to follow conversations effectively. Stable placement prevents distractions and ensures that subtitles remain prominent enough to be read quickly but not so intrusive as to become a social liability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define two subtitle placement rules: one for high-confidence text (clearly visible) and another for lower-confidence text (slightly less visible). This differentiation allows the system to balance clarity with non-intrusiveness based on the confidence level of the spoken words.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By implementing stable subtitle placement rules, users will experience more reliable and readable subtitles during conversations. This consistency will enhance their trust in the product, making it a more valuable tool for understanding real-world interactions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one fixed subtitle position rule for V1: keep subtitles at the bottom center of the display.

### Idea 2: Confidence Display

1. **Why it benefits the core**

Improving the confidence display format will enhance trust in both subtitle clarity and memory recall by making the system's certainty more transparent to the wearer. This transparency helps the user understand when they can rely on the subtitles or a name suggestion, reducing cognitive load and increasing overall usability.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

Define a confidence object format that includes a numeric score, a label indicating high, medium, or low confidence, and a short reason for why the system is certain or uncertain.

3. **Likely payoff**

By providing clear indications of the system's certainty, users will be better equipped to trust the subtitles and memory support during conversations. This improved trust can lead to more natural and effective use of the glasses, reducing misunderstandings and enhancing the wearer’s confidence in their ability to follow along and participate in conversations.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one confidence display format for V1: label only or label plus short reason.

### Idea 3: Memory/Cache Policy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the memory cache policy will enhance the trustworthiness of name recall, ensuring that new and likely known people are remembered accurately and consistently over time. This directly supports the attractor "Trustworthy memory support" by reducing the likelihood of incorrect or outdated information being presented to the wearer.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a recency-first cache eviction policy where recently seen names and faces are prioritized in the local memory cache, ensuring that they remain easily accessible for fast lookup and reinforcement over time.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By prioritizing recent interactions, the system will maintain more accurate and relevant name recall, reducing the risk of forgetting important people during conversations. This will improve the wearer's confidence in relying on the glasses for memory support, thereby deepening their trust in the core mission.

4. **Immediate next probe**

   Define one recency-first cache eviction rule for local face/name memory caching.

### Idea 4: Phone/Cloud Boundary

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Ensuring that live subtitles are processed on the phone rather than falling back to cloud processing can maintain real-time clarity and reduce latency, which is crucial for trust in noisy environments.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a rule where all subtitle processing tasks must stay local to the phone until proven otherwise. This can be done by setting up a local cache or buffer on the phone that handles initial text rendering and only offloads to cloud when necessary.

3. **Likely payoff**

   By keeping subtitle processing local, we ensure that subtitles remain clear, stable, and low-latency, which is essential for maintaining trust during live conversations. This approach also aligns with the core mission of providing real-time, reliable assistance without delays or disruptions.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Choose one phone-local versus cloud fallback boundary for face/name recall lookup in V1.

### Idea 5: Visual Hierarchy

1. **Why it benefits the core**

   Improving the visual hierarchy ensures that subtitles remain clear, readable, and unobtrusive, which directly supports the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity. By clearly distinguishing between subtitles and one-line support, the system can avoid overwhelming the wearer with too much information at once, thus maintaining focus on essential communication.

2. **What small change unlocks it**

   Define a visual hierarchy rule that prioritizes subtitles over one-line prompts based on confidence levels but allows for dynamic coexistence where necessary.

3. **Likely payoff**

   This small change will ensure that subtitles remain prominent and clear in noisy or high-stress environments, enhancing the wearer's ability to follow conversations effectively. It also supports the core attractor of real-time subtitle clarity by ensuring that critical information is prioritized over less important prompts.

4. **Immediate next probe**
   - Define one visual priority rule between subtitles and one-line support in V1.
